# AC Blacksite - App Store Submission Guide

## What Blacksite Actually Is

**Not just events.** Blacksite is a MSCHF-style platform for:

- **Drops** — Limited product releases, exclusive merch, flash sales
- **Operations** — Curated events, pop-ups, VIP experiences
- **Challenges** — Gamified experiences, scavenger hunts, interactive campaigns
- **Content** — Exclusive media, behind-the-scenes, archived vault content
- **Arcade** — In-app games and interactive experiences

The platform is designed to create urgency and exclusivity around anything AC wants to release.

---

## Privacy Policy & Support URL Options

### Option 1: Use Notion (Free, Recommended)

- Create a free Notion page with your privacy policy
- Make it public: Share → "Publish to web"
- URL format: `https://yourname.notion.site/Privacy-Policy-xxx`

### Option 2: Use your existing website

- Add a `/privacy` and `/support` page
- Example: `https://advancedcultivators.com/privacy`

### Option 3: Use GitHub Pages (Free)

- Create a repo with a simple HTML privacy policy
- Enable GitHub Pages
- URL: `https://username.github.io/blacksite-privacy/`

## App Review Notes

Include this in the "Notes for Review" section to explain the invite-only nature:

```
AC Blacksite is an invite-only platform for exclusive drops and experiences.

For review purposes, use the following test credentials:
- Email: [test account email]
- Password: [test password]

The app intentionally shows limited content to new users. Access to additional operations and drops increases based on user activity (clearance level system).

Some features are location-gated and may show as "locked" during review. This is intentional — users only see operations relevant to their region.
```
