## Blacksite RSVP Flow

### The Experience

```
1. INVITE RECEIVED
   - Personal DM/text with exclusive code
   - "You're invited to something exclusive. Download Blacksite."
   - Link to App Store / Google Play

2. APP DOWNLOAD
   - Clean, minimal app store presence
   - "AC Blacksite - Invite Only"

3. CODE ENTRY
   - Open app → "Enter Access Code"
   - Code tied to invite batch (track source)
   - Invalid code = "Not yet. Stay tuned."

4. CINEMATIC INTRO
   - 15-30 second mood video
   - AC aesthetic: black, gold, tactical
   - Ends with "You're In"

5. RSVP SCREEN
   - Event details (date, location, vibe)
   - Simple RSVP: "I'm In" / "Can't Make It"
   - Collect: Name, Phone, Email, Company/Role

6. CONFIRMATION
   - "See you Feb 8th"
   - Push notification permission prompt
   - Add to calendar option

7. PRE-EVENT DRIP
   - Day -7: "One week out. Get ready."
   - Day -3: "This Saturday. Bring your crew."
   - Day -1: "Tomorrow. Location details."
   - Day of: "Doors open at [time]. See you there."
```

### Data Capture

Every RSVP collects:
- Name
- Phone
- Email
- Company (dispensary name, brand, etc.)
- Role (buyer, budtender, owner, etc.)

This becomes your follow-up list.

## Cue Tease Plan

### Philosophy

**Don't launch. Create FOMO.**

Cue is separate from AC. The Superbowl event is AC's moment. Cue gets teased, not revealed.

### At The Event

**Halftime or End-of-Game Moment:**

Quick 60-second speech:

> "Before we get back to the game - quick thing. You know how brands always say they care about budtenders but never actually do anything? We're building something that changes that. Can't say more yet. But if you're a budtender, or you work with budtenders, you're gonna want to pay attention. We'll be reaching out soon. That's all."

**No demo. No screens. No details.**

### What Budtenders Experience

1. Hear the tease at the event
2. Feel like insiders ("I was there when they announced it")
3. Get Blacksite push notification Feb 15: "Remember what we teased? It's almost ready."
4. Get personal invite to Cue beta Feb 17-21
5. Feel exclusive, not marketed to

### Post-Event Cue Campaign

**Feb 15:** Blacksite push to all attendees
- "The thing we teased is almost ready. Budtenders, check your DMs."

**Feb 17-21:** Personal outreach to budtenders who attended
- DM/text: "You were at the party. We're inviting you to beta test [Cue]. Interested?"
- Collect interest, not signups yet

**Feb 22+:** Cue beta launch to event attendees first
- They're first cohort
- Creates "I was there first" loyalty
- Word of mouth starts
