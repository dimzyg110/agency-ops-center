# Cue Dispensary Pitch

**Use this to get Cue into new dispensary accounts.**

---

## The One-Liner

> "We built a platform that lets you run your own budtender incentive programs - and brands pay to train your team on top of it."

---

## The Problem (For Dispensaries)

1. **Budtender turnover is brutal** - Average tenure is 6-12 months
2. **Hard to motivate performance** - Hourly wage doesn't reward top sellers
3. **Training is inconsistent** - New hires learn on the job, no structure
4. **Brands want access but it's chaotic** - Reps showing up, swag everywhere, no tracking

---

## What Cue Does

### Your Programs (You Control)
Create incentive missions for YOUR goals:
- "Sell 2 Flight Kits per shift = $10 bonus"
- "Upsell to 7g+ = $2 per transaction"
- "Get 5 email signups = $5"
- "Hit $X daily sales = team bonus"

**You set the goals. You set the rewards. You see who's performing.**

### Brand Programs (They Pay, You Benefit)
Brands can run campaigns on the same app:
- They pay budtenders to learn their products
- They pay for verified sales
- Your team earns extra money - you pay nothing

**Your budtenders get trained AND paid by brands. Free education, free motivation.**

### Virtual Budtender Training
AI-powered training courses your team can take on their phone:
- Product knowledge (strain profiles, effects, terpenes)
- Sales techniques (upselling, recommendations)
- Compliance refreshers
- New product launches

**Consistent training without pulling people off the floor.**

## The Pitch Flow

### Open
> "Hey [Name], quick question - how are you handling budtender incentives right now? Like if you wanted to push a specific product or hit a sales goal, how do you motivate the team?"

### Listen for pain
- "We don't really have a system"
- "We do cash bonuses sometimes but it's informal"
- "Hard to track who's actually performing"
- "Turnover is killing us"

### Position
> "That's exactly why we built Cue. It's an app your budtenders use to earn bonuses - both from you AND from brands. You create your own programs - sell Flight Kits, upsell goals, whatever. And brands can pay to run campaigns too, so your team gets trained and rewarded without you spending extra."

### Handle Objections

**"We can't afford another expense"**
> "The brand side is free to you - they're paying to reach your budtenders. Your internal programs are optional, and most stores see ROI in the first month from increased upsells. We're talking $99-199/month to run unlimited programs."

**"My team won't use another app"**
> "They will when they're getting paid. Budtenders check it because that's where their bonuses come from. We've designed it to be 30 seconds to log a sale."

**"Brands already come in and do trainings"**
> "Right, but it's chaotic. Random reps, no tracking, no accountability. With Cue, you control which brands have access to your team. And everything is tracked - you see exactly who completed training, who's selling what."

**"How do I know they're not gaming it?"**
> "Photo proof required for every sale submission. Order ID deduplication so they can't submit the same sale twice. Fraud detection flags suspicious patterns. You review and approve before anyone gets paid."

### Close
> "Here's what I'm thinking - let's pilot it with your team for 30 days. I'll set up one internal program for you - pick a goal, Flight Kits, upsells, whatever - and we'll run one brand campaign so your team sees money coming in. If it's not working after 30 days, you walk. Fair?"
