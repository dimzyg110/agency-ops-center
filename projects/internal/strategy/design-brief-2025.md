# Design Brief: 2025 Human-Centric Sales Pages
**Date:** February 4, 2026
**Objective:** Redesign two sales landing pages to feel professional, high-converting, and distinctly "human"—avoiding the generic "AI-generated" aesthetic.

---

## 1. Anti-AI Design Principles
**The Problem:** Current "AI-style" designs suffer from "sterile perfection"—overused glassmorphism, generic dark modes, standard gradients (purple/blue), and stock 3D robots/abstract shapes. They lack soul and texture.

**The Solution (Human-Centric Trends 2025):**
*   **Texture & Imperfection (Wabi-Sabi):** Use subtle grain, paper textures, or noise overlays to break digital sterility.
*   **Grounded Color Palettes:** Move away from "Tech Neon" to "Earth + Acid"—grounded natural tones (mocha, clay, sage) paired with high-viz digital accents.
*   **Structured but Organic Layouts:** Use **Bento Grids** (modular boxes) but soften them with rounded corners and asymmetric spacing.
*   **Typography with Character:** Avoid neutral sans-serifs for headings. Use expressive grotesques or soft serifs that feel editorial.

---

## 2. Project 1: 14-Day Margin Rescue Sprint
**Target Audience:** Dispensary Owners (Retail, Brick & Mortar, High Stress)
**Vibe:** Urgent, Tactical, Hands-on, "Boots on the ground."

### Recommended Palette: "Grounded Urgency"
Avoid "Stoner Green." Go for financial/retail authority with a human touch.

*   **Primary (Background/Base):** ` #F5F2EA` (Warm Alabaster/Paper - *Not pure white*)
*   **Secondary (Structure/Cards):** `#E3E0D6` (Stone/Clay)
*   **Text/Ink:** `#1A1917` (Soft Black / Iron)
*   **Accent (Action/Rescue):** `#D94F3D` (Burnt Ember/International Orange - *Urgent but analog*)
*   **Support:** `#4A5D4E` (Deep Oxide Green - *Wealth/Stability*)

### Typography Stack
*   **Headings:** **[Space Grotesk](https://fonts.google.com/specimen/Space+Grotesk)** (Bold 600/700). Quirky, technical but friendly.
*   **Body:** **[Inter](https://fonts.google.com/specimen/Inter)** (Regular 400). Maximum readability for complex concepts.
*   **Accent/Kicker:** **[Gloria Hallelujah](https://fonts.google.com/specimen/Gloria+Hallelujah)** or similar handwritten font for annotations (arrows, circles, "Look here" notes).

### Visual Elements
*   **Imagery:** High-contrast b&w photography of retail spaces with colorful UI overlays.
*   **Texture:** Subtle **Noise/Film Grain** (5% opacity) on solid backgrounds.
*   **Icons:** Thick-stroke (2px), hand-drawn or "rough" edge icons. No filled generic glyphs.

---

## 3. Project 2: MA Operator Signals Pack
**Target Audience:** Vendors/Brands (B2B, Strategic, Data-Driven)
**Vibe:** Clarity, Signal vs. Noise, Insider Knowledge.

### Recommended Palette: "Digital Editorial"
A "Dark Mode" that feels like a premium magazine, not a SaaS dashboard.

*   **Primary (Background):** `#1E1C1A` (Deep Espresso/Mocha - *Pantone 2025 nod*)
*   **Secondary (Cards/Sections):** `#2C2926` (Lighter Espresso)
*   **Text:** `#ECEBE6` (Warm Grey)
*   **Accent (Signal):** `#CCFF00` (Acid Lime/Volt - *High visibility data*)
*   **Support:** `#A1A1AA` (Metallic Silver - *Tech/Premium*)

### Typography Stack
*   **Headings:** **[Fraunces](https://fonts.google.com/specimen/Fraunces)** (Soft Serif). Feels "Old Money" meets "New Tech." High trust.
*   **Body:** **[Plus Jakarta Sans](https://fonts.google.com/specimen/Plus+Jakarta+Sans)**. Modern, geometric, excellent screen rendering.
*   **Data/Numbers:** **[JetBrains Mono](https://fonts.google.com/specimen/JetBrains+Mono)**. For metrics and signal data.

### Visual Elements
*   **Layout:** **Bento Grid** style. Information packed into distinct, rounded-corner modules.
*   **Graphics:** "Data Art" style—thin lines, precise charts, but with "glow" effects restricted to key data points.
*   **Micro-interactions:** Subtle border glows on hover using the Accent Lime color.

---

## 4. Layout & Spacing (Global)
*   **Rhythm:** 8pt grid system.
    *   Small spacing: 8px, 16px
    *   Section gaps: 80px - 120px (Generous whitespace feels expensive)
*   **Containers:** Max-width 1200px.
*   **Border Radius:**
    *   Cards: 12px (Soft) or 24px (Friendly).
    *   Buttons: 100px (Pill shape) for CTAs.
