## 4. KEY BETS & RISKS

### The Core Bets

**Bet 1: Distressed Operators Will Pay for Help**  
*Assumption:* Operators with high distress + good reviews have money to pay for services  
*Risk:* Some are truly broke — qualification is essential  
*Mitigation:* Focus on operational operators with review velocity (they have revenue)

**Bet 2: Data Has Standalone Value**  
*Assumption:* PE firms, vendors, and service providers will pay $199-1,499/mo for this intel  
*Risk:* Market doesn't value the data as expected  
*Mitigation:* Start with DD packages (high ticket, one-time) to validate demand before building subscription infrastructure

**Bet 3: Toll Processing Is the Wedge**  
*Assumption:* Cultivators will pay $750 + 2% to access manufacturing margins  
*Risk:* They find manufacturers directly or don't have capital for setup  
*Mitigation:* Start with revenue share only model, take risk alongside them

**Bet 4: Market Doesn't Collapse**  
*Assumption:* MA cannabis market stabilizes or consolidates, doesn't disappear  
*Risk:* Federal legalization or major regulatory shift disrupts everything  
*Mitigation:* Build multi-state expansion plan (CT, NJ, NY) to diversify

### Risk Matrix

| Risk | Likelihood | Impact | Mitigation |
|------|------------|--------|------------|
| Can't close Sprint sales | Medium | High | Lower price, payment plans, success-based fees |
| Data becomes commoditized | Medium | Medium | Build proprietary signals (hiring, menus, predictive) |
| Operators can't pay | High | Medium | Strict qualification, focus on operational + high reviews |
| Competition emerges | Medium | Low | 3-6 month head start, network effects |
| Regulatory changes | Low | High | Stay agile, expand geography |
