## 4) Key Bets (assumptions, risks, failure modes)

### Bets / hypotheses
1. **High reviews = operational demand** and they’ll pay to protect it.
2. **Distress + operational status = urgency + ability to pay** (sweet spot).
3. A tight 14-day offer can close faster than general marketing retainers.
4. Intel Packs can be productized into repeatable cashflow and feed Sprint pipeline.

### Assumptions
- The contact info in the dataset reaches decision-makers often enough to book calls.
- Operators with 500–3,000 reviews have adequate cashflow to pay $2.5K–$3K (especially with split-pay).
- Reviews/velocity correlate with foot traffic (good proxy) in MA.

### What could go wrong
- **Payment risk:** distressed operators may agree but fail to pay. Mitigation: collect at least **50% upfront**.
- **Bad-fit “stalled ops” leads:** some may not truly be operating. Mitigation: first question on call: “Are you currently selling? Last month revenue range?”
- **Decision-maker mismatch:** emails/phones may hit lawyers/consultants. Mitigation: ask directly for GM/owner/CFO and get warm transfer.
- **Time squeeze:** delivery + selling in same 2 weeks. Mitigation: cap Sprint slots at **2 active at a time**.
