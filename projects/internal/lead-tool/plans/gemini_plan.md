## 4. Key Bets & Risks

### The Bets (What we believe):
1.  **Distress > Marketing:** Operators are too scared to buy "branding" but will pay for "survival" (Margin Rescue).
2.  **Data = Currency:** Vendors are hungrier for our data than operators are for our consulting.
3.  **The "Golden Gap":** High Reviews + High Distress is the only segment worth selling to.

### The Risks (What could go wrong):
1.  **Data Accuracy:** If Vanessa at Legal Greens says "We're fine, we just forgot to renew the license," the pitch fails. (Mitigation: Soften the opener—"Our data *suggests*...")
2.  **Budget Freeze:** Distressed operators might have *literally* zero cash. (Mitigation: Payment plans or "Success Fee" model for the most promising ones).
3.  **Vendor Skepticism:** Vendors might be tired of buying "lists." (Mitigation: Send a "free sample" of 3 leads to prove quality).
