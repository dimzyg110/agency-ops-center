## 4. Intel Vendors Would Pay For

### Pre-Pitch Questions a Brand Would Ask:

1. **"Is this dispensary worth my time?"**
   - Review count + velocity = foot traffic proxy
   - Rating = customer satisfaction/retention
   
2. **"Will they be around in 6 months?"**
   - Distress score
   - Expired license count
   - Google status (operational vs closed_temp vs closed_perm)

3. **"Are they open to new products?"**
   - Financial pressure signals
   - Missing license types (need to source vs make)
   - Time since license = burn rate indicator

4. **"Who's the right contact?"**
   - Verified email/phone
   - Decision-maker vs gatekeeper

5. **"What's their buying power?"**
   - Review velocity = sales proxy
   - Multi-location = volume potential
   - Vertical integration = lower margins but stability
