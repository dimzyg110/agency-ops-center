## Key Market Insights for GLASS Positioning

### 1. Market is in a "Scary" Downturn
Jennifer Ngo (Bud's Goods): "It's definitely a consumer's market... I don't think anyone could've predicted it would be as bad as it is from a legal and regulatory standpoint."
- **GLASS angle**: Data-driven operators survive downturns. GLASS helps you understand exactly where revenue is coming from and what to optimize.

### 2. 160+ Dispensaries, Most Have No Analytics
Over 160 licensed dispensaries in MA. The vast majority use Dutchie/Jane iFrame menus, which means:
- They don't own their customer data
- They can't do cross-platform analytics
- They have fragmented loyalty data
- **GLASS angle**: First-ever unified analytics for MA cannabis operators

### 3. Independent Operators Are the Sweet Spot
MSOs have corporate analytics tools. Independents have nothing. The 120+ independent MA operators are the ideal GLASS customer: they care about their data, they're locally invested, and they need every competitive advantage.

### 4. Industry Association Network is Small & Tight
MCC has ~15 board members. MassCBA is similarly small. Getting 2-3 board members as GLASS users would create a cascade effect through the entire MA cannabis community.

### 5. Hiring Signals Show Active Growth
331 open cannabis jobs (Glassdoor), 285+ on Indeed. Companies are investing in growth despite the downturn — these are the ones most likely to invest in analytics.
