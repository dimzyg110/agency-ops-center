# The Operator's Margin Report — Brand Guidelines

> Premium intel for MA dispensary operators who refuse to bleed out on margins.

---

## 1. Brand Identity

### Name Options

| Name | Pros | Cons |
|------|------|------|
| **The Operator's Margin Report** | Clear value prop, signals exclusivity ("operator"), professional | Slightly long, clinical |
| **Margin Watch** | Short, punchy, implies vigilance | Generic, could be any industry |
| **The Green Ledger** | Industry-relevant wordplay, memorable | Slightly cute, less urgent |

### Recommended Name

**The Operator's Margin Report**

**Rationale:** "Operator" immediately signals this is for people running the business—not investors, not enthusiasts, not consultants. It's insider language. "Margin Report" is the promise: this is about money, survival, and the numbers that matter. It sounds like something you'd pay for. The length works in email subject lines: "The Operator's Margin Report: Week 14 — Your COGs Are Lying to You"

### Tagline Options

1. **"The numbers they won't tell you."** — Implies insider access, positions against generic industry coverage
2. **"Weekly margin intel for MA operators."** — Straightforward, SEO-friendly, clear value
3. **"Survive. Then thrive."** — Emotional, acknowledges current market pain, aspirational

**Recommended:** Use #1 for brand positioning, #2 for functional contexts (landing page, email footers)

### Voice & Tone Guidelines

| Attribute | Description | Example |
|-----------|-------------|---------|
| **Direct** | No throat-clearing, no "I thought I'd share..." | ❌ "I wanted to take a moment to discuss..." ✅ "Your labor costs are eating your margin. Here's the fix." |
| **Insider** | We're in this together. Same trenches. | "You've seen the commission floor prices. I've seen the commission floor prices. Let's talk about what they actually mean." |
| **Data-backed** | Opinions come with receipts | "I talked to 6 operators last week. 4 of them are cutting shifts. The other 2? Different story. Here's why." |
| **Urgent but not panicked** | The house is on fire, but we have a hose | "This isn't about doom. It's about what you do next." |
| **Confident** | No hedging, no "maybe" | ❌ "This might help some of you..." ✅ "This will save you $2K/month. Here's how." |

### Brand Personality

1. **Battle-tested** — Been through it. Scarred but still standing.
2. **Generous** — Shares the playbook, not just the highlight reel
3. **Sharp** — Cuts through noise. No filler.
4. **Loyal** — Shows up every week. Reliable.
5. **Contrarian** — Questions conventional wisdom. Says what others won't.

## 2. Visual Identity

### Color Palette

| Role | Color | Hex | Usage |
|------|-------|-----|-------|
| **Primary** | Midnight Navy | `#1A2332` | Headers, primary backgrounds, text |
| **Secondary** | Slate Gray | `#64748B` | Body text, secondary elements |
| **Accent** | Signal Green | `#22C55E` | CTAs, positive metrics, highlights |
| **Warning** | Ember Orange | `#F97316` | Alerts, negative trends, urgency |
| **Background** | Off-White | `#FAFAFA` | Page/email backgrounds |
| **Card Background** | Warm White | `#FFFFFF` | Content cards, sections |

**Design Note:** The green is intentional but subtle—industry nod without being cliché. Navy establishes authority. Orange for danger signals (margin alerts, deadlines).

### Typography

| Role | Font | Weight | Size (Desktop) |
|------|------|--------|----------------|
| **Headlines** | [Space Grotesk](https://fonts.google.com/specimen/Space+Grotesk) | Bold (700) | 32-48px |
| **Subheads** | Space Grotesk | Medium (500) | 20-24px |
| **Body** | [Inter](https://fonts.google.com/specimen/Inter) | Regular (400) | 16-18px |
| **Data/Numbers** | [JetBrains Mono](https://fonts.google.com/specimen/JetBrains+Mono) | Medium (500) | 14-16px |
| **Captions** | Inter | Regular (400) | 14px |

**Why these fonts:**
- **Space Grotesk:** Modern, slightly technical, confident. Reads as premium without being cold.
- **Inter:** Workhorse body font. Clean, readable at all sizes. Trust-building.
- **JetBrains Mono:** For data, prices, percentages. Signals precision. Numbers should look like they matter.

### Logo Concept

**Primary Mark:** Stylized margin graph forming the letter "M" — a single ascending line that peaks and plateaus, contained within a rounded square. Clean, geometric, works at favicon size.

**Text Treatment:** "THE OPERATOR'S" stacked above "MARGIN REPORT" in Space Grotesk Bold. Letterspacing +2%.

**Variations:**
- Full lockup (horizontal)
- Stacked lockup (vertical)
- Icon only (for favicon, social)
- Reversed (white on navy)

### Icon Style

- **Stroke weight:** 2px
- **Style:** Rounded caps, geometric, minimal
- **Set:** Custom set of ~20 icons for recurring concepts:
  - Margin up/down arrows
  - Alert/warning
  - Calendar (for weekly cadence)
  - Dollar/percentage
  - Lightbulb (insights)
  - Lock (premium/insider)
  - Chart/graph
  - Checkmark (action items)

**Primary Sections** (The Big 3 recurring segments):

```

│                                                            │
```

- Navy header bar with white text

**Warning Boxes:**

- Background: `#FFF7ED` (very light orange tint)
- Used for: Margin alerts, deadline reminders, "watch out for this"

- **Min font size:** 16px for body (no pinch-zooming)
- **Button width:** Full-width on mobile (easier tap target)

- **Data tables:** Stack to single column on mobile, or use horizontal scroll container
- **Header "Margin Pulse" box:** Stacks vertically on mobile

## 5. Content Style Guide

### Writing Rules

| Rule | Example |
|------|---------|
| **Sentences: 15 words max average** | Long sentences lose operators who are reading between customers. |
| **Paragraphs: 3 sentences max** | Wall of text = skipped section. |
| **Lead with the insight** | ❌ "After analyzing the data, I found that..." ✅ "Wholesale dropped 4% this week. Here's what that means for your orders." |
| **Use "you" constantly** | This is about their business, not your analysis. |
| **Numbers are specific** | ❌ "significant savings" ✅ "$2,340/month" |
| **Active voice only** | ❌ "Margins were impacted by..." ✅ "Labor costs crushed margins." |
| **No jargon without explanation** | First use: "COGS (cost of goods sold)". After: COGS is fine. |

### Formatting Rules

**When to bold:**
- Key numbers and percentages
- The main takeaway of a section
- Action items

**When to use bullet lists:**
- 3+ related items
- Step-by-step instructions
- Quick wins section (always)

**When to use numbered lists:**
- Sequential steps
- Priority order matters
- "Top 3" or ranked lists

**When to use callout boxes:**
- One key stat that deserves attention
- Warnings or alerts
- Pull quotes from operators

**Emoji usage:**
- Sparingly. Max 3-4 per issue.
- Functional only: 📊 for data, ⚠️ for warnings, ✅ for action items
- Never decorative. Never in headlines.

### Section Templates

#### Margin Pulse (Opening data section)
```
📊 THIS WEEK'S MARGIN PULSE

**MA Wholesale Avg:** $X,XXX/lb (↑/↓ X% WoW)
**Commission Floor:** [Status + brief implication]
**Your Lever This Week:** [One specific focus area]

[2-3 sentence context on what these numbers mean for decision-making]
```

#### The Deep Dive (Main analysis)
```
## [TOPIC]: [Specific angle]

[Hook — why this matters right now, 1-2 sentences]

[The Problem — what operators are getting wrong, 2-3 sentences]

[The Data — specific numbers, comparisons, evidence]

[The Fix — actionable steps, numbered if sequential]

**Bottom line:** [One sentence summary of what to do]
```

#### Quick Wins (Tactical section)
```
⚡ QUICK WINS

Things you can do this week:

• **[Action]** — [Why it works + expected result]
• **[Action]** — [Why it works + expected result]  
• **[Action]** — [Why it works + expected result]
```

#### Operator Spotlight (Optional, rotating)
```
💬 OPERATOR SPOTLIGHT

[Quote from real operator, 2-3 sentences]

**What they did:** [Tactic, briefly]
**The result:** [Specific outcome]
**Your move:** [How reader can apply this]
```

### What to Avoid

| Avoid | Why | Instead |
|-------|-----|---------|
| "In this issue, we'll explore..." | Wastes time, sounds like a term paper | Just start with the insight |
| "It's important to note that..." | Filler phrase | Cut it. State the thing directly. |
| "Stakeholders" | Corporate speak | "Your team" or "your partners" |
| "Leverage" (as verb) | Buzzword | "Use" |
| "Cannabis space/industry" | Overused | "Market" or just drop it |
| "Navigate challenges" | Vague consultant-speak | Name the specific challenge |
| "Best practices" | Generic, implies you don't know the specifics | "Here's what's working" |
| "At the end of the day" | Cliché | Cut it |
| "Moving forward" | Filler | Cut it |
| "I hope this helps" | Weak | "This will [specific outcome]" |
| Any sentence that starts with "I" | Makes it about you | Rewrite to start with "You" or the insight |
