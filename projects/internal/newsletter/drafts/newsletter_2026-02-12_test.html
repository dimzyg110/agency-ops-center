💡 MARGIN TIP

Know Your Market Share Number

MA did $1.65B in adult-use sales in 2025, roughly $137M/month. Your monthly revenue ÷ $137M = your market share. If you don't know this number, you can't set realistic growth targets. Track it monthly and benchmark against your region (Boston, Valley, Cape, Berkshires).

Action: Pull your last 3 months of revenue and calculate your slice of the $137M/month pie.
