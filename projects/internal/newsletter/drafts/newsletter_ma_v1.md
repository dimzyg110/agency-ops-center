## 💬 CUSTOMER CORNER

### Scenario 1: "I've Never Done This Before"

First-time customers are gold — high conversion potential, loyalty waiting to happen. But they're nervous and easily overwhelmed. Here's how your team handles them:

**The situation:** Customer walks in, looks around hesitantly, maybe says "This is my first time."

**What NOT to do:**
- Immediately launch into THC percentages and strain genetics
- Ask "What are you looking for?" (they don't know!)
- Treat them like they should already understand the menu

**The script that works:**

> "Welcome in! First time? That's awesome — I remember my first dispensary visit. Let me ask you this: **What do you want to feel?** Relaxed after a long day? Energized and creative? Just trying something low-key?"

Then:
> "Based on that, I'd start you with [specific product] — it's [low/mild] dose, so you can see how it feels and come back for something stronger if you want. Most first-timers love it."

**Massachusetts twist:** Many first-timers are 50+ and coming from a medical background or total cannabis-naïveté. Don't assume they know what a "cart" is. Show, don't tell.

**Pro tip:** After checkout, say: "Let me know how it goes next time you're in!" This creates an implicit commitment to return.

---

### Scenario 2: The CT/NY Border Hopper

**The situation:** Customer with a Connecticut or New York ID asks: "Can I take this back home?"

**The answer is NO. Here's how to say it without killing the vibe:**

> "Legally, cannabis can't cross state lines — that's federal, not us. But while you're here in Massachusetts, you're good. A lot of our out-of-state visitors come back every time they're in the area. Want me to set you up with a loyalty account so you get points for next time?"

**Why this works:** You've explained the law, signaled you're not judging, and immediately pivoted to a return visit. Don't lecture. Don't wink. Just state facts and move on.

---

### Scenario 3: The Purchase Limit Math

**The situation:** Customer doesn't understand why they can buy 5g of concentrate but not an ounce of flower + 5g of concentrate.

**The quick explainer:**
> "Massachusetts limits you to 1 ounce of flower OR the equivalent per visit. Concentrates count as 5x — so 5 grams of concentrate equals your whole limit. If you want both, you'd need to do a mix: like a half ounce of flower plus 2.5g concentrate. Want me to help you build a combo that maxes out what you can take home?"

**Train your team on the math.** This comes up 5+ times per day.

## 💡 MARGIN TIP

### The "Add-On Ask" That Actually Works

Most budtenders skip the upsell because they feel salesy. Here's a technique that feels helpful instead of pushy:

**The setup:** Customer has picked their main product and is heading to checkout.

**The phrase:**
> "One more thing — a lot of people who buy [their product] also grab [complementary product] because [specific reason]. Want me to add one?"

**Examples:**
- "A lot of people who buy flower also grab a pack of cones — way easier than rolling. Want me to throw one in?"
- "Customers getting edibles for the first time usually add a CBD tincture — it helps mellow things if the THC hits harder than expected."
- "This strain is great for relaxing, and our lavender gummies pair really well with it for sleep."

**Why it works:** You're not selling — you're sharing what other people do. Social proof removes the friction.

**Expected lift:** $5-12 per transaction on average. At 50+ transactions/day, that's $250-600/week. That's $13K-31K per year. From one phrase.

## ⚡ QUICK WINS — MASSACHUSETTS EDITION

Four things you can do this week that take 30 minutes or less:

### 1. Audit Your Metrc Tags
Spend 15 minutes ensuring your physical inventory matches Metrc. The CCC is cracking down on "ghost inventory" — products in the system but not on the shelf. This is the #1 cause of compliance violations right now. Check it before they do.

### 2. Update Your ID Scanner Firmware
New CT and RI IDs are failing on old Veriscan versions. If you're rejecting valid customers because your scanner is out of date, that's money walking out the door. Check for firmware updates today.

### 3. Walk the Floor Like a Customer
Enter through the front door. Look at your store with fresh eyes. Is the first thing you see compelling? Is pricing clear? Is it obvious where to start? Is your local/craft section visible? Fix one thing.

### 4. Check Your Google Business Profile
When's the last time you updated your photos, hours, or product highlights? If a competitor nearby closed, add their name as a keyword. ("Convenient to former XYZ customers.") This is free advertising.

**Bonus:** Text your top 10 loyalty customers a personal message: "Hey [name], we just got [product] you might love. Thought of you." Takes 20 minutes. Retention gold.
