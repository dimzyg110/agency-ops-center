# Strategic Advisor Feedback — Feb 13, 2026

## Source
External strategic review of GLASS consumer-first strategy.

## Key Thesis
"He who owns the consumer demand, owns the industry." This is asymmetric warfare — we're not selling software, we're selling reputation control and demand capture. Yelp/Zillow/GasBuddy playbook.

## Four Brutal Landmines

### Trap 1: GPID Reality Check
- GPID can't be built "as a byproduct" — a price comparison site IS a product matching engine
- B2C consumers will roast us on Reddit if we match a 1g pre-roll with a 3.5g flower
- **FIX: Launch with Top 500 hand-verified SKUs. "We track the 500 most popular products in MA."**
- Expand incrementally as matching improves

### Trap 2: Pre-Tax vs Post-Tax
- MA dispensaries inconsistently show pre-tax vs tax-included prices
- A "Store A is $10 cheaper" card that's wrong after tax = instant credibility death
- **FIX: Normalize ALL prices to post-tax (out the door). Detect tax inclusion from Dutchie profiles.**

### Trap 3: Data Freshness ("Drive of Shame")
- Cannabis inventory moves fast — deals sell out in 3 hours
- Consumer drives to store, product is sold out, GLASS takes blame
- **FIX: "Verified X hours ago" on every price. "Report Out of Stock" button. Hide prices >24hr old.**

### Trap 4: SEO Sandbox
- Google sandboxes new domains for 3-6 months. Helpful Content algo penalizes scraped directories.
- **FIX: Month 1-2 traffic = 100% Social + Reddit. Make pages unique (price history charts, distance calculators, THC accuracy scores) so Google indexes them as high-value.**

## "Hell Yes" Elements

### 1. Dispensary Tag Mechanic
Tagging dispensaries in social posts forces engagement. Owners are vain and paranoid about competitors. They'll "Claim their Page" within 60 seconds of seeing they're overcharging.

### 2. THC Accuracy Score
Cross-reference CCC's 298K-row lab dataset against inflated menu claims. "30% of MA Dispensaries Inflate THC Numbers" → Boston Globe exclusive → instant trusted consumer watchdog status.

### 3. Dutchie WAF Counter-Attack
When Dutchie/Weedmaps deploy IP bans: "Dutchie is blocking us from showing your deals to our 25K shoppers. Generate a read-only API key for us." Consumer traffic forces dispensaries to bypass POS firewalls FOR us.

## Step 0: The Top 500
Query DB for the 500 most ubiquitous SKUs in MA. Hand-verify matching. Launch with curated, 100% accurate catalog. This is the MVP dataset.
