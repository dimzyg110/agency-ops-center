## 1. First Impression — Landing Page (glassintel.xyz)

**Do I understand what this does in 5 seconds?** Yes. "See what your competitors charge" — that's the one sentence that hooked me. The subtext — "Know your market position. React faster. Protect your margins." — that's speaking my language. I live in the margins. Every dollar matters.

**Would I sign up?** I'd create a free account. But here's the friction:

**What's good:**
- The headline is perfect. "See what your competitors charge" is exactly what I lie awake at 2am thinking about.
- "No credit card required" — good, I'd never enter a card for something I haven't tried.
- The design is clean and professional — doesn't look like every other cannabis tech company with weed leaves and green gradients. I'd show this to my accountant and they'd think it's legit.
- Signup page is simple: Google login or email/password. "Get Price Wire in 60 seconds." That's a good promise.

**What sucks:**
- The page asks for my email before showing me a SINGLE real price. You want my info before you prove anything? Show me the 3 dispensaries nearest to East Boston and what they're charging for flower. That would get me to sign up in 10 seconds.
- **3 of 4 headline features are "Coming Soon."** Price Wire is live. Threat Radar, Morning Brief, and Cue are all vaporware on the marketing page. That's a credibility problem. It feels like I'm signing up for a pitch deck.
- The "ecosystem" section at the bottom (GLASS, Virtual Budtender, Cue, Delivery) makes it look like you're building everything for everyone. Are you a pricing tool or a delivery company? Pick one and nail it.
- **No pricing information anywhere.** Is the useful stuff $50/month? $500/month? I see "Free forever for the core dashboard" buried at the bottom, but no actual pricing page. I hate surprises with my money.
- **Zero social proof.** No testimonials, no case studies, no "Dispensary X saved $Y by adjusting their prices." Not one quote from a real operator.

## 4. The Hidden Data Assets — What Would Actually Differentiate This

Reading about what's under the hood, there are some things here that could be game-changers that I don't see surfaced anywhere on the site:

### Lab Test Verification (147K state lab tests)
You can cross-reference what a brand claims on their menu (30% THC) with what the state lab actually tested (22% THC). **This is massive.** If a brand is lying to me about potency — and I'm putting those inflated numbers on my shelf — that's a trust issue with my customers. If GLASS could flag "this brand's claimed THC is 8% higher than lab tests show" that would:
1. Help me stock honest brands (builds customer trust)
2. Give me leverage in wholesale negotiations ("your numbers are inflated, I want a better price")
3. Differentiate my shop ("we only carry lab-verified potency")

**Why isn't this on the landing page?** This is a unique feature nobody else has and you're burying it.

### Facility Sales Data (67K records — actual revenue by category)
You have data on what's actually SELLING, not just what's priced. That's the difference between "what should I charge?" and "what should I stock?" If concentrates are growing 15% quarter over quarter and flower is declining, that changes how I allocate shelf space and purchasing budget.

**Why isn't this surfaced in the Report Card?** "Your flower takes up 60% of your shelf but it's the slowest-growing category in your area" — that's a recommendation that could save me thousands in wasted inventory.

### 71 Months of Price Collapse Data
$14.68/gram in Jan 2020 → $4.01/gram in Nov 2025. You have the entire timeline of the industry's margin destruction, month by month. **Use this to tell a story.** Not just "prices are down" but "here's where it stopped falling in other markets" or "here's the floor based on wholesale costs." Give me hope that there's a bottom, and help me plan for it.
