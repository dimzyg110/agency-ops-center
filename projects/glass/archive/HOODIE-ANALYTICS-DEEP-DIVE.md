## Executive Summary

Hoodie Analytics is the most dangerous competitor in the cannabis data intelligence space — and they just made a move that directly threatens GLASS's consumer-first thesis. Their December 2025 partnership with High Times creates a consumer-facing cannabis e-commerce platform powered by Hoodie's data engine, merging product discovery with editorial content and enabling real-time ordering from 10,000+ dispensaries.

**The good news:** Hoodie is a brand/enterprise-first company with $9.4M in VC funding and 36 employees — they're building a Nielsen for cannabis. They charge enterprise prices, serve brands and MSOs primarily, and have zero MA-specific depth. Their consumer play is brand-driven (brands pay for placement), not consumer-driven (best price wins). They have no delivery integration, no regulatory data, and no AI pricing recommendations for individual operators.

**The bad news:** They have 6 years of data history, 30M+ product variants cataloged, foot traffic data, POS/ERP/seed-to-sale integrations, a team of IRI/Nielsen/NPD alumni, and now the High Times brand pointing 20M+ monthly readers at their commerce platform. Their "Product Catalog" is the closest thing to our GPID in market. If they ever decide to go local and free, they could crush us.

**Kill shot:** GLASS wins by being everything Hoodie isn't — free, local, operator-first, consumer-driven, and integrated with fulfillment. We don't fight them on national data breadth. We make ourselves indispensable to MA operators before they even know we exist.

## 2. Product Analysis

### Product Suite Overview

Hoodie has evolved from a single competitive intelligence tool to a multi-product platform serving the entire cannabis supply chain:

#### For Brands / Wholesalers
1. **Sales Enablement** — Track product stocking, pricing & promotions across 10,000+ dispensaries. Both stores you supply and ones you don't. Mobile app for sales reps.
2. **Competitive Intelligence** — Next-day stocking, promotion & pricing on every SKU. Benchmark distribution & pricing against competitors.
3. **Sales Projections & Forecasting** — SKU, Strain, Store, Brand, Category & State level projections using menu data + foot traffic + regression modeling.
4. **Distribution Coverage Model** — Track where your products are stocked and where they're not.
5. **Master Data Management** — Integrate brand product data with Hoodie's market data for enrichment.

#### For Retailers / Dispensaries
1. **Dispensary Analytics** — Shelf composition, pricing trends, product performance, competitor monitoring.
2. **Retail Operations Tools** — Curate product mix, drive engagement, maximize ROI with data on consumer demand and competitive pricing.

#### For Distributors
1. **Sales Enablement** — Same as brands but focused on wholesale/distribution relationships.
2. **Competitive Intelligence** — Next-day pricing/promo data from 10,000+ menus.
3. **Master Data Management** — Integrate customer/product data with Hoodie data.

#### For Technology Providers (POS, ERP)
1. **Embedded Analytics** — POS companies can integrate Hoodie data to enhance their own platforms.
2. **Foot Traffic Data** — Hoodie tracks every US cannabis location for anonymized shopper visitation behavior.

#### Consumer-Facing Products
1. **Where to Buy** — Embeddable widget for brand websites:
   - **Store Locator** — Real-time store locator powered by menu data
   - **Product Locator** — Multi-filterable product search across all active dispensaries in real-time
   - **Consumer Analytics** — Brands track real-time analytics of locator traffic
2. **Ask Hoodie (askhoodie.com)** — Consumer search for products/brands/dispensaries (launched ~2022)
3. **High Times E-Commerce Platform** — The big new play (Dec 2025)

#### Services
1. **Managed Services / Embedded Analysts** — Fractional analytics resources embedded in client organizations
2. **Burst Dashboards & Reports** — On-demand analytics and dashboard creation
3. **Custom Data Warehouse** — Full-stack analytics solutions for MSOs
4. **Hoodie Connect** — Free program for retailers: enhanced exposure and order referrals through High Times network (launched Dec 2025)

### How They Collect Data

This is critical to understand. Hoodie has **four data collection methods**:

1. **Smart Menu Mining (Primary)** — "Actively mining over 10,000 POS systems nationwide, enhanced by AI that detects and corrects errors in menu data." They use real-time cart simulations to track inventory velocity. This is their bread and butter — essentially what GLASS does with our scraper, but at 50x the scale.

2. **Integrated Data Partnerships** — "Gathering data on inventory levels, sales velocity, and brand performance through partnerships across Retail Point-of-Sale, ERP, and Seed-to-Sale systems." This gives them actual sales data through voluntary data sharing, not just menu prices.

3. **Behavioral Geofencing / Foot Traffic** — "Tracking anonymized consumer behavior using digital polygons around dispensaries, including foot traffic patterns, time spent in stores, travel distances, and insights into consumer routines." They license cellphone location data (millions of signals in/out of all US dispensaries).

4. **Retail Demographic Profiling** — "Analyzing hundreds of geographic and demographic attributes for each dispensary, including location type, affluence, education levels, age brackets." Census/demographic data overlaid on dispensary locations.

### "3 Billion Daily Observations" — Decoded

This number comes from combining:
- **3.5M+ daily unique offers** tracked across menus (product×price×store combinations)
- **10,000+ stores** tracked daily
- **~10 million monthly customer store visits** (foot traffic)
- **30 million discovered cannabis product variants** in their product catalog
- **Cart simulations** running against live menus (checking stock/velocity)
- Hourly/daily delta checks on all tracked menus

Multiply frequency × stores × products × foot traffic signals and you easily get to 3B+ "observations."

### Product Catalog / Product Graph

This is the closest thing to our GPID concept:

> "Hoodie maintains over 30 Million discovered Cannabis Product Variants. Every item is mastered & categorized using our analyst team & machine learning."

Wes Shepherd in 2022: "We are aiming to create a nexus of industry data: a **product graph** to connect all industry players together and speed up critical insights."

Their "Product Catalog" lets brands ensure dispensary menus accurately represent their products. 1906 New Highs uses it across nine states to "ensure dispensary menus are accurate."

**Key difference from GPID:** Hoodie's Product Catalog is brand-facing (brands upload data, Hoodie matches to menus). GLASS's GPID is consumer-facing (match the same product across Dutchie/Weedmaps/Jane/Leafly for price comparison). Hoodie masters products from the brand side; we master from the platform side.

## 4. Strengths & Moat

### Where Hoodie Is Genuinely Ahead of Us

**BE BRUTALLY HONEST:**

1. **Scale**: 10,000+ dispensaries vs our ~235 (Dutchie) + ~300 (Weedmaps) in MA only. They have national coverage. We have one state.

2. **Data Depth**: 30M product variants cataloged. 6+ years of historical data. We have weeks of data and ~60K prices.

3. **Foot Traffic Data**: We have nothing like this. They license cellphone location data to track store visits, dwell time, travel patterns, demographics. This is expensive and hard to replicate.

4. **POS/ERP Integrations**: They have actual partnerships with POS and ERP systems for sales velocity data. We scrape public menus. They supplement scraping with real transaction signals.

5. **Team Pedigree**: IRI, Nielsen, NPD, Numerator alumni. CEO built and sold Channel IQ (same exact business model for e-commerce). They've done this before in CPG. We're building from scratch.

6. **Funding**: $9.4M raised from top cannabis VCs. We're bootstrapped with zero dollars in GLASS specifically.

7. **Brand Relationships**: Curaleaf, PharmaCann, 1906, Good Day Farm, Bhang, 710 Labs — they serve major MSOs and multi-state brands. We serve nobody yet.

8. **High Times Partnership**: 50+ year cannabis media brand pointing consumers at their platform. We have no equivalent consumer distribution channel.

9. **Product Graph / Catalog**: Their product mastering (30M variants, ML + human analysts) is more mature than our GPID concept. They've been building it for 6+ years.

10. **Sales Projections**: Even if imperfect, their regression models combining menu data + foot traffic + POS partnerships give them estimated sales velocity data. We only have price data.

### Their Moat

1. **Historical data moat** — 6+ years of daily pricing data creates trend analysis capabilities we can't replicate without a time machine
2. **Network effects** — 10K+ dispensaries tracked = comprehensive market picture. New clients get value because existing coverage is already deep
3. **Brand-side data ingestion** — Brands uploading product data to their catalog creates stickiness and improves data quality
4. **Foot traffic exclusivity** — Location data licensing is expensive and relationships take time to build
5. **Investor network** — SPINS founder, Arcview Group, cannabis VC ecosystem = distribution channels for sales

### What Would Be Hardest for Us to Replicate

1. **National scale** — We'd need to scrape 10,000+ stores across 30+ states. Possible but expensive and distracting from MA focus.
2. **Foot traffic data** — Requires licensing from data brokers (SafeGraph, Placer.ai, etc.). Expensive ($10K+/yr minimum).
3. **6 years of historical pricing data** — Literally impossible to recreate.
4. **POS integration partnerships** — Takes years of relationship building and trust.
5. **The High Times brand** — You can't buy 50 years of cannabis credibility.

## 5. Weaknesses & Gaps

### Where They're Weak

1. **Enterprise Pricing Excludes 90% of Operators**  
   The independent dispensary owner with 1-2 stores can't afford $1,000+/month for data. Hoodie's entire revenue model is built around brands and MSOs. GLASS is free. This is our single biggest advantage.

2. **Sales Projections Are Questioned**  
   Reddit user (r/weedbiz): "Hoodie is the same way, trying to get any sort of sales picture out of just menu data is ridiculous." Their projections from menu scraping are estimates, not actuals. Users who cross-reference with real sales data find discrepancies.

3. **Brand-First, Not Operator-First**  
   Their entire product suite is designed for brands asking "where is my product stocked and at what price?" — NOT for operators asking "how should I price my products to compete?" They show data but don't recommend action.

4. **No AI Pricing Recommendations**  
   Hoodie shows you what's happening. They don't tell you what to DO about it. No Margin Engine equivalent. No "lower Sour D by $3 to gain 12 units/week" recommendations.

5. **No Regulatory Data Integration**  
   Zero integration with CCC filings, license applications, enforcement actions, lab testing results. No Threat Radar equivalent. No THC Accuracy Score.

6. **No Delivery / Fulfillment**  
   Intelligence stops at intelligence. No toggle for delivery, no brand-funded incentives, no toll processing. No action layer.

7. **National = Generic for Any Given State**  
   Their MA data is part of a national dataset. They don't know which CCC meetings matter, which town is about to approve a new license, or what the MA wholesale price collapse means specifically for independent operators in Worcester.

8. **The High Times Consumer Play Is Brand-Driven**  
   Their consumer platform is "find this brand's product near you" — driven by brand marketing budgets. GLASS is "find the cheapest price for this product near you" — driven by consumer intent. These are fundamentally different value propositions.

9. **Hoodie Connect Is a Lead-Gen Tool, Not a Platform**  
   Free for retailers, but what do retailers actually get? Enhanced exposure on High Times and order referrals. That's advertising, not intelligence. GLASS gives free analytics, competitive pricing data, and AI recommendations.

10. **No Cross-Platform Product Matching**  
    Their Product Catalog matches brands' products to menus. But they don't appear to cross-reference the SAME product across Dutchie, Weedmaps, Jane, and Leafly to find price discrepancies. Our GPID does this.

11. **VC Pressure to Go Enterprise**  
    $9.4M raised means they need to show returns. Enterprise deals ($10K+/yr) are the fastest path. This means they'll always prioritize brands and MSOs over independent operators. The SMB market is unprofitable at their cost structure.

12. **Premium Brand Backlash on High Times**  
    From MJBizDaily: Max Vansluys (Dialed In Gummies): "As a premium cannabis brand, e-commerce is not always the friendliest. We're being put up against a much cheaper product." Premium brands don't love price-transparent platforms. Tension between Hoodie's brand clients and their consumer marketplace.

### Customer Complaints / Signals

- **Reddit r/weedbiz** (Sep 2024): "When I took the data they gave me, and went to my customers to see if it matched up with what the data said, it was totally wrong." (This was about menu-scraping based sales estimates generally, applied to Hoodie)
- **Glassdoor**: 3.7/5 compensation rating (only 3 salary data points — very small team)
- **No public customer reviews** on G2, Capterra, or similar platforms — enterprise product, so reviews are rare

## 7. How We Beat Them

### Short-Term: 0-6 Months — Establish Position Before Hoodie Notices

**Strategy: Be invisible to Hoodie, be indispensable to MA operators.**

1. **Launch the free tier NOW and get 20+ operators onboarded before Hoodie even knows GLASS exists.** They're focused on national enterprise sales and the High Times launch. Massachusetts is a rounding error for them. We have a 6-month window.

2. **Own the CCC data layer.** This is our unfair advantage that Hoodie will NEVER build because it's MA-specific and there's no ROI for a national platform to integrate one state's regulatory filings. Threat Radar and THC Accuracy Score are moats.

3. **Ship cross-platform price comparison.** GPID matching across Dutchie + Weedmaps + Jane is something Hoodie doesn't do and won't prioritize. Being able to show operators "Your same product is $3 cheaper on Dutchie vs Weedmaps at the same store" is a unique insight.

4. **Build local brand relationships in MA.** Go to NECANN, MA-specific events, connect with MA brands (Heirloom Collective, The Botanist, etc.). Hoodie sells to Curaleaf and PharmaCann. We sell to the 120 independent operators who can't afford Hoodie.

6. **Monitor Hoodie Connect uptake in MA.** If MA dispensaries start signing up for Hoodie Connect (the free High Times program), we need to know immediately. This is the canary in the coal mine.

### Medium-Term: 6-18 Months — Build Defensible Advantages

**Strategy: Create switching costs and data moats Hoodie can't replicate without going MA-deep.**

1. **GPID becomes the standard in MA.** If we can get operators and brands referencing GLASS Product IDs, we become the canonical product catalog for MA. This is what Hoodie is trying to do nationally. If we win MA first, we're the local standard.

2. **Delivery integration as a moat.** Delivered's license MD1303 is exclusive until 2029. "Toggle Delivery" from the GLASS dashboard is something Hoodie literally cannot offer in MA. This creates stickiness no data platform can replicate.

3. **Cue brand incentive network.** If brands are paying through Cue to incentivize MA dispensary staff, that's a revenue stream AND a data stream Hoodie doesn't have. We know which products are being promoted, how staff responds, and what consumers buy. This is richer than menu scraping.

4. **Operator data sharing (voluntary).** If 50+ operators are voluntarily sharing anonymized sales data through GLASS (in exchange for free analytics), we'll have actual transaction data in MA — the same advantage Headset has nationally and Hoodie lacks.

5. **Build the "Kayak for cannabis" consumer experience.** Our consumer play is fundamentally different from Hoodie's High Times play. They're editorial → commerce (brand-funded). We're search → price comparison (consumer-driven). If a consumer Googles "cheapest Blue Dream near Worcester" and GLASS comes up, that's organic traffic Hoodie can't intercept.

6. **THC Accuracy Score as trust signal.** Cross-referencing lab results with menu claims is unique. If consumers start using GLASS to verify THC percentages before buying, that's a trust moat. Hoodie has no lab data integration.

## 8. Ideas Worth Stealing

### Good Ideas We Should Adopt

1. **"Where to Buy" / Product Locator Widget**  
   Hoodie's embeddable widget for brand websites is smart. Brands embed it → consumers find products → Hoodie gets traffic data. GLASS could offer a similar widget for MA brands: "Find [Brand] near you at the best price." This drives consumer traffic AND gives us brand relationship leverage.

2. **Product Catalog / Brand Data Ingestion**  
   Hoodie lets brands upload their product data to improve menu matching accuracy. We should let MA brands upload their product info (images, descriptions, actual THC%, weights) to improve GPID accuracy. This creates brand engagement AND better data.
