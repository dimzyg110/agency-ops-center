## Tech Stack Analysis

### Menu/Ordering Platforms in MA Market
The MA cannabis dispensary market primarily uses:
- **Dutchie** — Dominant platform. ~50%+ of MA dispensaries use Dutchie for online ordering (235 known Dutchie slugs in GLASS DB)
- **Jane (iHeartJane)** — Second most popular. Used for embedded menus. Acquired some Leafly ordering functionality.
- **Weedmaps** — Primarily listing/menu, some ordering (263 known Weedmaps slugs in GLASS DB)
- **Custom/Native** — A handful of operators (especially MSOs) use proprietary ordering
- **Cannabis Creative Group** — MA-based agency offering native e-commerce menus as Dutchie/Jane alternative (Seth Worby, CEO)

### Key Insight for GLASS Sales
Most MA dispensaries use iFrame-embedded menus from Dutchie/Jane, which means:
1. **They DON'T own their menu data** → GLASS can offer data sovereignty
2. **SEO is weak** because iFrames aren't indexed → GLASS analytics + native content is a differentiator
3. **No cross-platform analytics** → Dispensaries can't easily compare Dutchie vs Weedmaps vs walk-in performance
4. **Customer data fragmented** → Loyalty programs are separate from ordering data

## Social Media Intelligence

### Known Instagram-Active Operators

- **Yamba Market** — Sieh Samura active on social

### Key Challenge for Cannabis Social Media
Meta policies prohibit cannabis businesses from:
- Promoting sales or providing contact info on Instagram
- Using "contact us" tabs in Instagram Business
- Running paid advertisements

This means organic content, community engagement, and alternative channels (email, SMS, in-store) are critical — all of which GLASS can help measure and optimize.
