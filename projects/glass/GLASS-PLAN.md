# GLASS Product Plan — Massachusetts Cannabis OS

**Version 1.0 | February 2026**

## 1. Executive Summary

GLASS is the central nervous system for Massachusetts cannabis operators. It is not another menu platform. It is the intelligence layer operators need to survive a market where wholesale prices have collapsed 72% and margins are razor thin.

We are building GLASS because every operator in Massachusetts is flying blind. They do not know what competitors charge until a customer shows them a cheaper menu. They do not know when a new license opens nearby until customers disappear. They do not know if their pricing is optimized or leaving money on the table.

**The Concept:**
*   **Hub (Free Core):** Price Wire (competitor scraping), Threat Radar (CCC intelligence), Margin Engine (AI pricing).
*   **Spokes (Revenue):** Cue (brand-funded budtender incentives), Delivered (delivery-as-a-service), AC Toll Processing (white-label manufacturing), Roviq (agency services).
*   **Flywheel:** More operators → better data → better recommendations → more operators.

**Strategy:**
GLASS is free for operators to ensure rapid adoption and data density. Revenue is generated by the ecosystem that depends on access to these operators (brands, delivery, processing). We seed the network with 20 "Founding Partners" who get white-glove onboarding, then scale to the full 200-operator market.

## 2. Product Architecture

### 2.1 Core Philosophy: The Command Center
Operators do not want twenty tabs open. They want one screen that tells them what matters right now. The UX follows three principles:
1.  **Zero friction:** No POS integration required to see value.
2.  **Signal over noise:** Every alert is actionable.
3.  **Mobile first, desktop deep:** Check status on phone, dig deep on laptop.

### 2.2 Dashboard Layout

**Primary Dashboard: The Morning Brief (Mobile & Desktop)**
Every operator starts their day here. Updated at 6 AM daily.

*   **Top Bar:** Location selector, "Price Alert" bell, User profile.
*   **Price Wire Today:**
    *   "Top 10 Price Movers" in 5-mile radius.
    *   Category heat map (Flower, Vapes, Edibles).
    *   Your price position: Percentile per category.
*   **Threat Radar:**
    *   New license applications within 5 miles.
    *   Enforcement actions (violations/fines) nearby.
    *   Distress signals (licenses surrendered).
*   **Margin Engine Recommendations:**
    *   3 specific price moves: "Lower Sour D by $3 to gain +12 units/week."
    *   "Apply," "Edit," or "Dismiss" buttons.
*   **Action Center (Spokes):**
    *   **Cue:** "Launch Incentive" for slow-moving inventory.
    *   **Delivered:** Toggle delivery ON/OFF for specific zip codes.
    *   **AC Toll:** "Request Quote" for white-label prerolls based on margin gaps.
    *   **Roviq:** "Schedule Growth Call" if marketing metrics lag.

### 2.3 Key Screens

**Price Wire (Intelligence)**
*   **Map View:** Heat map of competitor pricing by geography.
*   **List View:** Sortable table of SKUs, competitor price range, your price, delta, last change time.
*   **Drilldown:** Product history, volatility, and brand distribution.
*   **Key Interaction:** "Watch" specific products/categories for instant alerts.

**Threat Radar (Defense)**
*   **Data Source:** CCC public filings, meeting minutes, municipal agendas.
*   **Alerts:**
    *   **Red:** New competitor opening < 2 miles.
    *   **Yellow:** Regulatory violation or recall affecting carried brands.
    *   **Blue:** Opportunity (competitor closed/suspended).
*   **Impact Score:** Ranks relevance to specific operator location.

**Margin Engine (Offense)**
*   **Inputs:** Competitor prices, operator sales velocity (if shared), seasonality.
*   **Outputs:** Recommended price, confidence score, simulated impact (revenue/margin).
*   **Feature:** "Apply" button creates a task list for the floor manager.

### 2.4 User Journey

**Day 0: Onboarding (60 Seconds to Value)**
1.  Owner enters store name and city.
2.  System auto-detects menu source (Dutchie/Leafly/etc.).
3.  **Instant Value:** Dashboard populates with *real* competitor data from cache.
4.  Shows 3 "Price Alerts" immediately (e.g., "3 competitors dropped GMO prices today").

**Day 1-7: Habit Formation**
*   **Day 1:** Set up "Price Watch" for top 10 SKUs.
*   **Day 3:** First Margin Engine recommendation delivered via email/SMS.
*   **Day 7:** Spoke interaction (e.g., "Activate Delivery" or "Launch Cue Spiff").

### 2.5 Mobile vs. Desktop
*   **Mobile (70% usage):** Read-only intelligence, quick approvals, floor manager alerts.
*   **Desktop (30% usage):** Deep pricing analysis, CSV exports, campaign building, admin settings.

## 3. Technical Build

### 3.1 Scraping Pipeline (The Heartbeat)
If scraping fails, GLASS dies. We prioritize accuracy over volume.

*   **Targets:** Dutchie (60%+ market), Leafly, Weedmaps, Jane, iHeartJane, custom sites.
*   **Technology:** Playwright (Python/Node) for headless browsing, rotating residential proxies (BrightData), anti-detection (fingerprint rotation).
*   **Pipeline:**
    1.  **Discovery:** Seed list of 200+ MA retailers.
    2.  **Scrape:** Daily full refresh (4 AM), hourly delta checks for "Watched" items.
    3.  **Normalization:** Map raw strings to structured schema (SKU, Brand, Strain, Weight, Price). Fuzzy matching algorithms + manual override tool.
    4.  **Storage:** Postgres for relational data, ClickHouse for time-series price history.

### 3.2 CCC Data Integration
*   **Sources:** Public CCC data feeds, meeting minutes (PDF parsing via NLP), license lists.
*   **Processing:** Geocode all addresses to calculate distance-based threats.
*   **NLP:** Extract "Violation," "Fine," "New License" from unstructured text.

### 3.3 Margin Engine AI
*   **Models:** Gradient Boosted Decision Trees (XGBoost) for interpretability.
*   **Features:** Competitor price index, distance decay, brand strength, time of week.
*   **Output:** Price elasticity curve → Optimal price point.
*   **Feedback Loop:** Operator "Accept/Reject" actions retrain the model.

### 3.4 Tech Stack
*   **Frontend:** React, Vite, Tailwind CSS.
*   **Backend:** Node.js (NestJS or Fastify).
*   **Database:** PostgreSQL (Core), Redis (Queue/Cache), ClickHouse (Analytics).
*   **Infrastructure:** AWS (ECS/RDS) or Fly.io.
*   **Auth:** Clerk or Supabase.

### 3.5 Implementation Roadmap

**Phase 1: Foundation (Weeks 1-6)**
*   Scraper coverage of top 50 MA dispensaries.
*   Database schema and normalization pipeline.
*   Basic web dashboard (Price Wire).
*   Seed 5 friendly operators for feedback.

**Phase 2: Intelligence (Weeks 7-12)**
*   Threat Radar (CCC ingest).
*   Margin Engine v1 (Rule-based).
*   Mobile view optimization.
*   Expand to 20 "Founding Partner" operators.

**Phase 3: Spokes (Weeks 13-20)**
*   Cue integration (Brand funding portal + Budtender view).
*   Delivered integration (Toggle ON/OFF).
*   AC Toll Processing workflow.

## 4. Go-to-Market

### 4.1 Seed Strategy: The First 20
We do not launch to everyone. We hand-pick 20 "Founding Partners" who are market leaders and innovators.
*   **Offer:** Free lifetime access to Core features + white-glove onboarding.
*   **Requirement:** Weekly feedback call + data sharing (anonymized sales velocity).
*   **Outreach:** Direct 1:1 sales by Roviq team. "Price Wire in 48 hours or we pay you."

### 4.2 Pricing Strategy
*   **Core (Price Wire, Threat Radar, Margin Engine Lite):** **Free Forever.**
    *   Why? Zero friction adoption. We need the network effect.
*   **Margin Engine Pro:** **$399/month** per location (Free for first 30 days).
    *   Includes: Automated API pricing updates, advanced elasticity models, CSV exports.
*   **Cue:** **Brand-Funded.**
    *   Operators pay $0. Brands pay tiered subscription ($500-$5,000/mo) + per-spiff fees.
*   **Delivered:** **15% of GMV.**
    *   No fixed cost to operator. Pure performance.
*   **AC Toll Processing:** **Per-unit fee.**
    *   Market rate + material costs. GLASS takes referral commission.
*   **Roviq Agency:** **Service Retainer.**
    *   $2,500/mo base. GLASS users get priority scheduling.

### 4.3 Newsletter Funnel ("The GLASS Weekly")
*   **Content:** Market pulse, top price movers, CCC alerts, anonymous case studies.
*   **Goal:** Drive signups by showing data they *don't* have.
*   **CTA:** "Claim your Price Wire link" in every email.

## 5. Revenue Model

**Year 1 Projections (Conservative)**
*   **Assumptions:** 50 Operators (25% market share), 10 paying for Pro, 5 using Delivered/AC.
*   **Core Pro:** 10 stores * $399 * 12 = **$47,880**
*   **Cue:** 12 Brands * $800/mo avg = **$115,200**
*   **Delivered:** 5 stores * $1,500/mo margin = **$90,000**
*   **AC Toll:** 5M units * $0.05 comm = **$250,000**
*   **Roviq Upsell:** 4 clients * $30k/yr = **$120,000**
*   **Total Year 1:** **~$623,000**

**Year 3 Potential (Scale)**
*   150 Operators, deeply integrated ecosystem.
*   **Total Revenue:** **$3M+** driven largely by Cue and AC Toll volume.

**Unit Economics**
*   **CAC:** ~$500 (blended warm/cold outreach).
*   **LTV:** ~$4,000+ (36 months).
*   **Scraping Cost:** ~$40/month per operator.
*   **Break-even:** ~90 operators on free tier (covered by Spoke revenue).

## 6. Competitive Moat

1.  **Local Data Density:** MA is a small, unique market. National players (Dutchie/Leafly) use generic models. We use MA-specific scraping, CCC data, and local relationships.
2.  **Exclusive Delivery License:** Delivered holds license MD1303 (exclusive until 2029). No competitor can replicate the "Toggle Delivery" feature legally without this license.
3.  **Network Effects:**
    *   **Data:** More operators = better pricing models = more operators.
    *   **Commercial:** Brands flock to Cue because reliable operators are there. Operators flock to GLASS because brands are paying incentives.
4.  **Integrated Workflow:** Competitors sell isolated tools. GLASS integrates Intelligence (Price Wire) → Action (Margin Engine) → Fulfillment (Delivered/AC) → Growth (Roviq).

## 7. Risks and Mitigations

| Risk | Impact | Mitigation |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Scraping Blocks** | High | Rotate residential proxies; diversify sources (menu aggregators vs. direct sites); manual fallback teams. Escrow fund for proxy costs. |
| **Legal/Compliance** | Medium | Only scrape public data; strict adherence to CCC ad rules for Cue; legal review of "inducement" regulations for brand incentives. |
| **Data Accuracy** | High | Confidence scores on all matches; "Report Error" button for operators; manual QA on outliers >20% deviation. |
| **Adoption Lag** | Medium | Free core tier removes barrier; "60 seconds to value" onboarding; focus on pain (margin compression) rather than "tech." |
| **Runway** | High | Lean team; prioritize revenue-generating spokes (Cue/AC) early; use Roviq agency cash flow to subsidize dev. |
