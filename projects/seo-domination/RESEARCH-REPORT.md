## 1. Executive Summary {#executive-summary}

Cannabis Healing (CH) is a dispensary at 4 Neptune Road, East Boston, MA 02128. It has **massive untapped SEO potential** and faces a competitive but winnable local market. Our primary competitor model is Rolling Releaf, which dominates Boston-area cannabis delivery search rankings using SERPCOM's Prometheus platform—a programmatic SEO system that auto-generates local town/city landing pages.

### Key Findings

1. **SERPCOM's Prometheus** is a WordPress-based publishing platform that uses automated keyword targeting and programmatic page generation. Rolling Releaf has 40+ town-specific landing pages, each targeting "[service] + [town]" keywords. This is their #1 SEO advantage—and it's **completely replicable with AI tools at 10x speed**.

2. **Rolling Releaf** dominates Boston delivery search despite having only ~235 Instagram followers. Their SEO secret is purely programmatic local pages + native product catalog (not embedded iframes from Dutchie).

3. **Cannabis Healing's site is compromised**—there are spam/junk content pages indexed (e.g., "Chatib review" categories), minimal SEO optimization, and almost no local landing pages. With 1,173 Instagram followers (5x Rolling Releaf) and solid reviews, CH has brand recognition but zero organic search presence.

4. **East Boston is highly competitive** with BOUTIQ, Happy Valley, East Boston Cannabis, Berkshire Roots, and Apex Noire all competing. But NONE have robust programmatic SEO strategies. First mover wins.

5. **Answer Engine Optimization (AEO)** is the 2026 frontier. Structuring content for AI-generated answers (ChatGPT, Google AI Overviews, Perplexity) is the next competitive moat. No cannabis company in MA is doing this yet.

6. **GLASS data integration** (scraped price/product data from dispensaries) can power the most data-rich, SEO-optimized product pages in the state—a structural advantage no competitor can easily replicate.

### Bottom Line
An 8-week blitz can take CH from near-invisible to page-one rankings for 50+ local keywords. Cost: ~$800-1,500/month in tools. Execution is 90% automatable with AI. The SERPCOM Prometheus approach is solid but slow and manual—we can build a system that generates better content, faster, at scale.

## 2. SERPCOM Prometheus System — Reverse Engineering {#serpcom-prometheus}

### 2.1 What Is Prometheus?

Prometheus is SERPCOM's proprietary **"Publishing Platform"**—a WordPress-based CMS framework designed for SEO-first website generation. Key characteristics:

- **"SEO Booster Site" concept**: A secondary website (or subdomain structure) that can improve rankings **without editing or replacing the client's main site**
- **Social publishing integration**: Automated syndication of content across social channels
- **Deep keyword targeting**: Research-first approach where keyword analysis drives site architecture
- **Automated page generation**: Programmatic creation of location/service pages at scale
- **Enterprise-level SEO for small businesses**: Their tagline—providing big-agency SEO structure at small-business prices

### 2.4 Reverse-Engineered Methodology

Based on analysis of their client sites, blog posts, and public documentation, SERPCOM's cannabis SEO playbook is:

#### A. Technical Foundation
1. **WordPress CMS** with custom Prometheus framework
2. **SEO-first site architecture**: URL structure, header hierarchy, internal linking all planned before content
3. **Native product catalog** (NOT embedded Dutchie/Jane iframes): Products live on the WordPress site with proper schema markup, allowing Google to index individual product pages
4. **Product Schema markup** (JSON-LD): Proper structured data for cannabis products including price, availability, THC/CBD content, product type
5. **Dutchie/POS integration via sync** (not embed): They sync inventory data to native WordPress product pages, giving both real-time accuracy AND SEO indexability

#### B. Programmatic Local Pages
This is the **key weapon**. SERPCOM generates location-specific landing pages at scale:
- Template: `/weed-delivery-dispensary-company-greater-boston/weed-delivery-dispensary-company-[town]-ma/`
- Each page has: unique (but templated) copy, local service details, local area context, CTAs
- Rolling Releaf has 40-50+ such pages covering: Boston, Cambridge, Somerville, Malden, Dorchester, Allston, Framingham, Natick, Woburn, Seaport, Newton, and many more
- **Content quality**: Moderate. Pages are clearly templated with town-name swaps. Some have neighborhood-level content (e.g., Framingham mentions "Cochituate Road Corridor"). Most are 200-400 words of fairly generic copy.

#### C. Content Strategy
- Blog posts: Weekly to biweekly cadence covering strain education, delivery guides, compliance info
- SEO-optimized titles and meta descriptions
- Internal linking from blog posts to town pages and product pages
- Social media syndication (Instagram, Facebook)

#### D. Link Building
- Cannabis directories: Weedmaps, Leafly, Yelp, Nextdoor listings
- Local business citations: NAP consistency across platforms
- No evidence of aggressive backlink building beyond directory listings

#### E. Review Strategy
- Google review collection
- Yelp optimization
- Testimonials on website (Rolling Releaf has multiple featured reviews)

## 5. Top Cannabis SEO Strategies 2025-2026 {#top-strategies}

### 5.1 Local SEO & Google Business Profile (GBP)

**The #1 priority.** 46% of all Google searches have local intent. "Dispensary near me" is among the highest-converting searches in cannabis.

**Best Practices:**
- Complete GBP profile: all categories, attributes, products, services
- Weekly GBP posts with photos and offers
- Review velocity: aim for 5+ new reviews/month
- Q&A section optimization (seed your own questions)
- Products catalog in GBP
- Accurate hours, holiday hours updated
- Photo optimization: exterior, interior, products, team
- UTM-tagged website link for tracking

### 5.2 Programmatic Local Pages

The strategy that's winning in 2025-2026. Create templated but unique landing pages for every:
- **Town/city** in your service area
- **Neighborhood** within Boston (Back Bay, South End, Eastie, Southie, etc.)
- **Landmark proximity** ("dispensary near Logan Airport", "dispensary near TD Garden")
- **Competitor location** ("better alternative to [competitor] in East Boston")

**2026 Level-Up:**
- Each page should be 1,500+ words with genuinely unique local content
- Include local events, landmarks, parking info, transit directions
- Embed local Google Map with custom pin
- FAQ schema with location-specific Q&As
- Include real-time menu/product data synced from POS
- Spanish-language variant for each page (in East Boston)

### 5.3 Content Clusters & Topical Authority

Build pillar-and-cluster content architecture:

**Example Pillar: "Complete Guide to Cannabis in East Boston"**
- Cluster: "Best Strains for Beginners in Massachusetts"
- Cluster: "Indica vs Sativa: What Boston Consumers Need to Know"
- Cluster: "Cannabis Edibles Dosage Guide"
- Cluster: "How to Read Cannabis Labels in MA"
- Cluster: "Cannabis for Pain Management"
- Cluster: "Cannabis Laws in Massachusetts 2026"

**Key Themes for CH:**
- Strain education (effect-based: energy, relaxation, pain relief, sleep)
- Product category guides (flower, edibles, vapes, concentrates, pre-rolls)
- Local cannabis culture in East Boston
- Cannabis compliance/regulation in MA
- Wellness/healing content (aligned with "Cannabis Healing" brand)
- Multilingual content for East Boston's community

### 5.4 Schema Markup for Cannabis

Implement comprehensive JSON-LD structured data:

1. **LocalBusiness** (or more specific: Store > CannabisBusiness if available)
   - Name, address, phone, hours, geo-coordinates, image
2. **Product** schema for every product page
   - Name, description, price, availability, brand, SKU, THC/CBD content
3. **FAQ** schema on every educational page
4. **BreadcrumbList** for site navigation
5. **Review/AggregateRating** for products and business
6. **Event** schema for any in-store events
7. **Article** schema for blog posts
8. **HowTo** schema for guides

### 5.5 Answer Engine Optimization (AEO) — The 2026 Frontier

This is **the emerging battleground** that NO cannabis company in MA is executing on:

- **Structure content for AI extraction**: Clear headers, concise definitions, factual statements that LLMs can cite
- **FAQ sections on every page**: Direct question-answer format
- **"People Also Ask" targeting**: Research and answer the exact questions Google shows
- **Entities and knowledge graphs**: Establish CH as a known entity through consistent NAP, Wikipedia-style structured data, and cross-platform consistency
- **Voice search optimization**: Natural language queries ("Where's the closest dispensary to Logan Airport?")
- **LLM optimization**: Ensure content is structured so ChatGPT, Claude, Perplexity cite CH when recommending Boston dispensaries

### 5.6 Cannabis-Specific Link Building

**Proven Strategies:**
1. Cannabis directories (Leafly, Weedmaps, Dutchie, Jane, AllBud)
2. Local business citations (Google, Yelp, Bing Places, Apple Maps, Foursquare)
3. Product brand "Where to Buy" pages (ask brands you carry to link to CH)
4. Local event sponsorships (community events, charity, cultural events in Eastie)
5. Boston.gov and masscannabiscontrol.com links (CH is already listed on both!)
6. Chamber of Commerce listings
7. Guest posts on cannabis industry publications
8. Data-driven content that earns natural backlinks (price reports, market analysis via GLASS data)
9. East Boston community organization partnerships
10. HARO/journalist outreach for cannabis industry commentary

### 5.7 Native Product Catalog (NOT Iframe Embeds)

The #1 technical SEO insight from SERPCOM's approach:

**DON'T embed Dutchie/Jane/Weedmaps menu iframes.**

Instead:
- Sync POS data to native WordPress/site product pages
- Each product gets its own SEO-optimized URL: `/products/blue-dream-flower/`
- Unique AI-generated product descriptions (not manufacturer copy)
- Product schema markup with price, THC%, availability
- Internal linking from product pages to strain guides, effect pages, etc.
- User reviews on product pages

This is what makes Rolling Releaf rank for individual product searches. CH can do this better with GLASS data providing richer product information.

### 7.2 GLASS Data Integration

GLASS (the price/product data scraping system already being built) becomes the **secret weapon**:

| GLASS Data | SEO Use |
|-----------|---------|
| Product prices from competitors | "Price comparison" content pages, rich snippets |
| Product inventory/availability | Real-time "in stock" product pages with schema |
| Strain/product details (THC%, effects) | AI-generated unique product descriptions |
| Competitor pricing trends | Data-driven blog content ("Cannabis Price Trends in Boston") |
| Menu updates | Auto-updated product pages (freshness signal for Google) |
| New product launches | Auto-generated "New Arrivals" pages |

**Data Flow:**
1. GLASS scrapes competitor + client POS data → Database
2. AI engine generates unique product descriptions, price comparison content, market trend articles
3. WordPress REST API publishes content automatically
4. Schema generator adds JSON-LD to each page
5. Sitemap auto-updates and pings Google
