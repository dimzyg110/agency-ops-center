### SEO Connection (from Research Report)

The proposal references Rolling Releaf's programmatic SEO success and proposes building "the same system, better." Based on the SEO domination research:

1. **The programmatic SEO claim is validated.** Rolling Releaf has 40-50+ town pages built by SERPCOM, and they do dominate delivery searches in Greater Boston.
2. **The "200+ target municipalities" claim is ambitious but directionally correct.** MA has 351 municipalities; not all allow cannabis, but 100+ programmatic pages is realistic and valuable.
3. **The "$5,000-15,000/month equivalent in paid search value" claim is reasonable** for 50-100 page-one rankings in cannabis delivery keywords.
4. **The Delivered proposal's SEO approach should be upgraded** with the AEO, schema markup, Spanish-language, and GLASS data integration findings from the research report.

### Potential Weaknesses to Address
- Revenue projections are based on industry averages, not Delivered's actual numbers (acknowledged in doc)
- App Store risk with cannabis apps is real but mitigation plan is solid
- Equity ask (8-12%) is significant — Delivered may push back
- No specific SEO timeline or milestones in the proposal (the 8-week plan from the research report could be adapted)
- License renewal deadline (March 5, 2026) is ~3 weeks away — this is the most urgent item
