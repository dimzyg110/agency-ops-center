## 3. Actionability — **B+**

### What Makes It Executable
- ✅ Specific AI prompts for graphic creation (very practical)
- ✅ Copy templates with actual text that can be used directly
- ✅ Clear weekly posting schedule
- ✅ Implementation checklist with week-by-week breakdown
- ✅ Tools recommended (CapCut, InShot, Canva)
- ✅ Equipment list for video production (phone, stabilizer, ring light)
- ✅ Hashtag strategy included
- ✅ Alpine IQ trigger conditions clearly defined

### Concerns
- ⚠️ **Volume may be overwhelming** — 3-5 videos per week + all the graphics + Alpine campaigns is a lot. Who's doing this work?
- ⚠️ **"Questions for CH Team" section at the end suggests this hasn't been capacity-checked** — roles not yet assigned
- ⚠️ **Video production requires skills/time** — "Film 5 core videos" in Week 2 assumes significant production capacity
- ⚠️ **No clear owner specified** — Document says "assign roles" but doesn't have them filled in
- ⚠️ **Content folder system mentioned but not created** — This should exist before launch

### Recommendations
1. **Add a "Minimum Viable Calendar"** — What's the bare minimum if capacity is limited? (e.g., 3 posts/week instead of daily + 3 Alpine campaigns)
2. **Assign specific owners now** — Even if names need to be confirmed, role types should be defined (Social Lead, Alpine Manager, Video Producer, Copy Writer)
3. **Batch content creation is suggested but could be more prescriptive** — Add templates/checklists for the batch sessions

**Verdict:** The calendar is detailed enough to execute, but resource allocation is unclear. Needs capacity confirmation.

## 4. Missing Elements — **B**

### What's Not Covered That Should Be

#### Critical Gaps
1. **No crisis/contingency messaging** — What if there's a product recall? A PR issue? A major weather event (Boston blizzards)? Should have 2-3 template responses.

2. **No competitive differentiation content** — The calendar doesn't include any "why us vs. other dispensaries" content beyond location. Could add:
   - Comparison graphics (tastefully done)
   - "What makes CH different" series
   - Staff expertise spotlights

3. **No SMS vs. Email strategy distinction** — Alpine IQ supports both. The copy templates seem email-length. Should clarify:
   - Which campaigns are SMS?
   - Character limits for SMS?
   - Compliance disclaimers?

4. **No Google Business Profile optimization** — Critical for "near Logan" search intent. Should include:
   - Weekly GBP post schedule
   - Review response templates
   - Photo upload cadence

5. **No referral program content** — "Tell a friend" campaigns are missing. Given the travel audience (likely one-time visitors), referral incentives could drive local adoption.

#### Nice-to-Haves
- **User-generated content guidelines** — How to solicit, how to get permission, how to repost
- **Influencer/partnership strategy** — Travel bloggers? Boston creators? Flight attendant networks?
- **Event/in-store activation content** — 4/20 mentions live music, but no detailed plan
- **Blog/SEO content calendar** — Just social/email, no long-form content for Google rankings
- **Out-of-home advertising coordination** — If CH does any billboards/transit ads, content should align

**Verdict:** The calendar is strong on social and email but misses some important channels and edge cases.
