## 3. The App: First Cannabis Delivery App in Massachusetts

### React Native — iOS + Android from Day One

This is a delivery utility app, not a cinematic brand experience. **React Native** gives us both platforms simultaneously, eliminates the Android gap (Android exceeds 50% in urban/working-class demographics), and keeps development focused.

**The architecture is clean: the Delivered app is the frontend. Blaze POS is the backend.** Menu/inventory syncs via Blaze's open API (`apidocs.blaze.me`) in real-time. Order placement, payment processing, and delivery tracking all flow through Blaze — which already handles dispatch with route optimization, driver mobile tools (navigation, in-app chat, digital cash drawer, ID verification, signatures), and manifest compliance. We don't build those systems from scratch. We build the customer experience on top of them. This de-risks the technical build significantly.

### Core Features (V1 MVP)

- **Ordering**: Full product catalog synced via Blaze API, smart search (strain, THC%, price, effects), favorites, cart with real-time inventory verification
- **Real-time tracking**: Live driver location pulled from Blaze's driver app, dynamic ETA, "5 minutes away" alerts, delivery confirmation
- **Push notifications**: Order status, flash sales, restock alerts, loyalty milestones — $0.00 per send vs. $0.01-0.05 per SMS
- **Loyalty program**: Points per dollar, 3-tier progression (Silver → Gold → Platinum), referral bonuses (give $10, get $10), birthday rewards
- **Payment**: ACH, PIN-debit, and cash via Blaze's integrated payment processing. No credit cards — cannabis can't use them. The app solves the friction of compliant payment with a clean UX.

### MA Delivery Compliance (935 CMR 500)

The app isn't just convenient — it enforces compliance at every step:

- **Pre-verification**: First-time customers complete ID verification during account creation (stored encrypted, opt-in)
- **Door delivery**: ID scanned at the door — must match pre-verified customer. Customer must be physically present per 935 CMR 500 (no roommate/concierge/neighbor handoffs)
- **Residence only**: Delivery to private residences only — no hotels, dorms, public housing, or shelters
- **Manifests**: Every route generates a manifest tracked through Blaze, with real-time driver position logging
- **Municipal hours**: Delivery window enforcement varies by municipality — the app auto-enforces per zone
- **Purchase limits**: Automatic enforcement of per-customer, per-day limits

All integrated with Blaze's backend: ID verification photos, digital signatures, manifest tracking, and delivery confirmation flow through one system.

### Scaling: What It Actually Takes

### Digital Scaling (Cheap, Fast — No New License)

Delivered's license (MD1303) covers statewide delivery. Expanding to new zones is a digital operation:
- Coverage zone expansion: any municipality allowing adult-use retail
- Programmatic SEO page per zone goes live automatically
- No new license for new zones — only CCC amendments for new warehouse locations
- Cost: near-zero per new zone

### Physical Scaling (The Real Bottleneck)

**Warehouses:**
- Current: 1 warehouse (Clinton, 75 Green St) — serves ~60 min delivery radius efficiently
- Boston/Western MA/Cape each need additional warehouses
- New warehouse = CCC location amendment on existing license (not a new license application)
- Cost: $3-8K/month lease + security buildout (cameras, vault, access control per 935 CMR 500)

**Vehicles:**
- Must be unmarked — no cannabis branding per 935 CMR 500
- GPS tracking and real-time manifests required
- Cannabis transport insurance = higher premiums
- ~$500-800/month per vehicle all-in

**Drivers:**
- Must complete CCC Delivery Core Curriculum before first delivery
- Background checks + CCC agent registration required
- Body cameras, ID verification photos, digital signatures per 935 CMR 500
- One order per consumer per trip per day
- ~$18-25/hour + benefits in MA

### Scaling Phases

- **Phase 1 (Months 1-6)**: Densify Central MA — expand from ~30 to 50-60 towns with existing infrastructure. Digital only, no new ops costs.
- **Phase 2 (Months 6-9)**: Hub to Boston — second warehouse (metro west), 2-3 new drivers. Cost: ~$11-16K/month additional.
- **Phase 3 (Months 9-18)**: Statewide network with VC capital — 3-4 warehouses, 10-15 drivers, $40-60K/month ops burn, $200K+/month revenue target.

**The license IS the moat.** Scaling is operational, not regulatory. The real limiter is capital for warehouses + drivers — that's what the Seed round funds.
