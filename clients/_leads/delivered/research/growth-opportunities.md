## STEP 1: CURRENT REVENUE MODEL (Reverse-Engineered)

### Economics of Cannabis Delivery in MA
*   **Gross Margins:** Typical retail margins are 40-50%. However, as a delivery operator, Delivered likely faces higher COGS due to purchasing from cultivators at wholesale. 
*   **Net Margins:** Delivery is labor and logistics intensive. Between driver pay, fuel, insurance (GL/Auto/Cargo), and compliance (body cams, GPS), net margins are thin (5-15%).
*   **Order Minimums:** Delivered uses a tiered minimum system ($25 local, up to $150 for outlying zones like Millbury). This is a survival tactic to maintain a "Trip AOV" that covers the cost of the driver.

### Estimated Revenue Profile
*   **AOV (Average Order Value):** Estimated at **$95 - $115**. Delivery customers tend to buy in bulk to hit minimums and avoid multiple delivery windows.
*   **Customer Frequency:** High-intent consumers order **1.8x per month**. Low-frequency "tourist" or casual users order **0.5x per month**.
*   **Payment Methods:** 
    *   **ACH (Dutchie Pay/Aeropay):** Best for retention. Lower friction for re-orders.
    *   **Debit:** High processing fees (usually passed to customer as a convenience fee).
    *   **Cash:** High risk, high friction, requires "change" management for drivers. *Shift to 90% digital is required for scaling.*

## STEP 4: DELIVERY APP OPPORTUNITY

Delivered is currently "Web-First." To win, they must be "App-First."

### Why an App?
1.  **Push Notifications:** **98% open rate** vs 20% for email. Real-time "Happy Hour" alerts or "Your Favorite Strain is Back" triggers.
2.  **Frictionless Re-ordering:** "Buy again" in 2 clicks.
3.  **Real-Time Tracking:** Uber-style map tracking reduces "delivery anxiety" and customer support tickets.
4.  **Loyalty Integration:** Points balance visible on the home screen.

### Comparison
*   **Zyp Run:** Proprietary tech stack. This is why they are winning. They own the data and the UX.
*   **DoorDash:** High fees, low control, branding is diluted.

### Roviq Build Opportunity
*   **Cost:** $75k - $125k for a custom React Native wrapper with full POS (Dutchie/Blaze) integration.
*   **ROI:** A 15% increase in retention (frequency) pays for the app in < 6 months for a $3M/year operator.
