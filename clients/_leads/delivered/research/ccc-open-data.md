### Social Equity Exclusivity Window

| Event | Date |
|-------|------|
| **Exclusivity period began** | April 1, 2022 (when first Delivery Operator commenced ops) |
| **Original 36-month period would end** | April 1, 2025 |
| **First extension (12 months)** | Approved March 13, 2025 (unanimous 3-0 vote) |
| **Current expiration** | **April 1, 2026** |
| **Proposed further extension** | **Until April 2029** (voted unanimously 4-0 on Jan 2026) |
| **Status of extension** | In public comment period (Feb 2026), expected finalized before 4/1/2026 |

**Key context:** During the exclusivity period, delivery licenses (Courier, Operator, Microbusiness Delivery) are available **only** to businesses with majority ownership by Certified Economic Empowerment Priority Applicants (EEA) or Social Equity Program (SEP) participants.

The CCC commissioned a **UMass Donahue Institute study** that concluded the goal of "full industry participation" has NOT been fully met — delivery licenses represent only **5% of all adult-use licenses** in Massachusetts. The Commission is **highly likely to extend exclusivity to April 2029**.

**Implication:** Non-social-equity businesses cannot obtain delivery licenses until at least April 2026, and almost certainly April 2029.

## 4. Sales Data & Trends

### Annual Delivery Channel Sales (CCC Open Data)

| Year | Delivery Sales | Retail Sales | Delivery % of Total | YoY Growth |
|------|---------------|-------------|--------------------:|----------:|
| 2021 | $2,608,612 | $1,331,185,961 | 0.20% | — |
| 2022 | $8,810,705 | $1,483,917,799 | 0.59% | +238% |
| 2023 | $13,438,391 | $1,558,880,805 | 0.85% | +53% |
| 2024 | $16,875,235 | $1,631,709,668 | 1.02% | +26% |
| 2025 | $20,361,403 | $1,583,218,538 | 1.27% | +21% |

### Delivery Operators vs. Couriers Revenue Split

| Year | Operator Sales | Courier Sales | Total Delivery | Operator Share |
|------|---------------|--------------|---------------|---------------:|
| 2021 | $120,141 | $2,488,472 | $2,608,612 | 4.6% |
| 2022 | $1,918,771 | $6,891,935 | $8,810,705 | 21.8% |
| 2023 | $7,390,142 | $6,048,249 | $13,438,391 | 55.0% |
| 2024 | $12,312,516 | $4,562,719 | $16,875,235 | 73.0% |
| 2025 | $16,039,098 | $4,322,305 | $20,361,403 | **78.8%** |

### Key Trends
1. **Delivery is growing but still tiny** — 1.27% of total cannabis sales in 2025
2. **Delivery Operators are dominating Couriers** — Operators went from 4.6% of delivery revenue (2021) to 78.8% (2025). The warehouse/wholesale model is winning over the courier-from-dispensary model.
3. **Growth rate is decelerating** — 238% growth in 2022, down to 21% in 2025. The channel is maturing but still small.
4. **Total MA cannabis market:** ~$1.6B annually (retail + delivery combined in 2025)
5. **Delivery total through 2025:** ~$62M cumulative since inception
6. **Retail sales actually declined** in 2025 ($1.58B vs $1.63B in 2024) — possible market contraction/saturation

### Average Transaction Value
Not directly available in aggregate CCC data. The daily product-level data in the ITD Sales Report allows computation by dividing daily TOTAL_$ by transaction count, but individual transaction-level data is not published.

## Key Takeaways for Roviq

1. **Delivered, Inc. is a real, active, licensed Delivery Operator** (License MD1303, Clinton, MA) — Social Equity participant, operating since June 2023, covering Central MA extensively.

2. **The delivery market is still nascent** — $20M in 2025 on a $1.6B market (1.27%). Massive growth potential.

3. **Delivery Operators (wholesale model) are winning** — 79% of delivery revenue now flows through operators vs. couriers. This matters for any company positioning in this space.

4. **Social equity exclusivity almost certainly extends to 2029** — Non-SE businesses cannot get delivery licenses. This is a competitive moat for existing licensees like Delivered Inc., Zyp Run, and Rolling Releaf.

5. **Advanced Cultivators can supply delivery operators** — as a Lowell-based cultivator, AC could wholesale to any licensed delivery operator. Their pending manufacturer license would expand product range.

6. **Cannabis Healing has NO delivery presence** — retail only, East Boston. No delivery license or application found. If they want delivery, they'd need to partner with a licensed courier or operator (or apply themselves, but only if SE-eligible).

7. **SERPCOM is a marketing vendor to Rolling Releaf**, not an owner. Competitive intel: SERPCOM is active in cannabis digital marketing in MA.
