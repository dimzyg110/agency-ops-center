## STEP 4 — AUDIENCE ANALYSIS

### Demographics (Inferred from Platform Data)
- **Primary:** 21-35 year old cannabis consumers in Central Massachusetts
- **Secondary:** 35-50 year olds interested in convenience/delivery
- **Language:** English primary, with Spanish and Portuguese-speaking customer base (per Leafly feature, founders serve in all three languages — this is NOT reflected in social content)
- **Geographic:** Should be hyper-local (Clinton, Worcester, Leominster, Fitchburg, Marlborough, Shrewsbury service areas) but content doesn't geo-target

### Real vs. Purchased Followers Assessment
- **Verdict: Likely NOT purchased, but growth was artificially inflated through follow-for-follow**
- The 2,443 following count (nearly 2x their followers) is classic follow/unfollow growth hacking
- At 1,123 followers after ~2.5 years of operation, organic growth is very slow
- Engagement rates suggest followers are real but not deeply connected to the brand
- No signs of bot-like follower patterns (purchased followers typically show as massive counts with zero engagement)

### Local vs. Broader Audience
- **Concern:** Likely a significant portion of followers are NOT in service area
  - Follow-for-follow tactics pull in followers from anywhere
  - Cannabis accounts often attract national/global followers with no purchase intent
  - Content doesn't leverage location tags or local community hooks
  - **Estimated local/relevant followers: 30-40% at best**

### #1 Content Type to Create More

**🏆 Behind-the-scenes "Day in the Life" Reels showing real delivery runs**

Why: 
- Zero production cost (phone + driver)
- Humanizes the brand (delivery drivers ARE the brand)
- Perfect for Instagram Reels AND TikTok launch
- Localizes content naturally (driving through Central MA towns)
- Creates authentic connection that promotional posts never will
- Zyp Run's event content works because it shows REAL PEOPLE — Delivered needs to do the same with their daily operations

---

### #1 Thing to Stop Doing

**🛑 Stop the follow-for-follow growth strategy IMMEDIATELY**

Why:
- 2,443 following with 1,123 followers screams desperation to the algorithm and to potential customers
- It's attracting non-local, non-customer followers who will never order
- It tanks engagement rate (more followers who don't care = lower %)
- **Action:** Unfollow non-customers/non-industry accounts in batches of 50/day until following is under 500. Focus on organic growth through content quality.

### Quick Win This Week

**📱 Post a 30-second Reel: "What it's ACTUALLY like getting cannabis delivered to your door in Massachusetts"**

Script concept:
1. Open on phone — placing order on thedeliveredinc.com (3 seconds)
2. Text notification: "Your order is on its way!" (2 seconds)  
3. Cut to driver loading up, driving through Central MA (5 seconds)
4. Driver texts "5 minutes away" (2 seconds)
5. Doorbell. Customer opens door. Smiling delivery agent with bag. (5 seconds)
6. ID check. Sign. Done. (3 seconds)
7. Customer sitting on couch, product in hand. "That was easy." (5 seconds)
8. End card: "Don't Drive, Get Delivered 🍃" + website (5 seconds)

**Post natively to Instagram Reels. Cross-post to a new TikTok account. Pin to profile.**

Why this works:
- Demystifies cannabis delivery for the curious customer
- Shows the actual process (transparency builds trust)
- Features a real person (humanizes brand)
- Creates the exact content that search engines show people are looking for on TikTok ("cannabis delivery review")
- Can be produced TODAY with a phone
