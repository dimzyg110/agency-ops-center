## STEP 5 — CONTENT GAPS & OPPORTUNITIES

1.  **THE TIKTOK VOID:** Not being on TikTok is a death sentence for a new streetwear brand in 2026. This is where 90% of brand discovery happens.
2.  **Short-Form Video (Reels/Shorts):** The "Cinematic" nature of Roviq's offer is perfectly suited here. The brand needs high-quality, moody visuals of the *lifestyle* (cars, night drives, studio process), not just the shirt.
3.  **Founder Story:** Who is behind "Decoded"? The "Noir" mystery is cool, but people buy from people. A "Building the Brand" series is low-hanging fruit.
4.  **UGC/Seeding:** No evidence of influencers or customers wearing the product.
5.  **Educational/Contextual:** Explain the "911 Turbo" connection. Why that car? Is it the founder's? Create content *around* the car, not just the shirt.

## STEP 7 — VERDICT & ACTION PLAN

### **Overall Social Grade: D**
*The brand has a product and a vibe ("Noir"), but no microphone. It is speaking into the void.*

*   **Instagram:** C- (Exists, but static)
*   **TikTok:** F (Non-existent)
*   **YouTube:** F (Non-existent)

### **The #1 Thing to Create MORE Of:**
**Cinematic "Mood" Reels.**
*Example:* A 7-second loop of the "911 Turbo Shirt" draped over the seat of an actual Porsche, driving through the city at night. Lo-fi hip-hop audio. Text overlay: "Driven by purpose."
*Why:* It sells the **lifestyle**, not the cotton.

### **The #1 Thing to STOP Doing:**
**Posting "Flat Lays" on white backgrounds.**
*Why:* It looks like a drop-shipping store. Streetwear is about *context*. Shoot on concrete, on cars, on people.

### **Quick Win (This Week):**
**Launch the TikTok Account.**
*Action:* Secure `@decoded.shop` (or `@decodednoir`) on TikTok. Post ONE video: A high-energy montage of the "911 Turbo" shirt with the sound of a revving engine. Caption: "We don't just wear it. We drive it. Collection 001 Loading... #streetwear #911turbo"

### **90-Day Strategy (High Level):**
1.  **Month 1: The Tease (Community Building).** Don't push sales. Push the *vision*. Post daily Reels/TikToks defining "Decoded Noir". Use car content as a hook to find the audience.
2.  **Month 2: The Seeding.** Send 20 hoodies to micro-influencers in the car/fashion niche (not randoms). Ask for *video* returns, not photos.
3.  **Month 3: The Drop.** Close the website. Build an SMS list (SMSBump). Open the site for 24 hours only. Sell out. Repeat.
