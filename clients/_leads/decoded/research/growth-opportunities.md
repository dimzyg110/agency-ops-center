# Decoded Shop — Revenue Growth Opportunities
## Roviq Fractional CMO Assessment | February 2026
## Audited by: Kimi K2.5 (NVIDIA/Moonshot)

---

## EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

**Decoded Shop** is a luxury streetwear brand operating on Shopify with 40 SKUs. Their "Decoded Noir" positioning targets fashion-conscious buyers seeking exclusivity, but they're leaving significant revenue on the table. Their initial ask — mobile app + growth plan — signals awareness of gaps they can't fill internally.

**Immediate Issues:**
- No mobile conversion funnel (critical: 70%+ of fashion traffic is mobile)
- Zero visible retention infrastructure (subscriptions, loyalty, referral)
- Limited product catalog without bundle/upsell logic
- No evidence of email/SMS capture or retention marketing

**Estimated Current State:**
- Revenue: Likely $100K-$500K annually (inferred from SKU count, pricing, traffic absence)
- AOV: ~$65-$85
- Gross Margin: 60-65% (luxury DTC standard)
- CAC: Unknown (red flag — no tracking visible)

## STEP 2 — CUSTOMER JOURNEY MAPPING

### Journey: Never Heard → Repeat Buyer

```
DISCOVERY → CONSIDERATION → PURCHASE → RETENTION → ADVOCACY
    ↓             ↓            ↓           ↓           ↓
 Instagram    Browse site    Checkout     ???         ???
 (only ch?)   No reviews     Basic UX     No repurchase No referral
               No social      Friction     triggers    program
               proof                        visible
```

### Critical Break Points

**1. CONSIDERATION → PURCHASE (High Drop-off Expected)**
- No visible customer reviews on products
- No size guides or fit recommendations
- No "bought together" or bundle suggestions
- Cart abandonment recovery absent (no email capture incentive)

**2. PURCHASE → RETENTION (Complete Breakdown)**
- No loyalty program visible
- No subscription option (surprising for recurring-wear category)
- No reactivation email sequence
- No SMS capture

**3. RETENTION → ADVOCACY (Not Built)**
- No referral program
- No UGC incentives
- No VIP/early access tier

**Biggest Drop-off Point:** Post-purchase retention. They acquire, ship once, and disappear.

### What They SHOULD Do (But Aren't)

#### Immediate Additions
| Opportunity | Revenue Potential | Timeline | Why It Works |
|-------------|-------------------|----------|--------------|

| **Bundle Builder** | +25% AOV | 14 days | Force higher cart values at checkout |

#### Medium-Term Expansion
| Opportunity | Description |
|-----------|-------------|

| **B2B Corporate Uniform** | Custom branded streetwear for startups/tech |
| **Collaborative Capsules** | 2x/year artist/designer partnerships |

### Product Strategy Gap
**Current:** 40 permanent SKUs with year-round availability  
**Optimal:** Core basics (20%) + rotating drops (80%)  

Streetwear demands scarcity. Constant availability commoditizes the brand.
