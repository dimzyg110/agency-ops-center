01 - We Get It

The Brand Is Real. The Infrastructure Isn't.

We audited everything - website, social, email, SEO, revenue infra. The full picture:

The brand DNA is undeniable. "Decoded Noir." "Hand of God." "Kingdom Has Fallen." The automotive × faith × luxury aesthetic. The two-tier pricing. The 911 Turbo visual language. You can't manufacture this. It's the one thing money can't buy.

But the infrastructure is killing it. No custom domain. Zero product descriptions. $0 from email/SMS. No retention system whatsoever. Every customer who buys disappears forever - no follow-up, no community, no reason to come back. You're acquiring customers at full cost, selling them one item, and never speaking to them again.

The gap isn't identity - it's the system around it. That's what we build.

02 - The Vision

A Digital Speakeasy

Most agencies would fix the Shopify store, run some Meta ads, and call it a day. That's not what we're proposing.

We're proposing Decoded stop competing as a clothing brand and start operating as a digital speakeasy - an experience-first world where the app isn't a store, it's the front door to a culture. The clothes aren't products. They're trophies you earn by being inside.

Think MSCHF - they built a $200M+ brand because every time you open the app, something different happens. You never know what you'll find. That creates compulsive engagement. People open the app not to buy, but to see what's happening. The buying follows naturally. The difference? MSCHF had to invent reasons for people to open the app. You already have them - your drop cadence is the engine.
