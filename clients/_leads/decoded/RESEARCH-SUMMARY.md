# DECODED SHOP — RESEARCH SUMMARY
## Phase 2 Complete | February 9, 2026

## 🎯 THE PICTURE

**Decoded Shop** is a Texas-based (806/Panhandle) luxury streetwear brand with strong brand DNA but severely underbuilt digital infrastructure. They have the raw ingredients — provocative naming, a compelling "Decoded Noir" luxury sub-line, automotive/faith aesthetics, and a real community via "806 The Show" podcast. What they don't have is any of the systems needed to convert that into revenue.

## 🔑 TOP FINDINGS (Cross-Agent Consensus)

### What's Working
1. **Brand voice is genuine** — "Hand of God," "Homeless Rich," "Decoded Noir" naming is culture-forward and memorable
2. **Two-tier pricing architecture** — Decoded ($25-$70) and Decoded Noir ($120-$150) creates aspiration
3. **Real community** — 20.4K IG followers, 806 The Show podcast, TNG Brands ecosystem
4. **Automotive × luxury × faith** aesthetic is unique — no competitor owns this

### What's Broken (Every Agent Flagged These)
1. **No custom domain** — still on `decodedshop.myshopify.com` (old domain `shopdecode.us` is dead)
2. **Zero product descriptions** on ALL 37-40 products
3. **No email/SMS capture** anywhere — no retention possible
4. **No Google Business Profile** — invisible in local search
5. **Single-platform brand** — Instagram only, no TikTok (fatal for streetwear in 2026)
6. **No reviews, no about page, no brand story, no size guide** on the website
7. **Post-purchase retention is completely broken** — acquire once, disappear

### White Space Identified
- **"Elevated Texas Streetwear"** — nobody owns this positioning
- **806/Panhandle geography** — untapped identity in streetwear
- **Automotive × luxury streetwear** — the 911 Turbo aesthetic is a goldmine
- **Women's luxury streetwear in Texas** — empty category

## 🏆 COMPETITIVE POSITION (Opus 4.6T)

**Direct Competitors:** Heights District, Made in the South, Faded Decade, Southside, Urban Stylez, The South's Apparel — all similar scale, most with weaker brand identity

**Aspirational Competitors:** DIOS, Madhappy, Hellstar, A Ma Manière, Homme Femme (149K), MENACE (131K), Who Decides War (246K) — show the ceiling with proper marketing

**Decoded's Position:** Mid-price ($25-$150), mid-sophistication — the most crowded and dangerous part of the market. Needs to move UP or OUT.

---

## 📱 MOBILE APP ASSESSMENT (Opus 4.6T — earlier run)

**Recommendation:** Vajro ($150/mo) over custom build ($100K+)
- Live on both app stores in 2-3 weeks
- Push notifications for drops = 10-15× higher conversion than email
- Year 1 total cost: ~$2K vs $100K+ custom
- **BUT: website fundamentals must be fixed first** (every agent agrees)

---

## 🎯 TOP OPPORTUNITIES (Ranked by Impact × Likelihood ÷ Effort)

1. **Email/SMS + Website Fix** — highest ROI, lowest effort, proves value fast
2. **Custom domain + product descriptions** — immediate credibility boost, near-zero cost
3. **TikTok launch** — cinematic Noir aesthetic is made for short-form video
4. **Limited drops model** — shift from 40 permanent SKUs to scarcity-driven drops
5. **Paid Meta + retargeting** — immediate revenue acceleration once site converts
6. **"Noir VIP" membership** — transforms business model from commodity to community
7. **Mobile app** (Vajro) — after website is fixed, unlocks push notifications for drops

---

## ⚠️ EVERY AGENT AGREES ON THIS

**Build the brand before building the app.** The mobile app request is premature — the website can't even convert basic traffic right now. Fix the foundation (domain, descriptions, email capture, brand story), THEN layer on growth tools.
