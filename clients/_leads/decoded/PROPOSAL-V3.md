# THE VISION: THE DIGITAL SPEAKEASY

Most brands would fix the Shopify store, run some Meta ads, and call it a day.

We're not proposing that.

We're proposing that Decoded stop competing as a clothing brand and start operating as a **digital speakeasy**: an invite-only, experience-first world where the app isn't a store—it's the front door to a culture. The clothes aren't products. They're trophies you earn by being inside.

**The model:** MSCHF built a $200M+ brand on one principle: every time you open the app, something different happens. You never know what you'll find. That creates compulsive engagement. People open the app not to buy, but to *see what's happening.* The buying follows naturally.

Decoded's frequent drops are the perfect fuel for this. Most brands struggle to create enough reasons for people to open their app. Decoded's owner drops constantly, and that's the engine.

**What this looks like:**
- A cinematic boot sequence that feels like starting a 911 Turbo
- Real games hidden inside the app—not loyalty points, actual arcade games
- 15-minute "Flash Raid" drop windows that appear without warning
- Podcast episodes that contain verbal codes unlocking hidden app content
- "The Vault Lock," where unsold inventory gets permanently pulled and vaulted—never sold again
- A tiered community where the deepest levels are earned, not bought

**Nobody at Decoded's level has this. That's the point.**

# KEY FEATURES

## The Boot Sequence

When someone opens the app for the first time, they don't see a login form. They see a movie.

- **THE IGNITION:** A 911 Turbo tachometer fills the screen. The needle climbs from 0 to redline. Engine audio fades in, low, throbbing. The Decoded logo burns into frame, letter by letter.

  *"This isn't a store."*

- **THE WORLD:** Split-screen montage—Porsche in motion / Noir fabric close-up / hands in prayer / city night aerial. Quick cuts. Cinematic.

  *"This is a world. You're either in it, or you're watching."*

- **THE GATE:** Dark screen. A fingerprint scanner pulses. The user holds it until the vault unlocks. Then: "Choose Your Lane."

  | **DECODED** | **DECODED NOIR** |
  |-------------|-----------------|
  | Main line. $25–$70. Streetwear with edge. | Luxury tier. $120–$150. Elevated. Exclusive. |

- **ENTER THE CODE:** Dark input field. Blinking cursor. *"Enter your code name."* Push notification permission triggers after entry.

---

## The Vault — Hidden Games

- **Midnight Run:** 2D top-down racer. 911 Turbo sprite dodging traffic on a dark highway. Score 5,000+ → "Check Engine" light triggers access to a hidden product collection.

- **The Confessional:** Noir puzzle game. Questions drawn from "806 The Show" podcast lore and biblical references in the designs. Correct answers → 1-hour access code to a flash discount.

- **Global Leaderboard:** "Driver's License" — shareable scorecard graphic with user handle + score, designed for IG Stories.

---

## The Drop Zone

- **Standard Drops:** 72-hour → 1-hour → live push sequence. 10-minute cart timer. Real-time stock meter.

- **Flash Raid:** No warning. Push notification: *"THE WINDOW IS OPEN. 15 MINUTES."* The button detonates after time expires. Miss it, it's gone.

- **Versus Drops:** Two colorways drop simultaneously. Users vote. Loser gets locked forever. Winner gets sold. Creates tribalism. Generates organic debate.

- **Vault Lock:** Unsold inventory after 48 hours gets permanently vaulted. Never sold again. Every drop feels urgent because the alternative isn't a sale—it's permanent removal.

---

## Podcast Integration

"806 The Show" becomes an engagement engine:

- **Verbal codes:** During episodes, the host drops a code (e.g., *"Romans 12:2"*). Entering it in the app triggers an easter egg—hidden discount or exclusive product.

- **Cross-platform loop:** Podcast feeds app downloads. App feeds podcast listens. Both feed purchases.
