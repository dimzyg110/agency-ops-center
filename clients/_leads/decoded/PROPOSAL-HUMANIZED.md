# 2. The vision: Digital speakeasy

Most brands fix Shopify, run Meta ads, and call it innovation. That is not what we're doing.

Decoded stops being a clothing brand. It becomes a **digital speakeasy** — invite-only, experience-first, where the app is the front door to a culture and clothes are trophies you earn by being inside.

**How MSCHF built $200M:** Every time you open the app, something's different. You don't know what you'll find. That creates compulsive engagement. People open to *see what's happening*. The buying follows.

Decoded's constant drops? That's the fuel. Most brands struggle to create reasons to open. Your owner drops more than he should. That's the engine.

**What it looks like:**

- Boot sequence that feels like starting a 911 Turbo
- Real games hidden inside — Space Invaders, not loyalty points
- 15-minute "Flash Raid" drops that appear without warning
- Podcast codes that unlock hidden app content
- "The Vault" — unsold inventory gets pulled forever, never discounted, just gone
- Quarterly boxes with NFC artifacts that unlock experiences
- Tiers earned, not bought

The website is Balenciaga-clean, dark, minimal — just a front window to drive traffic to the app. The app is where the world lives.

Nobody at Decoded's level has this. That's the whole point.
