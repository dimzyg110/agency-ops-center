## EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

Decoded Shop doesn't need a better Shopify store. There are 10,000 streetwear brands with Shopify stores. What Decoded needs is its own world — a cinematic, gamified mobile app that turns every drop into an event, every visit into an experience, and every customer into a member of something they can't get anywhere else. The frequent drop cadence that most brands would call "too much" is actually the perfect fuel for an app-first strategy: every new product becomes a reason to open the app, play the game, and discover something hidden. Right now, Decoded has a $100K–$500K business with zero retention infrastructure — customers buy once and vanish. The app changes that equation entirely. It becomes the brand's front door, loyalty program, and content platform in one.

### PHASE 2: The App — The Main Event (Days 15–60)

This is what makes Decoded different from every other streetwear brand. Not a shopping app. Not a mobile-optimized website in a wrapper. A world.

#### 2.1 — App Philosophy

> **"Every time you open the app, something different happens."**

The model isn't "mobile commerce." The model is **MSCHF meets streetwear.** MSCHF built a $200M+ brand on one principle: every visit is a new experience. You never know what you'll find. You open the app and something surprises you. That creates compulsive engagement — people open the app not to buy, but to see what's happening. The buying follows naturally.

Decoded's frequent drops are the PERFECT fuel for this. Most brands struggle to create enough reasons for people to open their app. Decoded's owner drops constantly — that's not a weakness, it's the app's engine.

**Core principles:**
- **Cinematic, not clinical.** Every screen should feel like a movie, not a storefront.
- **Gamified, not gimmicky.** Real games. Real rewards. Hidden mechanics that reward exploration.
- **Surprise-first.** The home screen changes. Hidden content appears and disappears. Easter eggs reward the obsessive fans.
- **Community-driven.** The app isn't just for buying — it's for belonging.

#### 2.3 — Cinematic Onboarding Flow

When someone opens the app for the first time, they don't see a login screen. They see a movie.

**Screen 1 — "THE CODE"**
- Full-bleed dark screen. Engine sound fades in. The Decoded logo emerges from darkness, letter by letter.
- Text: *"This isn't a store."*
- Auto-advance after 3 seconds.

**Screen 2 — "THE WORLD"**
- Split-screen montage: Porsche 911 Turbo in motion / Noir Strata fabric close-up / hands in prayer (faith element) / city night aerial.
- Text: *"This is a world. You're either in it, or you're watching."*
- Auto-advance after 4 seconds.

**Screen 3 — "THE ENTRY"**
- Dark input field with blinking cursor. Label: *"Enter your code name."*
- User enters their name or handle. Email capture below.
- CTA button: *"DECODE"*
- Push notification permission prompt triggers after entry.

**Why this matters:** The first 10 seconds determine if someone keeps the app or deletes it. This onboarding makes them feel like they're entering something exclusive. It's not "create an account" — it's "join the world."

**Reference:** This is exactly the philosophy behind the AC Blacksite app — cinematic, intentional, premium. Every screen earns the next tap.

#### 2.4 — Gamification Strategy — Real Games, Not Points

This isn't "earn 10 points per purchase." This is actual gameplay embedded in the app that rewards attention and loyalty.

**THE VAULT — Hidden Mini-Game**

- **Discovery:** Users find The Vault by tapping a hidden element on the home screen (the Decoded logo pulses subtly every 30 seconds — observant users tap it and discover the entrance).
- **Game concept:** A driving-themed arcade game. Think: your car (a 911 Turbo silhouette) dodging obstacles on a dark highway. Score-based. 60-second runs.
- **Rewards:**
  - Score 500+ → Unlock 10% discount code (one-time use)
  - Score 1,000+ → Unlock "Vault Member" badge on profile
  - Score 2,500+ → Unlock early access to next drop (24 hours before public)
  - Score 5,000+ → Unlock an app-exclusive colorway (only available through The Vault)
  - Monthly top scorer → Free Noir piece (announced via push notification to all users)
- **Social element:** Global leaderboard. Users see their ranking. Top 10 get a "Decoded Elite" badge.
- **Rotation:** The game changes quarterly. Season 1: Highway Run. Season 2: Something new. Keeps users guessing.

**SURPRISE DROPS — Hidden Content Mechanics**

- **Random reward screens:** 1 in every 20 app opens triggers a "glitch" screen — dark screen, distorted text, then a flash reveals a hidden discount code or exclusive product link. Disappears after 10 seconds. Screenshot it or lose it.
- **Hidden product pages:** Certain products are ONLY discoverable through easter eggs in the app. They don't appear in the main shop. Users who explore find them.
- **"Decoded Radio":** A hidden audio player (accessible through a specific gesture on the profile screen) that plays curated playlists. It's not a music app — it's atmosphere. It keeps the app open longer and creates emotional association.

#### 2.5 — Drop Mechanics Inside the App

The owner drops frequently. Instead of that being chaotic, the app turns every drop into a structured event.

**Pre-Drop (72 hours before):**
- Push notification: *"Something's coming. 72 hours."* — with a blurred product teaser image.
- Home screen hero changes to a countdown timer with teaser imagery.
- Noir Lounge members get an unblurred preview (VIP early look, not early buy).

**Drop Day (T-minus 1 hour):**
- Push notification: *"1 hour. Are you ready?"*
- App home screen enters "Drop Mode" — dark theme intensifies, ambient sound shifts, countdown dominates.
- Live "viewer count" — shows how many people are in the app waiting (social pressure).

**Drop Live (T-0):**
- Push notification: *"IT'S LIVE. GO."*
- Product pages unlock. Sizes and quantities visible (real scarcity, real-time).
- Cart timer: 10 minutes to checkout once added to cart (creates urgency without being scammy).

**Post-Drop (24 hours after):**
- Push notification to non-buyers: *"You missed it. [X] pieces left."* (if stock remains)
- Push notification to buyers: *"Your drop shipped. Welcome to the code."*
- Sold-out notification to all: *"Gone. The next one's in [X] days."*

**App-Exclusive Drops:**
- 1–2 drops per quarter are ONLY available in the app. Not on the website. Not on Instagram. This is the #1 driver for app installs. When people see "APP ONLY" on Instagram, they download.

**DECODED MEMBERSHIP (Free — all app users)**

**NOIR MEMBERSHIP ($15/month or automatic with $300+ lifetime spend)**

- Noir-only drops (exclusive colorways and products)
- Free standard shipping on all orders
- Noir member badge in-app
- Access to founder Q&A events (quarterly, in-app)
- Birthday drop: Personalized discount or exclusive item

**DECODED ELITE (Top 1% — earned, not bought)**
- Top game scorers + highest lifetime spend
- Invitation to design input (vote on next colorway, name the next drop)
- Exclusive "Elite" product line (ultra-limited, 10–20 units, highest quality)
- Direct line to the founder (group chat or voice memo channel)
- Name listed in app credits (yes, like a movie)

#### 2.7 — Shopify Integration

The app doesn't replace Shopify — it wraps around it.

- **Product catalog:** Syncs directly from Shopify via Storefront API. Products added in Shopify appear in the app automatically.
- **Checkout:** Uses Shopify's checkout (web view or SDK). No need to rebuild payment processing.
- **Inventory:** Real-time inventory sync. When a size sells out in the app, it updates on the website and vice versa.
- **Order management:** All orders flow through Shopify's dashboard. The owner manages everything in one place.
- **Analytics:** App-specific events (game plays, screen views, Vault entries, push notification opens) tracked separately and reported to the owner via a simple dashboard.

**Tech stack:**
- **Frontend:** React Native (cross-platform — iOS + Android from one codebase)
- **Backend:** Shopify Storefront API + lightweight Node.js server for game logic, push notifications, and membership management
- **Push notifications:** Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM) for Android, APNs for iOS
- **Database:** Firebase/Supabase for user profiles, game scores, membership tiers
- **Hosting:** Vercel or Railway for the backend

### PHASE 4: Long-Term Vision (90+ Days)

#### 4.1 — App as the Primary Sales Channel

By month 6, the goal is:
- **60% of revenue through the app** (vs. website)
- **Website becomes the "front window"** — it exists for SEO and for people who Google the brand, but the real experience is in the app
- **Instagram becomes the megaphone** — it drives awareness, the app drives conversion and retention

This is the MSCHF model: the website is a landing page. The app is the business.

#### 4.2 — Expanding Game Mechanics

**Season 2 Vault Game (Quarter 2):**
- New game mechanic entirely. Maybe a rhythm game (tap to the beat of a custom Decoded soundtrack). Maybe a puzzle game (decode encrypted messages to unlock products). The surprise is the point.

**Community Challenges:**
- "Decoded Days" — 24-hour challenges where the entire app community works toward a collective goal. Example: "If 500 people play The Vault today, everyone gets free shipping on the next drop." Creates shared experience.

**AR Features (Month 6+):**
- Virtual try-on using the phone camera. See how the 911 Turbo tee looks on you before buying. This is increasingly standard in fashion apps and significantly reduces returns.

#### 4.3 — Data-Driven Drop Strategy

The app generates data the website never could:

| Data Point | Strategic Use |
|-----------|---------------|
| **Push notification open rates by product category** | Know which drop types generate the most excitement BEFORE producing |
| **Vault game engagement by time of day** | Schedule drops for peak engagement windows |
| **Noir Lounge vs. free tier purchase rates** | Quantify the revenue lift of the VIP tier |
| **Product page views that don't convert** | Identify pricing or sizing issues in real-time |
| **Referral code tracking** | Know exactly which customers are bringing in new customers |
| **App open frequency per user** | Identify at-risk users (haven't opened in 14 days) for re-engagement push |

**Goal:** Within 6 months, every drop decision is informed by data. What to drop, when to drop it, how much to produce, what price point, which tier gets early access — all backed by real user behavior.

#### 4.4 — Community Moat — The App IS the Loyalty Program

Long-term, the app becomes the thing competitors can't replicate:
- **Switching cost:** A customer has game scores, Noir membership status, earned rewards, a referral network, and a history inside the app. Moving to another brand means starting from zero.
- **Network effect:** More users = better leaderboards = more competitive = more engagement. UGC from the app creates organic marketing.
- **Content lock-in:** Behind-the-scenes content, founder Q&As, Noir exclusives — this content doesn't exist anywhere else.
- **Identity:** "I'm Decoded Elite" becomes part of how a customer sees themselves. That's a moat no competitor can buy.

## WHAT THEY SHOULD STOP DOING

Be direct? Here it is:

| Stop This | Why |
|-----------|-----|
| **Stop treating the Shopify store as the brand** | The store is infrastructure. The app is the brand. Stop pouring energy into making Shopify look slightly better — invest that energy in the app experience. |
| **Stop posting flat-lay product photos on white backgrounds** | It looks like a dropshipping store. Streetwear is about context. Shoot on concrete, on cars, on people, at night. The Noir aesthetic should bleed into every piece of content. |
| **Stop dropping without a campaign** | Every drop without a pre-launch tease, countdown, and post-drop follow-up is wasted. Drops without marketing are just restocks. |
| **Stop keeping 40 SKUs permanently available** | Constant availability kills scarcity. Move to: 20% core basics (always available) + 80% rotating drops (limited windows). Streetwear is built on "if you miss it, it's gone." |
| **Stop ignoring TikTok** | In 2026, not being on TikTok as a streetwear brand is like not having a phone number in 2005. The Noir aesthetic is MADE for short-form video. This should have been launched yesterday. |
| **Stop selling without product descriptions** | Every single product has zero description. This is costing 50%+ of potential conversions. Even basic copy (fabric, fit, inspiration) would transform the buying experience. |
| **Stop operating on a .myshopify.com domain** | This signals "hobby project" to every potential customer. A custom domain costs $15/year. There is no excuse. |
