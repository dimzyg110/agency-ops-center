# Decoded Shop Growth Plan — Roviq (App-First)

## Executive Summary
Decoded Shop doesn’t need another Shopify facelift — it needs a **brand vehicle** that creates obsession and retention. The mobile app is that vehicle: a cinematic, gamified experience that turns frequent drops into an always-on experience and makes Decoded stand out from 10,000 look‑alike streetwear brands. **Why now:** the owner drops frequently (perfect for app experiments), but there’s **zero retention infrastructure** (no email/SMS), so every sale dies at checkout. Roviq will stabilize the basics while building the app as the primary channel that powers drops, community, and repeat revenue.

---

## Current State Diagnosis (1–10)
- **Brand (7/10):** Strong naming and concept (Decoded Noir, Hand of God, Homeless Rich). Unique automotive × luxury × faith aesthetic.
- **Digital Presence (2/10):** Still on `decodedshop.myshopify.com`, zero product descriptions, no brand story, no trust signals.
- **Marketing (2/10):** Instagram only, no TikTok, no email/SMS, no paid media, no content engine.
- **Revenue Optimization (2/10):** AOV ~$68, no upsells/bundles, no retention flows.
- **Customer Experience (3/10):** No size guide, no reviews, no clear returns, one image per product.

**#1 Growth Killer:** **No retention or owned audience.** Every visitor who doesn’t buy immediately is lost forever. The app fixes this by making Decoded a destination, not a one‑off store.

## The Transformation Plan

### Phase 1 — Quick Wins (First 30 Days)
*Goal: stop the bleeding while the app is built.*

**Website / credibility basics (non‑negotiable):**
- **Custom domain** (decoded.shop or similar) to replace the myshopify subdomain.
- **Product descriptions for all 37–40 SKUs** (materials, fit, story, care). This alone can lift conversion 30%+.
- **Email + SMS capture** (Klaviyo): popup + footer capture + welcome flow.
- **Trust layer:** add About page, size guide, returns policy, social links, and remove “Powered by Shopify.”

**Social quick wins:**
- **TikTok launch** (missing platform = fatal gap). 3–5 short “Noir” reels weekly.
- **Cinematic content**: 911 Turbo / car culture visuals as the hook. Dark, moody, high‑contrast clips.

**Revenue moves tied to Decoded data:**
- **Bundled sets** (Noir top + bottom) to raise AOV above $68.
- **Sold‑out cleanup** (remove dead products like Noir Eclipse if not restocking).

### Phase 2 — The App (Days 15–60)
*Goal: build the brand’s primary channel and the differentiator.*

#### Technical Architecture
- **React Native** app (fast multi‑platform release).
- **Shopify integration** via Storefront API for products, inventory, checkout.
- **Push notifications** via Firebase (critical for drop conversion).
- **Auth + VIP gating** for app‑only content.

#### App Feature Spec (Specific Screens & Flows)
1. **Cinematic Onboarding**
   - 3–5 screen immersive intro (Noir aesthetic).
   - “Choose your lane”: Decoded / Decoded Noir.
   - Audio + motion (subtle engine rev / synth wave).

2. **Home Screen (Experience Layer)**
   - Hero carousel for active drop.
   - “Hidden Content” tiles (locked content reveals after interactions).
   - Featured drop timer + “Unlock early” CTA.

3. **Drop Room (Core Commerce)**
   - Drop countdown, launch timer, exclusives.
   - **App‑only drop variants** (colorways or numbered runs).
   - Stock progress meter to emphasize scarcity.

4. **Game Layer (Real Games, Not Points)**
   - Hidden **Space‑Invaders‑style game** (unlock codes or early access).
   - “Turbo Run” mini‑game: collect parts to earn early access or free shipping.
   - Weekly mystery game drops with Easter eggs.

5. **Decoded Noir Vault (VIP)**
   - Members‑only access to Noir drops.
   - “Founder Notes” video drops.
   - Early access windows (24–48 hours).

6. **Community Layer**
   - “Noir Signals” feed: curated posts + podcast highlights from 806 The Show.
   - User‑generated photo wall (app‑only).

7. **Account / Profile**
   - Drop history, purchase history, earned unlocks.
   - “Decoded Rank” based on engagement.

#### Drop Mechanics (Built for frequent drops)
- **App‑first drop announcements** (push → countdown → live drop).
- **Unlock codes** hidden in the app/game or podcast episodes.
- **Limited colorway exclusives** only available in app.

#### Push Notification Strategy
- Drop countdowns (T‑24h, T‑1h, T‑5m).
- “Vault Open” alerts for Noir VIP.
- Surprise “Easter Egg” releases (MSCHF‑style).

#### MSCHF‑Inspired Experience (Translated to Decoded)
- Every interaction feels like **secret access**.
- Surprise mechanics (random screen reveals, unlockable hidden products).
- Drop culture as a game, not a schedule.

### Phase 3 — Launch & Growth (Days 60–90)
*Goal: shift demand into the app.*

**App Launch Campaign**

**Instagram → App Funnel**

**Paid Acquisition (lean + targeted):**
- Meta + TikTok retargeting on site visitors + IG engagers.
- Creative angle: automotive + noir cinematic short‑form.
- Budget: start $1–2K/month to validate CAC.

### Phase 4 — Long‑Term (90+ Days)
*Goal: app becomes the primary channel and community moat.*

- **App = primary channel** for drops, loyalty, and community.
- **Data‑driven drops** using app engagement data (what games, what products, what time).
- Expand game mechanics (seasonal mini‑games, scavenger hunts tied to IRL events).
- **Community moat**: VIP tier + early access + exclusive colorways + in‑app community.
