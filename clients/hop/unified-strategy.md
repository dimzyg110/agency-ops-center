## 3) Visual Identity Guidelines

### Color Palette (Core)
- **Black** `#000000` — primary background
- **Gold** `#e7cc95` — accents, highlights, premium cues
- **White** `#FCFCFC` — text and contrast
- **Tan** `#DFD5C5` — secondary accents

### Typography Recommendations
- **Primary:** Open Sans (clean, modern, readable)
- **Secondary:** Poppins (luxury-modern, strong headers)
- **Optional Display:** Cinzel or Playfair Display for editorial luxury headlines

### Logo Usage
- Use high contrast (gold on black or white on black).
- Maintain clear space equal to 1x the logomark height.
- Never stretch, rotate, or apply drop shadows.
- Approved versions: gold on white, white on black, black on white.

### Photography Style
- **Mood:** Editorial, high-contrast, cinematic.
- **Subjects:** Product hero shots, founder-led storytelling, community events.
- **Lighting:** Soft directional, moody shadows, warm highlights.
- **Composition:** Minimalist, luxury fashion framing, negative space.

### NEW: AI Product Photography Approach

**Why AI Photography:**
Product photos are being taken THIS WEEK. AI generation provides proven quality, fast turnaround, and consistent aesthetic - critical for our accelerated timeline.

**The Workflow:**
1. **Input:** High-quality product samples (actual merch items)
2. **AI Generation:** Create lifestyle scenes, flat lays, on-model shots using AI tools
3. **Refinement:** Manual touch-up for perfection (remove artifacts, enhance details)
4. **Output:** Web-ready, social-optimized assets in multiple formats

**Use Cases:**
| Need | AI Solution |
|------|-------------|
| Shop listings | Clean product shots on branded backgrounds |
| Social content | Lifestyle scenes (merch in use, styled environments) |
| Ad creative | Multiple variants for A/B testing |
| Seasonal refreshes | Same products, new backgrounds/themes |

**Benefits:**
- **Speed:** 10x faster than traditional photoshoot (critical for Week 1 launch)
- **Consistency:** Same lighting, angles, aesthetic across all products
- **Iteration:** Easy to change backgrounds, seasons, contexts without reshoot
- **Cost:** Fraction of traditional photography for startup phase

**Quality Assurance:**
- Always start with high-quality physical product samples
- Human review and touch-up on all outputs
- Test print key images before bulk use
- Maintain "AI-assisted" not "AI-only" philosophy

## 12) Compliance: Cannabis Regulatory Requirements

### Overview
Operating in the California cannabis market requires strict adherence to state regulations governed by the Department of Cannabis Control (DCC, formerly BCC). Non-compliance can result in fines, product seizures, or license suspension.

### State-Specific Requirements (California)

**1. Packaging & Labeling (BCC/DCC Standards)**
- **Universal Symbol:** Mandatory cannabis symbol on primary panel
- **THC Warning:** "GOVERNMENT WARNING" statement required
- **Content Disclosure:** THC/CBD content, batch number, manufacturing date
- **Child-Resistant:** All cannabis products must be in child-resistant packaging
- **Tamper-Evident:** Seals required for consumer protection

**2. Metrc Track-and-Trace**
- **What:** California's mandatory seed-to-sale tracking system
- **Requirements:** Every plant and product batch must be tagged and logged
- **HOP Impact:** 
  - All pre-roll batches need Metrc tags
  - Sales to dispensaries require Metrc manifests
  - Inventory reconciliation required every 30 days
- **Cost:** ~$40/month per license + tag costs (~$0.45/tag)

**3. Testing Requirements**
- All cannabis products must pass state-mandated lab testing:
  - Potency (THC, CBD, other cannabinoids)
  - Pesticides, residual solvents, heavy metals
  - Microbial contaminants, moisture content
- **HOP Action:** Ensure all batches have Certificates of Analysis (COAs) before distribution

**4. Distribution Licensing**
- HOP must either hold a distribution license or partner with a licensed distributor
- Transport requires METRC manifests and licensed transport vehicles
- **Recommendation:** Partner with established distributor for logistics; focus brand resources on marketing/sales

### Compliance Timeline Integration

| Task | Lead Time | Owner | Status |
|------|-----------|-------|--------|
| Packaging compliance review | 2 weeks | Compliance consultant / designer | Pending |
| Metrc onboarding | 1-2 weeks | Operations / distributor partner | Pending |
| Lab testing protocols | Ongoing | Cultivation / distributor | Established |
| Label approval (internal) | 1 week | Design / legal | Pending |

### Recommended Actions
1. **Hire compliance consultant** for packaging review ($500-1,500 one-time)
2. **Confirm distributor partner** handles Metrc logistics
3. **Build buffer time** (2-4 weeks) for any packaging revisions required
4. **Document all SOPs** for future retail expansion to other states
