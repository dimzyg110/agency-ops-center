## NEW: Existing Order Transition Plan

### Current Reality
Existing orders need to transition to new labels + temporary v1 packaging while maintaining customer satisfaction.

### Transition Strategy

| Phase | Timeline | Action |
|-------|----------|--------|
| **Immediate** | Week 1 Day 1-2 | Identify all open/pending orders in system |
| **Inventory Sort** | Week 1 Day 2-3 | Separate inventory: old labels vs. new labels |
| **Hybrid Fulfillment** | Week 1-2 | Existing orders: Ship with OLD labels if already packaged; NEW labels for unfulfilled orders |
| **Communication** | Week 1 | Email to customers: "Exciting packaging upgrade coming" |
| **Complete Switch** | Week 2 | All new orders use new labels exclusively; remaining SKUs labeled |

### Temporary v1 Packaging Specs
- **Materials:** Use existing boxes/bags with new label applied over old (if needed)
- **Quality Check:** Ensure new labels adhere properly, no peeling
- **Documentation:** Photo log of transition batches for quality tracking
- **Customer Experience:** Include small card: "You're getting our new premium labels first"

### Risk Mitigation
| Risk | Mitigation |
|------|------------|
| Mixed inventory confusion | Color-code storage bins: GREEN = new labels, YELLOW = transition, RED = old stock |
| Customer confusion | Proactive email: "Packaging refresh in progress - same great product, sharper look" |
| Label adhesion issues | Test print 10 labels, apply to sample products, 24hr adhesion test before batch |

### AI Product Photography Approach
**Why AI:** Already proven quality, fast turnaround, consistent aesthetic

**Workflow:**
1. **Input:** High-quality product samples (actual merch items)
2. **AI Generation:** Create lifestyle scenes, flat lays, on-model shots

4. **Output:** Web-ready, social-optimized assets

**Benefits:**
- 10x faster than traditional photoshoot
- Consistent luxury aesthetic across all products
- Easy to iterate (new backgrounds, seasons, themes)
- Cost-effective for lean startup phase
