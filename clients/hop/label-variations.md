## Gold Band Typography Options

### Keep Current Layout (Recommended)
- Clean, elegant, works well
- Consider: Slightly larger logo, bolder "HOUSE OF PRIDE" font weight

### Variation 1: Centered Stack
```
[HOP Logo]
HOUSE OF PRIDE
```
- Logo above text, both centered
- More vertical, regal

### Variation 2: Left-Aligned Signature
```
[HOP Logo] HOUSE OF PRIDE
           Est. 2026
```
- Add establishment year for authenticity
- Left-aligned feels more editorial

### Variation 3: Minimal
```
HOP
```
- Just the monogram, no text
- Ultra-luxury (Chanel-style confidence)
- Let the logo speak for itself

### Variation 4: Split Text
```
HOUSE          OF          PRIDE
        [HOP Logo]
```
- Text spread across with logo centered below
- More dramatic, runway-inspired

## Typography Recommendations for Gold Band

**Font Pairing:**
- Primary: Cinzel or Trajan Pro (elegant serifs)
- Alternative: Bodoni or Didot (high contrast luxury)

**Sizing:**
- "HOUSE OF PRIDE": 18-24pt
- Logo: 40-50px height
- Maintain generous spacing (tracking +50)

**Color:**
- Keep gold (#D4AF37 or similar)
- Consider subtle gradient (lighter at center, darker at edges) for depth
