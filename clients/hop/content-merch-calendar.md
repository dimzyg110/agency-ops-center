## Merch Content Integration

### Photography Guidelines

#### Flat Lay Photography
**Use for:** Product detail shots, website listings, email hero images

**Setup:**
- Clean background (white, concrete, or wood texture)
- Natural lighting or softbox
- Props: Cannabis accessories (grinders, papers, minimal), plants, coffee
- 45-degree overhead angle
- Consistent brand color palette

**Shot List per Drop:**
- 1 hero flat lay (all pieces)
- 1 detail shot per product (logo close-up)
- 1 styled flat lay (lifestyle props)

#### Lifestyle Photography
**Use for:** Social posts, ads, lookbook

**Setup:**
- Real locations (home, park, studio)
- Natural poses, candid moments
- Show product in use/worn
- Golden hour lighting preferred

**Shot List per Drop:**
- 3-5 lifestyle shots per piece
- Group shots (if multiple pieces)
- Candid "in action" moments

#### On-Model Photography
**Use for:** Size reference, fit guidance, premium positioning

**Setup:**
- Diverse models (sizes, genders, ethnicities)
- Clean backdrop or lifestyle setting
- Front, side, back angles
- Detail shots (fit, drape, texture)

**Models:**
- Friends and community first (cost-effective)
- Local micro-influencers (trade for product)
- Professional for premium line (Q2)
