## 1. Executive Summary

Decoded is an emerging streetwear / luxury-adjacent fashion brand operating on Shopify with a catalog of **40 products** spanning graphic tees ($25–$65), premium hoodies/sweatshirts ($70–$125), and the higher-end **Decoded Noir** collection ($120–$150). The brand's positioning — *"unapologetic, precision-crafted luxury for those who move different"* — places it in the aspirational streetwear category alongside brands like Represent, Corteiz, and Fear of God Essentials.

**Current State Assessment:**
- **Catalog depth:** 40 SKUs, all in stock. No sold-out scarcity yet, suggesting room for drop model adoption.
- **Price range:** $25–$150 (AOV estimate: ~$70–$85 based on product mix).
- **Store infrastructure:** Standard Shopify theme (Dawn variant), minimal navigation (Home + Contact only), no blog, no email capture beyond basic footer subscribe, no reviews, no loyalty program.
- **Brand presence:** Instagram @decoded.shop, Pinterest /decodedshop (21 pins, minimal activity). No custom domain — still on myshopify.com subdomain. No blog/SEO content.
- **Collections:** Two identifiable collections — general Decoded line and Decoded Noir (luxury tier).

**The Opportunity:** Decoded has strong product and brand DNA but near-zero marketing infrastructure. This means almost every lever we pull will generate outsized returns relative to effort. The brand is at the **"zero to one" inflection point** where foundational work compounds rapidly.

**12-Month Revenue Target:** $180,000–$250,000 in gross revenue (from estimated current baseline of <$2,000/month to $15,000–$21,000/month by Month 12).

### 4.1 Email & SMS Marketing (Highest Priority)

**Platform Recommendation:** Klaviyo (best-in-class for Shopify fashion brands)

**Core Flows to Build (in order):**

1. **Welcome Series** (3–5 emails)
   - Email 1: Brand story + 10% first-order discount (immediate)
   - Email 2: Founder story & brand values (Day 2)
   - Email 3: Best-sellers showcase (Day 4)
   - Email 4: UGC/social proof (Day 6)
   - Email 5: Urgency — discount expires (Day 7)
   - *Expected: 40–50% open rate, 8–12% click rate*

2. **Abandoned Cart** (3-email sequence)
   - Email 1: Reminder with product image (1 hour)
   - Email 2: Social proof + scarcity (24 hours)
   - Email 3: Discount incentive (48 hours)
   - *Expected: 45% open rate, 5–10% recovery rate*

3. **Post-Purchase** (3–4 emails)
   - Thank you + brand story
   - Styling tips / how to wear
   - Review request (Day 7)
   - Cross-sell recommendation (Day 14)

4. **Browse Abandonment** (2 emails)
5. **Win-Back** (3 emails at 60/90/120 days)
6. **VIP Segment** (top 20% customers — early access to drops)

**SMS Strategy:**
- Collect phone numbers via popup (offer additional 5% for SMS opt-in)
- Use SMS for: flash sales, drop alerts, restock notifications, shipping updates
- Target: SMS click rate 8–12%, RPR $0.40–$0.80
- *Top 10% apparel brands see SMS RPR of $0.80 per Klaviyo benchmarks*

**Email Campaign Calendar:**
- 2–3 campaigns per week minimum
- Segmented by: purchase history, browse behavior, engagement level
- *Segmented campaigns average 29% open rates vs. 18–20% for unsegmented (Klaviyo 2025)*

**Targets:**

| Email list size | 500 | 2,000 | 5,000+ |
| SMS subscribers | 100 | 500 | 1,500 |
| Email revenue % | 10% | 20% | 25–30% |

### 4.3 Paid Acquisition

#### Meta Ads (Instagram & Facebook)

**Phase 1 (Months 1–3): Foundation**
- Campaign structure: Advantage+ Shopping Campaigns (ASC)
- Creative types: UGC-style video ads, carousel (outfit builds), single product with lifestyle context
- Targeting: Broad (let Meta's algorithm optimize) + lookalike from email list (once built)
- Budget: $30–$50/day
- Target ROAS: 2.5–3x (acquisition mode)

**Phase 2 (Months 4–6): Scaling**
- Introduce retargeting (site visitors, IG engagers, email openers)
- Dynamic Product Ads (DPA)
- Scale winning creatives
- Budget: $75–$100/day
- Target ROAS: 3.5–4.5x

**Phase 3 (Months 7–12): Optimization**
- Advanced segmentation
- Catalog-based campaigns
- Seasonal pushes (holiday, back-to-school)
- Budget: $100–$150/day
- Target ROAS: 4–6x

**Creative Strategy:**
- Minimum 3–5 new ad creatives per week
- A/B test: UGC vs. studio, short-form vs. carousel, benefit-driven vs. lifestyle
- Key angles: "luxury streetwear under $150," provocative product names, car culture tie-in

#### Google Shopping / Performance Max

**Timeline:** Start Month 2 (after product feed is optimized)

- Optimize product titles/descriptions for search intent
- High-quality product images (white background + lifestyle)
- Start with Performance Max campaigns
- Budget: $20–$30/day initially, scale to $50–$75/day by Month 6
- Target ROAS: 4–6x (Google Shopping typically outperforms Meta on ROAS for apparel)
- Priority products: Decoded Noir collection (higher margin, higher AOV)

#### TikTok Ads

**Timeline:** Start Month 3 (after organic TikTok presence is established)

- Spark Ads (boosting top-performing organic content)
- UGC-style creator ads
- Budget: $20–$30/day, scale based on performance
- Target ROAS: 2–3x initially (TikTok is more top-of-funnel)
- Best for: new customer acquisition, brand awareness, viral potential

### 4.6 Retention, Loyalty & Referral Programs

- **100 points = $5 reward**

- **Tiers:**
  - **Decoded (0–500 pts):** Base rewards
  - **Decoded Elite (500–2,000 pts):** Early access to drops, exclusive colorways

**Expected Impact:**
- Loyalty members spend 12–18% more per order
- Repeat purchase rate increase of 20–30%
- 10–15% of revenue attributable to loyalty program within 6 months

#### Referral Program

**Structure:**
- "Give $15, Get $15" — friend gets $15 off first order ($50 minimum), referrer gets $15 credit

- Track via Smile.io or ReferralCandy
- Target: 5–10% of new customers from referrals by Month 9

**Expected CAC from referrals:** ~$15–$20 (vs. $66 industry average) — highest-ROI acquisition channel

## 9. Mobile App Growth Lever

### Current State & Opportunity

If Decoded has (or plans to build) a mobile app, this becomes a significant competitive advantage. Key benchmarks from Tapcart data:

- Fashion brands with mobile apps see **67% higher conversion rates** vs. mobile web
- App-driven AOV is **19% higher** than mobile web
- Top brands attribute **13% of total U.S. revenue** to their app
- Push notifications have **3–5x higher engagement** than email

### Mobile App Strategy

**Platform Recommendation:** Tapcart (purpose-built for Shopify fashion brands, $200–$400/month)

**Phase 1 — App Launch (Month 3–4):**
- Build and publish app to iOS App Store + Google Play
- Promote app download via:
  - Post-purchase email: "Download our app for exclusive access"
  - IG/TikTok content driving to app
  - Website banner: "Shop on our app for early drop access"
- App-exclusive benefit: Early access to all Noir drops (24-hour head start)

**Phase 2 — App Growth (Month 5–8):**
- Push notification strategy:
  - New product alerts
  - Flash sale announcements (app-exclusive pricing)
  - Abandoned browse reminders
  - Restock alerts
- App-exclusive products or colorways (drives downloads)
- In-app loyalty point tracking
- Integrate with Klaviyo for cross-channel orchestration

**Phase 3 — App Optimization (Month 9–12):**
- Personalized product recommendations
- In-app live shopping events for drops
- VIP tier app experiences
- A/B test app layouts for conversion

## 10. Risk Mitigation

| Risk | Likelihood | Impact | Mitigation |
|------|-----------|--------|------------|
| Meta ad account suspension | Medium | High | Diversify ad spend across Google + TikTok; maintain organic channels |
| Rising CAC across all paid channels | High | Medium | Invest in organic (SEO, email, referral) to lower blended CAC |
| Low initial conversion rate | High | Medium | Prioritize CRO (trust signals, reviews, UX) in Month 1 |
| Influencer partnerships underperform | Medium | Low | Start with product-trade deals; only pay for proven performers |
| Inventory stockouts on viral products | Medium | High | Monitor sell-through rates; use pre-orders for drops |
| Brand dilution from discounting | Low | Medium | Keep discounts limited to welcome offer and BFCM; preserve premium positioning |
| Slow organic traffic growth | High | Low | SEO is a long game — focus on paid + email for near-term revenue |
| Customer data privacy changes | Medium | Medium | Build first-party data (email, SMS, app) to reduce platform dependency |
