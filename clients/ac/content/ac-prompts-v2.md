## The Research: What Makes Prompts Work

### VIDEO PROMPT FORMULA (Runway Gen-3 / Kling)

**Universal 4-Part Structure:**
```
[CAMERA] + [SUBJECT] + [ACTION] + [CONTEXT/STYLE]
```

**Key Principles:**
1. **Be direct, not conceptual** — Describe what you SEE, not what it means
2. **Positive phrasing only** — Say what IS, not what ISN'T
3. **Limit to 3-5 key elements** — Too many elements = confusion/failure
4. **Specify motion endpoints** — "then settles back into place" prevents glitches
5. **Camera movement is critical** — No camera direction = static/random results

**Camera Keywords That Work:**
- Low angle static shot
- Tracking shot following from side
- Slow push-in
- Crane shot rising
- FPV continuous flight
- Wide angle establishing
- Macro close-up
- Over the shoulder

**Motion Keywords:**
- Slowly, deliberately, briskly, energetically
- Walking, rotating, gliding, floating
- Settles, maintains position, comes to rest

---

### IMAGE PROMPT FORMULA (Midjourney / Flux)

**Structure:**
```
[SUBJECT with specific details] + [ACTION/POSE] + [ENVIRONMENT with 3-4 elements] + [LIGHTING] + [STYLE/AESTHETIC] + [CAMERA/LENS] --ar X:X --v 6
```

**Key Principles:**
1. **Specific visual details** — "35-year-old man with weathered hands" beats "a man"
2. **Material + texture words** — hammered gold, matte black, brushed chrome
3. **Lighting is everything** — golden hour, rim lighting, Rembrandt lighting
4. **Reference real cameras** — "Sony A7III 85mm f/1.8" triggers photorealistic training data
5. **Reinforce key elements** — Repeat important things in different ways
6. **Use weight markers** — `++important element++` for Kling

## QUICK REFERENCE: DO vs DON'T

### DO:
- Specify exact camera movement and position
- Use specific ages, materials, colors
- Include lighting direction ("from camera left")
- Add motion endpoints ("then settles into place")
- Reference real camera specs for photorealism
- Limit to 3-5 key environmental elements

### DON'T:
- Use negative phrasing ("no clouds")
- Write conceptually ("hacking the mainframe")
- Be vague ("a person," "moving around")
- Overload with 10+ elements
- Skip camera direction entirely
- Use conversational language ("can you make...")
