## VIDEO PROMPTS (Runway Gen-3 / Kling 2.6)

### Shot 1: The Spark (3s)
```
Static macro shot, pure black background: Thousands of tiny gold particles slowly materialize from darkness, floating and drifting like dust catching light. Particles cluster and disperse organically. Deep black negative space dominates frame. Single warm light source from below creates subtle glow on particles. Hypnotic, meditative opening. Camera remains still as particles build density over 3 seconds.
```

### Shot 2: The Rise (4s)
```
Low angle tracking shot following from behind: Silhouette of figure in premium black jacket walking toward rooftop edge at golden hour. City skyline stretches below, buildings catching warm orange sunset light. Wind moves jacket fabric slightly. Camera tracks steadily behind, then rises to reveal vast cityscape as figure reaches edge and stops. Golden hour rim lighting creates halo effect on figure's outline. Cinematic shallow depth of field.
```

### Shot 3: The Gathering (4s)
```
Quick cut montage, handheld energy: Three rapid cuts — hands clasping in greeting with gold ring visible, group of friends in black and earth tones entering modern penthouse doorway, genuine laughter caught mid-moment with warm lamp lighting on faces. Each shot 1.3 seconds. Warm interior lighting with gold accents. Movement feels natural, documentary-style. Shallow depth of field throughout. Premium lifestyle aesthetic.
```

### Shot 4: The Moment (4s)
```
Wide establishing shot, slow dolly forward: Rooftop gathering at night, 8-10 people in premium casual wear spread across modern terrace. Large TV visible in background casting blue glow (game implied, not shown). Warm string lights and golden lamp accents. Someone raises crystal glass in foreground. Genuine energy of anticipation and connection. Camera dollies forward slowly into the scene. City lights twinkle in distance beyond railing.
```

### Shot 5: The Symbol (3s)
```
Abstract macro shot, static camera: Liquid gold flows across pure black surface, forming organic shapes that pulse and merge. The flowing gold briefly suggests geometric Aztec sun pattern before dissolving back into abstract flow. Dramatic side lighting catches metallic reflections. Hypnotic and mysterious. Gold pools and settles as pattern completes. Premium luxury brand aesthetic.
```

### Shot 6: The Triumph (4s)
```
Wide shot from behind, slow crane rise: Group of 5 friends standing at rooftop edge, backs to camera, overlooking illuminated city skyline at night. Gold-tinted city lights stretch to horizon. Wind catches clothing. One figure in center slowly raises fist in triumph — not aggressive, celebratory. Camera rises slowly, revealing more of the vast cityscape. Moment of quiet victory. Cinematic scale.
```

### Shot 7: End Card (3s)
```
Static centered frame: Pure black background. AC circular badge logo in brushed gold metallic finish fades in at center, catching subtle rim light. Logo holds for 1.5 seconds. Text fades in below in clean gold sans-serif: "ELEVATE THE RITUAL" — then smaller: "ADVANCED CULTIVATORS". Premium brand endcard. Minimal, powerful.
```

## AUDIO DIRECTION

**0-3s (The Spark):**
- Deep bass pulse synced to heartbeat
- Subtle low-end rumble building

**3-7s (The Rise):**
- Bass continues, subtle synth pad enters
- Wind ambience, distant city sounds

**7-11s (The Gathering):**
- Energy picks up, rhythmic percussion enters
- Muffled laughter, door sounds, glasses

**11-15s (The Moment):**
- Full beat drops, electronic/tribal fusion
- Crowd energy, anticipation

**15-18s (The Symbol):**
- Beat strips back, just bass and texture
- Liquid/metallic sound design

**18-22s (The Triumph):**
- Beat builds back, triumphant swell
- Distant cheering, wind

**22-25s (End Card):**
- Music resolves to single sustained note
- Silence with reverb tail

**Reference:** Hans Zimmer meets Kaytranada — cinematic but rhythmic
