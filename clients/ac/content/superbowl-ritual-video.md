## VIDEO PROMPTS (Runway Gen-3 / Kling 2.6)

### Shot 1: Ancient Preparation
```
Low angle close-up, static camera: Weathered bronze hands of a man in his 50s grinding in a circular stone mortar, firelight flickering from camera right casting warm orange glow and dancing shadows. Ancient stone workshop environment with obsidian walls. Hands move in slow circular motion, deliberate and ritualistic. Dust particles float in firelight. Shallow depth of field, warm color grade.
```

### Shot 2: Sacred Material
```
Macro overhead shot, slow push-in: Interior of ancient stone mortar containing golden powder catching firelight, creating subtle sparkle. Hands visible at edges of frame. Warm amber lighting from below. Camera pushes in slowly over 3 seconds, revealing texture of gold material. Premium luxury aesthetic, shallow depth of field.
```

### Shot 3: Time Transition
```
Close-up tracking shot: Ancient weathered hands holding stone mortar smoothly transition to modern clean hands with subtle gold ring, holding premium glass container. Camera maintains steady close-up throughout morph. Lighting shifts from warm firelight to cool modern ambient. Seamless 3-second transformation. Cinematic quality.
```

### Shot 4: Modern Reveal
```
Medium close-up, slight low angle: Modern hands in early 30s carefully twisting cork top off glass Bullet Chamber, revealing 7 premium joints in revolver pattern inside. Soft warm interior lighting from camera left. Hands move deliberately, cork releases with satisfying motion. Camera holds as contents are revealed. Premium product commercial aesthetic.
```

### Shot 5: The Gathering
```
Wide establishing shot, slow dolly forward: Premium modern living room at dusk, three friends in late 20s in upscale casual wear (black, navy, earth tones) settling onto leather sofa. Large TV visible but not in focus, casting subtle blue glow. Warm lamp lighting creates cozy atmosphere. Camera dollies forward slowly. Genuine relaxed energy between friends. Aspirational lifestyle aesthetic.
```

### Shot 6: Product Hero
```
Low angle close-up, slow push-in: Bullet Chamber glass tube resting on dark walnut coffee table, circular gold badge with crossed axes catching warm lamp light. Friends visible in soft bokeh background. Camera pushes in slowly, badge reflections intensify. Premium product photography in motion. 3-second hold on product detail.
```

### Shot 7: End Card
```
Static centered frame: AC circular badge logo centered on pure black background, gold metallic finish catching subtle rim light. Logo holds for 2 seconds. Text fades in below: "Game Day. Elevated." in clean sans-serif gold type. Premium brand endcard aesthetic.
```

## AUDIO DIRECTION

**0-7s (Ancient):**
- Deep ceremonial drums, low frequency
- Crackling fire ambience
- Subtle wind/breath sounds

**7-10s (Transition):**
- Drums fade, modern bass tone rises
- Woosh/morph sound effect

**10-20s (Modern):**
- Subtle hip-hop instrumental (low, chill)
- Room ambience, distant TV crowd noise
- Cork twist/pop foley

**20-22s (End Card):**
- Music resolves to single bass note
- Silence with subtle reverb tail

## PRODUCTION NOTES

### What to Generate First:
1. Shot 1 (Ancient hands) — sets the tone
2. Shot 4 (Modern hands opening) — product hero
3. Shot 6 (Product on table) — can use as standalone

### Editing Flow:
- Cut on motion (hands moving)
- Transition shot (3) is the trickiest — may need multiple generations
- Color grade: warm/orange for ancient, warm/amber for modern (consistent gold thread)

### Compliance Check:
- ✅ No consumption shown
- ✅ No plant material visible
- ✅ Product is sealed/packaged
- ✅ All characters appear 25+
- ✅ No prices or health claims
