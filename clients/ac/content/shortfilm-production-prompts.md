# AI VIDEO GEN TIPS

**For Runway/Kling/Pika:**

- Use prompts exactly as written—they're optimized for clarity
- Generate shots individually, edit together in post
- For logo visibility: generate base shot first, composite logo in After Effects if AI struggles
- Slow motion shots may need 2x generation length, then speed-ramped
- Chalk/particle effects work best with "slow motion, dust particles, volumetric light" keywords

**Recommended settings:**

- Resolution: 1080p or 4K where available
- Duration: 4-5 seconds per clip
- Style: "Cinematic, film grain, shallow depth of field" added to all prompts
