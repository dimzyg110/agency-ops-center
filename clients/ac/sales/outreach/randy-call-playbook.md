## Scripts

### Pop-Up No Reorder (Priority 1)

**Opener:**
> "Hey [name], this is Randy from AC - Advanced Cultivators. You guys did a pop-up with us a while back, and I wanted to follow up personally. How'd it go from your side?"

**Listen.** Then:
> "Got it. Anything we could've done differently?"

**Transition to event:**
> "I appreciate the feedback. Hey, quick thing - we're doing an industry event Feb 8th in Woburn with TNG. Pretty low-key, watching the game, good people. You should come through."

**If interested:**
> "Cool - what's the best number to text you details?"

**Book the meeting:**
> "Before we hang up - I'd love to grab 15 minutes this week or next to talk about what working together could look like. What's your schedule?"

---

### Dormant Big $$$ (Priority 2)

**Opener:**
> "Hey [name], this is Randy from AC. I'm helping the team with partnerships and realized we haven't talked since [month]. No pitch today - I just wanted to check in and see how things are going on your end."

**Listen.** Then ask:
> "Anything we could've done better last time?"

**If they had issues:**
> "I hear you. That's good feedback - I'll make sure the team knows. Things have been evolving on our end."

**Transition to event:**
> "Hey, we're doing something Feb 8th - industry event in Woburn with TNG. Thought of you. You should come."

**Book the meeting:**
> "I'd love to reconnect properly - grab coffee or a call this week? What works?"

---

### VIP Intro (Priority 3)

**Opener:**
> "Hey [name], this is Randy - I work on the partnership and marketing side at AC. Just wanted to introduce myself since [Clarice/Debbie] handles your account but I figured we should know each other too."

**Quick rapport:**
> "How's business been? Anything new on your end?"

**Transition to event:**
> "Quick thing - Feb 8th we're doing an event in Woburn, Superbowl watch party with TNG. Industry people, good vibes. You on the list?"

**If not:**
> "Let me add you. What's the best number?"

**Plant the seed:**
> "I'd love to grab 15 min sometime - we've got some stuff coming that I think would be interesting for you guys."

---

### Boston Metro VIP (Priority 4)

Same as VIP Intro, but emphasize:
> "It's in Woburn so you're right there - easy drive."

---

### B2B Upsell (Priority 5)

Same opener as VIP Intro, then:
> "I know you guys have been doing [retail] with us - I'm curious, have you ever thought about [toll processing / bulk / white label]?"

**Listen.** If interested:
> "Cool - let's grab a call this week. I can walk you through what that looks like."

## Objection Handling

**"We're good right now"**
> "Totally get it. Hey, the event Feb 8th is just industry people hanging out - no pressure. You should come through regardless."

**"Send me an email"**
> "For sure - what's your email? Actually, I'd rather just grab 10 minutes on the phone if you've got time this week. Email gets lost."

**"Who are you again?"**
> "Randy from AC - I handle partnerships and marketing. [Clarice/Debbie] handles the day-to-day, but I wanted to introduce myself since we'll probably be working together more."

**"We had issues last time"**
> "I appreciate you telling me that. What happened?" [Listen, don't defend] "That's helpful to know. Things have been evolving - I'd love a chance to show you."
