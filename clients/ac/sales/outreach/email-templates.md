# AC Outreach Email Templates

**Purpose:** 4 email templates for different outreach scenarios. Short, direct, value-first.

---

## TEMPLATE 1: Cold Outreach (New Contact)

**Subject:** Quick question about [STORE NAME]

---

Hey [NAME],

I'm Randy with Advanced Cultivators. We're a craft cannabis brand out of MA focused on quality flower and prerolls.

I've been following [STORE NAME] - love what you're doing with [specific thing: selection, vibe, location, community focus].

We work differently than most brands. Instead of dropping product and disappearing, we partner with stores to actually move it. Staff meals, pop-ups, content for your social - the whole thing.

Worth a quick call to see if there's a fit?

Randy
Advanced Cultivators
[PHONE]

---

**When to use:** First touch with someone you've never met. Personalize the [specific thing].

## USAGE NOTES

### Do:
- Personalize the [BRACKETS] - generic emails get ignored
- Keep it short - these are meant to be scanned
- End with a clear ask (call, meeting, RSVP)
- Send from personal email, not marketing blast

### Don't:
- Attach PDFs or decks in first email
- Write paragraphs about AC's history
- Use "just checking in" as subject line
- Send same template to everyone at same company

### Follow-Up Cadence:
- Email 1: Initial outreach
- Wait 3-4 days
- Email 2: Short follow-up ("Bumping this up - any interest?")
- Wait 5-7 days
- Email 3: Final ("Last one from me - let me know if timing's off")
- Then stop (don't spam)
