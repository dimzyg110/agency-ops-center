## The Call Flow

### 1. OPEN (30 sec)

> "Hey [Name], this is Randy from Advanced Cultivators. I handle partnerships for AC. Quick question - you guys carrying any AC product right now?"

**If yes:** → "Nice. How's it moving? I'm calling about something new we're doing with a few stores."

**If no:** → "Got it. That's why I'm calling - looking for a few pilot stores. Open to a quick conversation?"

---

### 2. DISCOVERY (2-3 min)

Ask:
- "What's moving for you? Best-selling flower tier?"
- "Where are you seeing gaps on the shelf?"
- "How's your current supplier on consistency?"
- "How many budtenders on your floor?"

Listen for:
- Price sensitivity → Zapacka pitch
- Supply issues → "We grow what we sell" pitch
- Shelf gaps → Variety pitch
- Budtender disengagement → AC Certified pitch

---

### 3. POSITION (1-2 min)

**Price-conscious:**
> "Zapacka. $10 eighths wholesale, retail at $25-30, that's 60%+ margin. Quality at a price point that moves."

**Supply issues:**
> "We grow everything we sell. No broker games. I can show you inventory - 5,000+ units of core SKUs right now."

**Variety gaps:**
> "11 strains in rotation. RS-11, Suave, Truffle Popz, Strawberry Pavé. I'll send you the full menu."

**Budtender engagement:**
> "AC Certified - we feed your team, give them swag, run pop-ups. Plus we're launching something for budtender incentives that I can tell you about."

---

### 4. THE ASK (Be Specific)

**New account:**
> "Let's start with a $3,000 test order. Mix of your choice. See how it moves in 2 weeks. If it works, we talk monthly. If not, no hard feelings. Fair?"

**AC Certified:**
> "We're rolling out AC Certified with 5-10 stores. 4-week cycle - product drop, staff meal, swag, pop-up, review. First order $5,000, then $7,500/month. Want to be a pilot?"

**Reactivation:**
> "Not here to pitch hard. What happened last time? ... [Listen] ... Would you try again with a smaller order? $2,500, see how it goes?"

---

### 5. HANDLE OBJECTIONS

**"Prices too high"**
> "Compared to what? ... Our A Bud is $17, you retail at $40+, that's 55%+ margin. Want lower? Zapacka is $10."

**"Happy with current suppliers"**
> "Not asking to replace anyone. One shelf spot to test. $3,000. If it doesn't beat what you have, you don't reorder."

**"Tried AC before, didn't sell"**
> "What happened? ... Here's what's different - AC Certified means we're in your store, training your team, running pop-ups. We're invested in sell-through."

**"Need to think about it"**
> "What specifically? Would it help if I sent the menu so you can look at numbers? Then we reconnect [day]?"

**"Send me an email"**
> "For sure. But email gets buried. Can we do 10 minutes on [day]? I'd rather answer questions live."

---

### 6. CLOSE WITH NEXT STEP

**Interested:**
> "I'll send the menu now. Can you review by [day] and we'll hop on for 15 min to finalize?"

**Needs time:**
> "Let's put 15 minutes on calendar for [day/time]. I'll send info, you review, we decide together."

**Coming to event:**
> "Let's talk more on the 8th. I'll send the menu now so you can look beforehand."
