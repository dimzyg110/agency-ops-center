## Content Strategy Overview

**Primary Audience:** Dispensary buyers, cannabis industry professionals, wholesale partners
- Decision-makers at licensed dispensaries
- Purchasing managers seeking premium craft cannabis
- Cannabis retailers looking for consistent, high-quality supply

**Secondary Audience:** Cannabis enthusiasts, future retail customers, industry observers
- Connoisseurs who appreciate craft cultivation
- Local community members anticipating retail opening
- Budtenders and industry workers

**Brand Voice:**
- **Expert cultivators** - Technical knowledge, precision, mastery
- **Craft-focused** - Artisanal approach, attention to detail, small-batch quality
- **Science meets art** - Data-driven decisions balanced with cultivation intuition
- **B2B professional** - Reliable, consistent, partnership-oriented
- **Transparent** - Open about processes, genetics, and quality standards
