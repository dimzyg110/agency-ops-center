| Videographer | [TBD] | Content capture |
| Photographer | [TBD] | Event photos |

| Check-In | [TBD] | Blacksite app verification |

### The Play
1. **Arrival:** Greet top 20 personally → they feel important
2. **First Hour:** Introduce targets to each other → you're the connector
3. **Halftime:** Quick thank you + Cue tease (don't pitch, intrigue)
4. **End of Game:** "Let's grab coffee this week" → book meetings AT the event
5. **Exit:** Have AC Certified one-pagers available (on table, not pushed)

### What NOT To Do
- Hard sell during the party
- Corner people with pitch decks
- Make it feel like a sales event
