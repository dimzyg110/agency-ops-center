## Part 3: Content Strategy — Team Doc Style

### No Single Host. Documentary Crew.

BGC content is not talking-head interviews. It's the AC cultivation team doing what they do best—growing exceptional cannabis—documented like a nature documentary meets behind-the-scenes.

### Content Pillars

**1. The Grow Series (40%)**
Multi-episode documentation of each breeder collaboration:
- Episode 1: The Challenge (breeder drops off seeds, shares vision)
- Episodes 2-6: The Journey (weekly/monthly progress updates)
- Episode 7: The Harvest (results, analysis, breeder reaction)
- Episode 8: The Smoke Test (final evaluation)

**2. Breeder Check-ins (25%)**
Conversations with the breeder throughout the grow:
- Not interviews—these are working sessions
- Problem-solving discussions
- Breeder seeing their vision executed
- Real reactions, not scripted content

**3. The Lab (20%)**
Technical deep-dives:
- Data overlays on time-lapses
- Equipment and technique explanations
- Pheno hunting process
- Environmental control breakdowns

**4. Team Moments (10%)**
The human side of AC:
- BTS banter and real conversations
- Problem-solving under pressure
- Celebrations and setbacks
- The culture of the cultivation team

**5. Community (5%)**
- Member grows and questions
- Community submissions
- Q&A sessions

### Visual Style

| Element | Approach |
|---------|----------|
| **Camera work** | Multiple angles, time-lapse, macro shots of trichomes |
| **Data** | Overlaid graphics showing temps, humidity, feed schedules |
| **Sound** | Natural sound + subtle score, not overproduced |
| **Editing** | Documentary pacing—not rushed, not slow |
| **Graphics** | Clean, scientific, premium feel |
| **Vibe** | Raw, authentic, not polished talking-head style |

### Signature Formats

**"72 Hours"** — Time-lapse of critical growth phases with data overlay

**"The Check-In"** — Weekly breeder conversation (unscripted, working session)

**"Under the Scope"** — Microscopic trichome and plant structure analysis

**"The Numbers"** — Data deep-dives for cultivation nerds

**"Smoke Report"** — Final harvest evaluation with breeder

## Part 4: From 0 to Caring — Launch Strategy

### The Challenge

We have no audience. How do we get attention with zero followers?

### The Answer: Borrowed Credibility + Proof

**Phase 1: The Strain Hunters Launch (Month 1-2)**

The Strain Hunters partnership IS the launch catalyst:
- Their name carries weight in the genetics world
- Their challenge to AC creates narrative tension
- Their audience becomes our initial reach
- Documenting their grow is inherently interesting

**Launch Sequence:**
1. **Week 1:** "The Challenge" video drops (Strain Hunters gives AC seeds)
2. **Week 2:** Episode 1 of the grow series
3. **Week 3-4:** Consistent documentation of early growth
4. **Ongoing:** Weekly episodes documenting the journey

**Why this works:**
- Strain Hunters audience is exactly our target
- The story (will they succeed?) creates engagement
- No need to explain who we are—the work speaks

**Phase 2: Existing Relationships (Month 2-4)**

AC has spent 20+ years building breeder relationships. Activate them:
- Reach out to existing breeder contacts
- Offer the VIP service model
- Even one "yes" creates the next content series
- Each collaboration builds the portfolio

**Outreach approach:**
> "We've launched a VIP service where we grow your genetics to the highest standard and document the entire process. You get the documentation to use however you want, plus validation of your work. We're already doing this with Strain Hunters. Interested in being next?"

**Phase 3: Build Credibility Through Documentation (Month 3-6)**

The seeds → harvest journey IS the content. Every grow:
- Proves AC's expertise
- Creates shareable documentation
- Gives the breeder content to share
- Builds the BGC library

**No audience required—just execute at the highest level and document it.**

**Phase 4: Community Formation (Month 4-6)**

As content builds, audience follows:
- Comments and questions become community
- Discord/forum for serious cultivators
- Members share their own grows
- AC provides expertise and feedback

### The Flywheel Effect

```
VIP Grow → Documentation → Content → Audience → More Breeders Want In → More VIP Grows
```

Each successful grow makes the next easier to sell to breeders.
