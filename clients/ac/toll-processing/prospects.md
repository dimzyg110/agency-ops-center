Advanced Cultivators should position the **RollPros** technology as the key differentiator against standard cone-filling services.

#### 1. The "Quality at Scale" Pitch (For Tier 2-11 Cultivators)
*Target: Cultivators with >5,000 sq ft canopy.*
- **Pain Point:** Manual rolling is too slow; standard cone-vibration machines pack too tight at the crutch and loose at the tip, leading to canoeing/bad burn.
- **Pitch:** "Stop selling flower to others to brand. Launch your own premium preroll line with hand-rolled quality at production speed. Our RollPros technology ensures perfect airflow and consistent burn—something vibration machines can't match."

#### 2. The "No-CapEx Scale" Pitch (For Tier 1 & Micros)
*Target: Smaller growers & micros.*
- **Pain Point:** Capital is tight; can't afford a $150k+ automated line.
- **Pitch:** "Don't burn cash on equipment. Use our infrastructure to scale your brand immediately. Low MOQs to get you started."

#### 3. The "House Brand" Pitch (For Retailers)
*Target: Dispensaries without cultivation.*
- **Pain Point:** Low margins on third-party brands.
- **Pitch:** "Create your own high-margin house brand. We handle the sourcing (or use yours), processing, and packaging. You just sell it."
