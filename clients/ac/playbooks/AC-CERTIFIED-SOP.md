## 1. PROGRAM OVERVIEW

### What Is AC Certified?

AC Certified is a retail partnership program where we don't just sell product — we help stores sell through.

**The Deal:**

- Store commits to a 4-week rotation cycle
- AC provides pop-up support, staff training, swag, and content
- We review sell-through together and plan the next cycle

**Why It Works:**

- Stores get dedicated support (not just another vendor)
- AC gets predictable recurring orders
- Budtenders get trained, fed, and equipped to actually sell

### Who Is It For?

| Good Fit                    | Bad Fit                      |
| --------------------------- | ---------------------------- |
| Stores open to pop-ups      | "Just send me a menu" stores |
| Engaged management          | Absent/unreachable buyers    |
| Decent foot traffic         | Dead locations               |
| Staff who participate       | Apathetic budtenders         |
| Willing to give shelf space | No room for new brands       |

## 2. QUALIFICATION & ONBOARDING

### Step 1: Initial Conversation

Before pitching AC Certified, ask these **qualifier questions**:

```
1. "How do you currently handle slow-moving inventory?"
   → Looking for: Flexibility, not "we never discount"

2. "Do you have humidity-controlled storage for flower?"
   → Looking for: Yes, or willingness to store properly

3. "Would you be open to us doing pop-ups and staff trainings?"
   → Looking for: Enthusiasm, not "maybe"

4. "Who makes the ordering decisions, and can they commit to a 4-week cycle?"
   → Looking for: Direct access to decision maker

5. "What's your average weekly flower movement?"
   → Looking for: Enough volume to justify the program
```

**If they answer poorly to 2+ questions → Not a fit for AC Certified. Offer standard wholesale instead.**

### Step 2: The Pitch

Use the AC Certified one-pager. Key points:

> "We're not just dropping product and disappearing. We're going to:
>
> - Train your staff
> - Do pop-ups
> - Feed your team
> - Create content featuring your store
> - Review sell-through with you every 4 weeks
>
> In exchange, we need premium shelf placement and a commitment to the cycle."

### Step 3: Agreement

Before first order, store must agree to:

1. **AC Certified Terms** (see Section 4)
2. **Participation requirements** (pop-ups, training, shelf placement)
3. **Storage responsibility** (they're liable once delivered)

**Get verbal confirmation or signed agreement before first shipment.**

### Step 4: First Order

- Use standard AC Certified order form
- Minimum order applies (see Section 4)
- Schedule Week 1 pop-up/training BEFORE product ships

## 3. THE 4-WEEK CYCLE

### Week 1: LAUNCH + TRAINING

**Goal:** Product lands, staff trained and ready to sell from Day 1

| Task                     | Owner     | Details                                  |
| ------------------------ | --------- | ---------------------------------------- |
| Product delivery         | AC Ops    | Confirm receipt with buyer               |
| Staff training           | Field Rep | 15-20 min huddle: strains, terps, effects, selling points |
| Cheat sheets distributed | Field Rep | One per budtender — keep behind counter  |
| Swag distribution        | Field Rep | Shirts, hats, lighters — quality only    |
| Content capture          | Field Rep | Photos/video of team, product on shelf   |

**Training Covers:**
- Quick brand story (first Latino-owned cultivator in MA, craft flower, Fitchburg)
- This cycle's strains: THC%, terps, flavor, effects, who it's for
- How to recommend: "If they like X, try this because Y"
- Quick close lines from cheat sheet

**Deliverable:** Staff can sell AC products confidently starting today

### Week 2: REINFORCEMENT

**Goal:** Answer questions, keep momentum, light accountability

| Task                      | Owner     | Details                              |
| ------------------------- | --------- | ------------------------------------ |
| Check-in call/visit       | Field Rep | How's it moving? What questions came up? |
| Answer staff questions    | Field Rep | Via manager or direct — fill any gaps |
| Share content from Week 1 | Marketing | Tag store on social                  |
| Mini-challenge (optional) | Field Rep | "Sell 10 this week, winner gets [reward]" |
| Quick refresher           | Field Rep | 5-min recap if needed, reinforce top sellers |

**Deliverable:** Staff confident, questions answered, early momentum tracked

### Week 3: ACTIVATION

**Goal:** Drive consumer traffic and sales

| Task                | Owner     | Details                            |
| ------------------- | --------- | ---------------------------------- |
| Pop-up event        | Field Rep | 90-min in-store activation         |
| Customer engagement | Field Rep | Education, not hard selling        |
| Content capture     | Field Rep | Photos/video for store + AC social |
| UGC push            | Marketing | Encourage customers to post        |

**Pop-Up Run of Show (90 min):**
| Time | Activity |
|------|----------|
| 0:00-0:10 | Setup + manager check-in |
| 0:10-0:20 | Staff huddle (most important) |
| 0:20-1:10 | Customer engagement |
| 1:10-1:20 | Breakdown + sales recap with manager |
| 1:20-1:30 | Content capture + thank yous |

**Deliverable:** Visible sales lift, content captured

### Week 4: CONVERSION

**Goal:** Secure the reorder, plan next cycle

| Task                   | Owner             | Details                          |
| ---------------------- | ----------------- | -------------------------------- |
| Sell-through review    | Field Rep + Buyer | How much moved? What worked?     |
| Performance assessment | Field Rep         | Green/Yellow/Red (see Section 5) |
| Reorder conversation   | Field Rep         | "Let's plan the next 4 weeks"    |
| Next cycle strains     | Field Rep + Buyer | What do they want? What's fresh? |
| Schedule Week 1        | Field Rep         | Lock in the pop-up date          |

**Deliverable:** Reorder placed, next cycle scheduled

## 5. PERFORMANCE & EXIT CRITERIA

### Sell-Through Benchmarks

| Sell-Through          | Status    | AC Response                                                |
| --------------------- | --------- | ---------------------------------------------------------- |
| **70%+**              | 🟢 Green  | Healthy. Continue to next cycle.                           |
| **50-70%**            | 🟡 Yellow | Check in. What's blocking? Adjust next order size down.    |
| **<50%**              | 🔴 Red    | Serious conversation. Reduce order significantly or pause. |
| **<30% for 2 cycles** | ⬛ Exit   | Not a fit. Transition to standard wholesale.               |

### What Happens If Product Doesn't Sell?

**Our position:**

- AC provides significant support (pop-ups, training, content, 4 weeks of attention)
- If product still doesn't move after all that, something else is wrong
- We'll work with you to diagnose (placement? pricing? wrong strains? staff not engaged?)
- But AC cannot be liable for unsold inventory

**If sell-through is consistently low:**

1. First occurrence: Conversation + smaller next order
2. Second occurrence: Pause program, assess fit
3. Third occurrence: Exit AC Certified, return to standard wholesale

**AC Certified is for stores that work with us, not stores we have to carry.**

### Exit Process

When a store exits AC Certified:

1. Field Rep has direct conversation with buyer
2. Offer transition to standard wholesale (no support, just orders)
3. Update tracker status to "Exited"
4. Note reason in CRM
5. No hard feelings — door stays open if things change

## 10. FAQ & OBJECTION HANDLING

### "We're already working with too many brands"

> "I hear you. That's actually why this works — we're not asking you to add another brand that just sits there. We're going to actively help move it. Most brands don't do that."

### "We don't do pop-ups"

> "Totally get it. Can I ask — is it a bandwidth thing or a policy thing? Because we handle everything. Your team just shows up and gets fed."

### "What if it doesn't sell?"

> "That's the whole point of the program. We're not just dropping product — we're doing trainings, pop-ups, giving your staff everything they need. If it still doesn't move after all that, we figure out why together. But we've found that when stores actually work with us on the cycle, it sells."

### "Your prices are too high"

> "I get it. Here's the thing — we're not trying to be the $15 eighth that races to the bottom. We're positioning as craft. Tower 3 does the same thing with organic and they're selling at higher prices. The support we provide helps your team justify the price to customers."

### "We tried AC before and it didn't work"

> "I appreciate you being honest. Can I ask what happened? [Listen] Yeah, that's exactly why we built AC Certified. Before, we'd drop product and hope. Now we're committed to the sell-through — pop-ups, training, the whole thing. Would you be open to giving it another shot with the new approach?"
