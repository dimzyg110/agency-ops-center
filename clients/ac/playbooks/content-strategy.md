# AC Content Strategy

## Core Themes (Always Present)

These themes should be woven into ALL content:

1. **Quality** - Craft, precision, standards, attention to detail
2. **Heritage** - Mexican/Aztec roots, culture, identity, pride
3. **Hustle/Grind** - Discipline, work ethic, "built not bought" mentality

### Inspirational/Motivational Content
Add more inspirational, motivational, and hustle/grind mentality content regularly. This should be a constant presence in the feed - not just product posts.

**Quote directions:**
- Hustle: "Built, not bought." / "While they talk, we work." / "Discipline looks boring until it wins."
- Quality: "Craft takes time. Excellence takes longer." / "We don't cut corners. We set standards."
- Heritage: "Rooted in culture. Refined by craft." / "Our ancestors were cultivators too."

### Holiday Content Rule
**Every holiday needs at least 1 holiday-themed post.** Plan ahead and create assets for:
- Major holidays (New Year, Valentine's, 4/20, Cinco de Mayo, Memorial Day, July 4th, Labor Day, Halloween, Thanksgiving, Christmas)
- Cultural holidays (Dia de los Muertos, Hispanic Heritage Month)
- Fun/meme holidays relevant to audience

### Bullet Chamber Campaign (PRIORITY 1)

**Concept:** "Loaded"

The prerolls in the circular glass case look like bullets in a revolver chamber. Lean into this:
- Tactical/luxury aesthetic
- Slow, deliberate motion
- Sound design: clicks, locks, mechanical precision
- Color: AC dark + gold accents
- Mood: Controlled power, not aggressive

**Assets Available:**
- Photos of prerolls in circle glass case (front, top, side views)

**Deliverables Needed:**
1. Product mockups (circular case → bullet chamber packaging concept)
2. 3 Kling/Veo motion videos
3. Social copy options

**Packaging Mockup Ideas:**
1. Circular case shown from above, rotating slowly like a chamber
2. Case in a custom box that opens like an ammo case (foam insert, premium feel)
3. Single preroll being pulled from the chamber (product hero)

**Kling/Veo Motion Prompts:**
