# AC Certified: Retail & Partnership Operating Playbook

This is a system for turning dispensaries into long-term revenue partners.

---

## 1. Mission & Philosophy

### The Shift: From "Vendor" to "Growth Partner"

Most brands dump product on the shelf and hope it sells. We don't do that.

**Our mission is not just to get AC products into the store; it is to move them out of the store.**

We sell our ability to Sell-Through. We provide the energy, the education, and the content that makes a dispensary manager look good and a budtender want to sell our product.

**The Golden Rule:** We don't just take an order. We create a plan to sell it.

---

## 2. The Product Menu

We're selling two things to dispensaries. They are inseparable.

### 1. The Product (Physical)
High-quality flower, pre-rolls, vapes, and concentrates that justify the shelf space.

**Value Prop:** Reliability, quality, consistency.

### 2. The "AC Certified" Program (Service)
A structured retail support system.

**Includes:**
- Staff education and onboarding programs
- High-energy pop-ups
- Budtender incentives
- Custom content creation for the store

**Value Prop:** We bring the energy and resources.

## 3. Lead Generation & Data Strategy

We use our Data Engine to identify the stores that actually matter.

### Strategic Targeting

We use our database (Lead Tool) to filter Massachusetts retailers by:
- **Location:** High foot-traffic zones
- **Menu Gaps:** Stores missing our specific product categories
- **Volume:** The "Top 50" doors that drive 80% of the state's volume

### The Segmentation

| Tier | Description | Approach |
|------|-------------|----------|
| **Tier 1 (Target Partners)** | Top 50 stores. High volume, brand-conscious. | In-person visits, "Certified" pitches |
| **Tier 2 (Standard Retail)** | Good stores needing routine maintenance | Occasional pop-ups |
| **Tier 3 (Transactional)** | Low volume or difficult management | Take the order, don't invest heavy marketing |

- **Bucket B (48 leads):** Watchlist - soft outreach

## 4. The Sales Process (Relationship Cycle)

This is not a transactional sale. It is a relationship cycle.

### 1. Outreach (The Value Add)
We don't ask "Want to buy?"

We ask: **"Can we sponsor a staff meal and show you how we drive sell-through?"**

### 2. The Drop-By (Sample + Momentum)
In-person visits are mandatory for Tier 1.
- Product samples (where allowed)
- "Pop-Up Menu" showing how we drive sell-through

### 3. Qualification (The Fit)
- Does this store have traffic?
- Is the manager open to pop-ups?
- If they won't let us support the sell-through, they are not a Tier 1 partner.

### 4. The "Pilot" Order
Small initial order tied to a scheduled Pop-Up date.

**Pitch:** "Give us 2 weeks on the shelf, and we'll come in and blow it out."

### 5. The Activation (The Show)
We execute the "AC Certified" playbook (see Section 5).
- We capture content
- We feed the staff
- We make noise

### 6. The Reorder (The Goal)
- Review sell-through data
- Book the next drop and the next activation

## 5. The "AC Certified" Program

This is our competitive advantage. We don't just visit stores; we activate them.

An AC Certified Store is not a "buyer", they are a **strategic partner**. We invest in them, and in return, they prioritize us.

### Value Exchange

| They Get | They Give |
|----------|-----------|
| Priority Access: First allocation on new drops | Shelf Presence: Premium placement |
| Content Engine: Video/photo of their store/team, co-branded social assets | Staff Buy-In: Budtenders actually participate |
| Tech & Perks: App access + high-quality swag | Feedback: Real data on what's moving |
| Recurring Energy: Consistent pop-ups | Consistent Reorders: The cycle continues |

### What We Deliver (The Toolkit)

**Staff Energy (The Meal)**
We feed the team during drops (Pizza, Tacos, Coffee runs). A fed budtender is a happy budtender.

**Premium Swag**
No cheap junk. We drop high-quality shirts, hats, and gear that staff actually want to wear on the floor.

**The Content Engine**
We film the activation. We edit it. We deliver co-branded social assets featuring their team.

**Digital Incentives**
Staff get exclusive access to training, tracking, and earning direct rewards.

*Note: This could integrate with Cue for budtender incentives, but Cue is a separate standalone product.*

## 6. The 4-Week Activation Cycle

We run a 4-week repeatable loop.

### Week 1: THE LAUNCH (Hype)
- Product drop lands
- Staff onboarding + Meal
- Swag distribution + Content capture starts

### Week 2: THE EDUCATION (Knowledge)
- Budtender Cheat Sheets distributed
- App onboarding / "Mini-Challenge" for staff
- Focus: Making them experts on the SKU

### Week 3: THE COMMUNITY (Vibe)
- In-store consumer activation
- Local angle (community partner or theme)
- UGC (User Generated Content) push on social

### Week 4: THE CONVERSION (Business)
- Review sell-through numbers with Manager
- **The Lock:** Secure the reorder
- Schedule the next cycle

### Rules of the Game

**Entry (Qualification):**
To become Certified, a store must complete 1 successful order + 1 pilot activation with positive engagement.

**Renewal (Staying In):**
Reorder = Remain Certified. No Reorder = Downgrade to standard retail account.

## 7. The Pop-Up Standard

We do not stand behind a table looking at our phones. AC Pop-ups are events.

### The Kit
- Clean, branded table setup
- "AC Certified" Signage
- QR Codes for Budtender education/signup

### The Run of Show (90 Minutes)

| Time | Activity |
|------|----------|
| 0-10 min | Setup + Manager Check-in |
| 10-20 min | **Staff Huddle (The most important part).** Feed them, educate them, hype them up. |
| 20-80 min | Customer engagement. Focus on education and directing traffic to the register. |
| 80-90 min | Breakdown + Content Capture + Sales Recap with Manager |

## 8. CRM & Tracking

We use Apex to track relationships, not just transactions.

### Required Fields

| Field | Purpose |
|-------|---------|
| Account Status | Prospect / Active / Certified Partner / Dormant |
| Last Visit Date | Must be <30 days for Tier 1 |
| Last Pop-Up Date | When were we last on the floor? |
| Next Action | e.g., "Schedule Reorder Call" |
| Gatekeeper Name | Who actually places the order? |
| Budtender Champions | Names of staff we have the best relationships with |

**The Rule:** If a Tier 1 account hasn't been visited in 30 days, it is flagged as "At Risk."
