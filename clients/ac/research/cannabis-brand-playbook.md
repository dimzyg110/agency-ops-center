# Cannabis Brand Content Playbook
## Moving from Nugs to Nike-Level Branding

*Research compiled for Advanced Cultivators | February 2026*

---

## Executive Summary

The goal: Create compliant, cinematic, professional content that looks like it's from major brands like Nike and Jeeter—without showing flower/nugs.

**Key Insight**: The best cannabis brands (Jeeter, Cookies, STIIIZY) succeed by selling *lifestyle and culture*, not product shots. Nike succeeds by selling *stories of human achievement*, not sneaker specs.

---

## Part 1: Massachusetts Compliance Rules

### CCC Advertising Regulations (935 CMR 500.105)

**The 85% Rule**
- Any ad on TV, radio, mobile apps, social media, or internet must ensure 85%+ of audience is 21+
- Instagram/TikTok's demographics make this achievable for age-gated content

**Prohibited Elements**
- ❌ Medical symbols
- ❌ Images of marijuana/cannabis plant
- ❌ Colloquial references to cannabis
- ❌ Cartoons, mascots, bright neon colors
- ❌ Celebrity endorsements that appeal to minors
- ❌ Gifts, giveaways, discounts, coupons
- ❌ Price mentions in marketing materials
- ❌ Branded clothing, cups, novelty items in ads

**Required Disclaimers**
Must include "Please Consume Responsibly" plus 2+ of:
- "This product may cause impairment and may be habit forming."
- "For use only by adults 21 years of age or older."
- "There may be health risks associated with consumption."

**What's Allowed**
- ✅ Lifestyle imagery (no plant visible)
- ✅ Packaging shots
- ✅ Team/culture content
- ✅ Behind-the-scenes (facility, not plants)
- ✅ Community/event content
- ✅ Brand storytelling
- ✅ Logo and brand name (can include "Cannabis")

## Part 2: Nike's Playbook (Transferable Tactics)

### Core Philosophy
**"Sell the feeling, not the product."**

Nike rarely shows sneakers in isolation. They show athletes achieving, communities celebrating, barriers breaking.

### Key Strategies

**1. Emotional Storytelling**
- Universal themes: adversity, hope, ambition, resilience
- Focus on the human, not the product
- Example: "Dream Crazy" featured Kaepernick narrating athlete stories—not shoes

**2. Athlete Sensorials**
- Let ambassadors tell their own stories
- Hand the camera to real people
- Documentary-style content performs well

**3. Campaign Structure**
- Build anticipation → Live moment → Ongoing narrative
- Each post is part of a larger story
- Breaking4 campaign: behind-the-scenes → live stream → follow-up content

**4. Visual Consistency**
- High-quality, cinematic production
- Consistent color grading
- Unified brand voice across platforms

**5. Community Integration**
- UGC campaigns (hashtag-driven)
- Engage with audience interests (sports events, culture moments)
- Fans become ambassadors

### Nike Content Mix
- 40% Athlete stories / human moments
- 30% Campaign content / product launches
- 20% Cultural moments / event tie-ins
- 10% Community / UGC highlights

## Part 3: Cookies/STIIIZY Strategy

### Cookies (Berner)

**The Streetwear Play**
- Cannabis couldn't be trademarked early on → pivoted to clothing
- Streetwear (Cookies SF) became the compliant front door
- Comparison: Supreme x Cannabis

**Key Tactics**
- Founder as face of brand (Berner in every video)
- Music integration (Berner wears Cookies in music videos)
- Celebrity collabs (athletes, artists wearing the brand)
- Strain stories become cultural moments (Gary Payton, etc.)
- Named AdAge "Hottest Brand" 2021—first cannabis brand ever

**Visual Style**
- Blue dominant color
- Street/urban aesthetic
- High production value
- Lifestyle > product

### STIIIZY

**Premium Tech Aesthetic**
- Clean, minimal, Apple-like design language
- Sleek vape hardware as lifestyle accessory
- Focus on design and experience, not consumption

## Part 4: Compliant Content Categories

### What to Post Instead of Nugs

**1. Packaging as Hero**
- Beautifully lit product shots (sealed packaging only)
- Unboxing moments
- Packaging design details

**2. Lifestyle Imagery**
- Outdoor adventures (hiking, beach, urban)
- Social settings (dinner, gatherings—no consumption)
- Morning routines, self-care moments
- Music, art, culture scenes

**3. Team & Culture**
- Facility tours (non-plant areas)
- Employee spotlights
- Behind-the-scenes of operations
- Company values / mission content

**4. Agricultural Heritage**
- Soil, water, hands working (not plant visible)
- Farming heritage, family roots
- Sustainability practices
- Craft and care in process

**5. Abstract/Artistic**
- Macro textures (not plant)
- Color stories matching brand palette
- Motion graphics
- Cinematic b-roll of facilities

**6. Community**
- Event coverage
- Local partnerships
- Culture moments
- Customer stories (testimonial style, not consumption)

## Part 5: Cinematic Techniques

### Production Quality Markers

**Camera/Lighting**
- Shallow depth of field (f/1.8-2.8)
- Golden hour / blue hour lighting
- Consistent color temperature
- Professional color grading

**Motion**
- Slow-motion for emphasis
- Smooth gimbal/dolly movements
- Match cuts between scenes
- Intentional pacing (not rushed)

**Sound Design**
- Ambient audio layers
- Music that matches brand energy
- Voiceover as storytelling device

**Edit Style**
- Clean transitions
- Consistent aspect ratio
- Branded lower thirds / typography
- End with logo lockup

### Equipment Recommendations
- iPhone 15 Pro + gimbal (minimum viable)
- Sony A7 series or Blackmagic Pocket (ideal)
- DJI Ronin for motion
- LED panels for controlled lighting

- #CraftCannabis
- #CannaCommunity
- #LowellMA
- #BostonCannabis
- #CannabisCulture
- #PlantMedicine
- #WellnessLifestyle

- Price/discount language

- [ ] Monthly content planning

---

## Summary: The Formula

1. **Stories over products** — Tell human stories, not strain specs
2. **Lifestyle over consumption** — Show the life, not the use
3. **Craft over commodity** — Emphasize process, care, heritage
4. **Community over customers** — Build belonging, not just sales
5. **Compliance as creative constraint** — Use restrictions to force creativity

**Goal**: Someone scrolling should think "this looks like a Nike or Apple ad" before they realize it's cannabis.
