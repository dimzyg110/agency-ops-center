## Massachusetts CCC Compliance Rules

### The 85% Rule
**All advertising on TV, radio, mobile apps, social media, and the internet must target an audience where at least 85% are reasonably expected to be 21 years or older.**

### Age Restrictions
- Website must have age-gate verification (21+)
- No one under 21 in any content
- Prohibited elements:
  - Mascots or cartoons
  - Celebrity endorsements that appeal to minors
  - Bright/neon colors that resemble children's products
  - Images resembling existing non-cannabis consumer products (candy, snacks)
  - Symbols commonly used to market to minors

### Logo & Branding Restrictions
**Massachusetts prohibits:**
- Medical symbols in logos
- Images of marijuana or marijuana paraphernalia
- "Colloquial references" to cannabis in logos (no slang terms)

### Required Warnings
Every public-facing advertisement must include **"Please Consume Responsibly"** PLUS at least **two** of these warnings:
1. "This product may cause impairment and may be habit forming."
2. "Marijuana can impair concentration, coordination and judgment. Do not operate a vehicle or machinery under the influence of this drug."
3. "There may be health risks associated with consumption of this product."
4. "For use only by adults 21 years of age or older. Keep out of the reach of children."
5. "Marijuana should not be used by women who are pregnant or breastfeeding."

### Extended Warning (Required on Packaging)
> "This product has not been analyzed or approved by the Food and Drug Administration (FDA). There is limited information on the side effects of using this product, and there may be associated health risks. Marijuana use during pregnancy and breast-feeding may pose potential harms. It is against the law to drive or operate machinery when under the influence of this product. KEEP THIS PRODUCT AWAY FROM CHILDREN. There may be health risks associated with consumption of this product. Marijuana can impair concentration, coordination, and judgment. The impairment effects of edible marijuana may be delayed by two hours or more. In case of accidental ingestion, contact poison control hotline 1-800-222-1222 or 9-1-1. This product may be illegal outside of MA."

### Prohibited Advertising Methods
- ❌ Gifts, giveaways, coupons, or "free" cannabis products
- ❌ Radio or loudspeakers (including vehicle-mounted)
- ❌ Unsolicited pop-up ads on the internet
- ❌ Ads on public/private vehicles or transportation waiting areas
- ❌ Ads on clothing, cups, drink holders, accessories, electronic equipment, sporting equipment, or novelty items
- ❌ Mentioning price in marketing materials
- ❌ External illuminated signage beyond 30 minutes before sundown

## Instagram/Meta Cannabis Policies

### What Instagram Explicitly Prohibits
| Category | What's Banned |
|----------|---------------|
| **Sales** | Any attempt to buy, sell, trade, or facilitate transactions |
| **Promotion** | Direct promotion of cannabis products (even where legal) |
| **Explicit Content** | Images showing sale, use, manufacturing, or consumption |
| **Advertising** | All paid ads for cannabis or drug-related products |

### Content That Triggers Flags
- Pricing information
- "DM to buy," "Available now," "Shop now," "Get your order"
- Direct links to product/purchase pages
- Explicit cannabis imagery (plants, flower, concentrates)
- Consumption imagery (smoking, vaping, eating edibles)

### Red-Flag Keywords to Avoid
- "Weed," "Bud," "Pot," "Ganja," "THC," "Canna," "420," "Marijuana," "Blunt," "Zaza"
- These trigger automated moderation in bios, captions, hashtags, story text, and alt-text

### Shadowban Indicators
- Posts not appearing under hashtags
- Sudden engagement drop
- Decreased profile visibility in search
- Content not appearing in Explore or recommendations

### How to Check Account Status
1. Go to Instagram profile
2. Tap three-line menu (upper right)
3. Navigate to **Settings & Activity > Account Status**
4. Check for removed posts or recommendation ineligibility

### Age-Gating Requirements
- Activate age restrictions in Facebook Business Page settings (linked to Instagram)
- Include "21+ Only" or "Adults Only" in bio
- Avoid any aesthetic that could appeal to minors

## Content Categories That Work

### ✅ Tier 1: Safest Content (Low Risk)
| Category | Examples |
|----------|----------|
| **Educational** | Terpene profiles, endocannabinoid system explainers, strain effects (without product) |
| **Team/Culture** | Staff spotlights, "day in the life," team celebrations |
| **Community** | Local partnerships, charity work, neighborhood involvement |
| **Behind-the-Scenes** | Dispensary tours (no product focus), build-outs, design process |
| **Brand Values** | Mission statements, sustainability efforts, company philosophy |

### ✅ Tier 2: Moderate Risk (Requires Careful Execution)
| Category | Examples |
|----------|----------|
| **Lifestyle** | Branded merch in daily scenarios, accessories in lifestyle settings |
| **Events** | Event coverage without product focus, community gatherings |
| **Customer Stories** | Testimonials focusing on experience (not product specifics) |
| **Wellness** | General wellness content, self-care routines (no consumption) |

### ✅ Tier 3: Creative Risk (Expert Execution Required)
| Category | Examples |
|----------|----------|
| **Artistic/Abstract** | Macro textures, color studies, branded abstract art |
| **Cinematic Mood** | Atmosphere shots, lighting studies, brand aesthetic pieces |
| **Industry News** | Policy updates, legal developments (educational framing) |

### ❌ Avoid Completely
- Direct product shots with pricing
- Consumption imagery (any form)
- Purchase CTAs or links
- Harvest/cultivation imagery
- Smoke/vapor imagery
- "Get high" humor or glorification

## Visual Alternatives to Nugs

### Instead of Flower Photography, Use:

#### 1. Texture & Color Abstraction
- Macro photography of brand colors, packaging textures
- Close-up fabric, materials, or surfaces that evoke brand aesthetic
- Color gradients that match strain profiles (without showing product)

#### 2. Lifestyle Integration
- Rolling trays on a picnic table (empty or with accessories only)
- Branded merch in real-life scenarios (gym bag, coffee shop, hiking)
- Premium lifestyle settings (modern homes, outdoor scenes)

#### 3. The "Ritual" Without the Product
- Hands opening a branded box (contents not visible)
- Someone in a relaxation setting (meditation, bath, reading)
- Pre-roll accessories artfully arranged (no actual product)

#### 4. Architecture & Environment
- Dispensary exterior and interior design shots
- Beautiful retail displays (focus on design, not product)
- Neighborhood/community location shots

#### 5. People & Emotion
- Happy customers outside the store
- Staff interactions (genuine moments)
- Community event candids

#### 6. Brand World Building
- Mood boards as content
- Brand color palette explorations
- Typography and design system showcases

## Cinematic Techniques (Non-Plant)

### Visual Storytelling Without Cannabis

#### 1. The "Feeling" Film
Focus on the emotional state, not the substance:
- Slow-motion relaxation scenes (stretching, unwinding)
- Golden hour lighting on faces and spaces
- Close-ups of hands engaged in calming activities
- Ambient sound design (no voiceover needed)

#### 2. Documentary Style
Tell human stories:
- Staff interview setups (no product on screen)
- Community member spotlights
- "A Day in the Life" without consumption
- Founder story content

#### 3. Architectural/Design Focus
Let the space tell the story:
- Smooth gimbal walks through the dispensary
- Detail shots: door handles, signage, fixtures
- Time-lapse of store opening/closing
- Interior design showcase videos

#### 4. Abstract Motion Graphics
Brand expression through design:
- Logo animations
- Color gradient transitions
- Typography motion pieces
- Particle effects in brand colors

#### 5. Reel-Specific Techniques
- Quick cuts synced to trending audio (no product)
- Stop-motion with branded items (merch, accessories)
- Before/after space transformations
- Team lip-sync or trend participation

### Lighting & Mood Palettes
| Mood | Technique | Best For |
|------|-----------|----------|
| **Calm/Wellness** | Soft natural light, muted tones | Relaxation/indica-adjacent content |
| **Energetic** | Bright, high-contrast, dynamic | Daytime/sativa-adjacent content |
| **Premium** | Low-key, dramatic shadows, rich colors | Luxury positioning |
| **Community** | Warm, inviting, natural | Local engagement content |

## Caption Templates

### Template 1: Educational Post
```
[Emoji] Did you know? [Educational fact about wellness, terpenes, or the endocannabinoid system]

[2-3 sentences expanding on the topic - educational focus only]

This is for education only. Must be 21+ to view our content.

#[BrandedHashtag] #WellnessEducation #[LocalHashtag]
```

### Template 2: Team Spotlight
```
Meet [Name], our [Role] 👋

[2-3 sentences about their role, passion, or fun fact]

Come say hi next time you visit us at [Location].

For adults 21+.

#MeetTheTeam #[BrandedHashtag] #[CityName]Community
```

### Template 3: Community Engagement
```
We're proud to be part of the [Neighborhood/City] community 🏙️

[Information about community involvement, local partnership, or neighborhood event]

[Soft CTA - "Stop by and say hi" or "Find us at [location]"]

21+ only.

#SupportLocal #[CityName]Business #CommunityFirst
```

### Template 4: Behind the Scenes
```
Behind the scenes at [Brand Name] ✨

[Description of what's happening - team activity, store setup, design detail]

[Tie back to brand values or customer experience]

For adults 21+.

#BehindTheScenes #[BrandedHashtag] #[LocalHashtag]
```

### Template 5: Lifestyle/Vibe
```
[Aspirational statement about relaxation, wellness, or self-care]

[1-2 sentences connecting to brand values - no product mention]

Visit us in-store to learn more. Link in bio.

21+ only.

#SelfCare #WellnessJourney #[BrandedHashtag]
```

### Template 6: Event/Announcement
```
📍 [Event Name or Announcement]

[Details about community event, store hours, or non-promotional news]

[Location/date if applicable]

For adults 21+.

#[CityName]Events #[BrandedHashtag] #CommunityEvent
```

### Language Swaps
| ❌ Don't Say | ✅ Say Instead |
|-------------|----------------|
| "Buy now" | "Visit us in-store to learn more" |
| "Available now" | "Now part of our collection" |
| "Get your order" | "Explore what we offer" |
| "DM to purchase" | "DM us with questions" |
| "Check out our menu" | "Check out our latest blog post" |
| "On sale" | "Featured this week" |
| "Smoking/vaping" | "Exploring wellness" |
| "Get high" | "Unwind" or "Relax" |

## Quick Reference: Do's and Don'ts

### ✅ DO
- Focus on education, lifestyle, and community
- Use compliant warnings on all public-facing content
- Age-gate your account and include "21+" in bio
- Create branded hashtags
- Show lifestyle and brand aesthetic, not products
- Engage authentically with your audience
- Back up your content regularly
- Build email/SMS lists (own your audience)
- Have a backup account ready

### ❌ DON'T
- Show cannabis flower, plants, or consumption
- Include pricing or purchase language
- Use banned keywords or hashtags
- Target or appeal to anyone under 21
- Use celebrity endorsements or mascots
- Run paid ads for cannabis products
- Use illuminated external signage after hours (MA specific)
- Give away or promote free cannabis products

---

## Emergency Protocol

If your account is flagged or suspended:
1. **Don't panic** - Appeals are possible
2. Review Account Status for specific violations
3. Submit appeal through Instagram's process
4. Activate backup account if needed
5. Continue building owned channels (email, SMS, website)
