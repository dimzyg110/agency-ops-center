# APEX CULTIVATION — "Lo Que Sembramos"
## 30-Second Cinematic Social Video Concept

**Tagline:** *"They planted seeds we never saw bloom. Now we grow our own."*

---

## CLIP 1 — THE FIELDS (0:00–0:06)

**First Frame:**
Wide shot, golden hour. A Mexican father in his 40s, weathered hands, sweat-stained shirt, stands in an endless agricultural field. 1990s Chevy pickup blurred in background. Film grain heavy. Kodak Portra 400 color tones. The man is small against the vast field — emphasizing how much land he works but will never own.

**Last Frame:**
Same composition but the father is walking away from camera, toward the truck. A young boy (8 years old) watches from the truck bed, chin resting on the tailgate. Silhouette against burnt orange sunset.

**Video Motion:**
Static wide establishing shot. Slow push-in (barely perceptible). Dust particles floating in golden light. Father wipes brow, looks at his hands, then walks toward truck. Boy's eyes follow.

**Audio/Dialogue:**
*Voiceover (adult son, slight accent, low and reverent):*
"Apá worked fields that weren't his. Came home smelling like dirt and diesel."

*Sound design:* Distant crows. Wind through crops. Boots on dry earth.

---

## CLIP 2 — KITCHEN TABLE (0:06–0:12)

**First Frame:**
Tight shot: Mother's hands counting crumpled bills on a formica kitchen table. Catholic candle flickering. A child's math homework visible at edge of frame. 1990s kitchen — rotary phone on wall, Virgin de Guadalupe calendar. Warm tungsten light. Everything golden-brown.

**Last Frame:**
Pull back to reveal mother's tired face, soft smile despite everything. She slides a few dollars toward the child's hand — lunch money. The gesture is small but everything.

**Video Motion:**
Slow dolly back. Hands stay in focus while background softens. Mother's face enters frame gradually. Child's small hand reaches into frame from bottom.

**Audio/Dialogue:**
*Voiceover (continued):*
"Amá stretched every dollar like it was made of gold. We didn't know we were poor — we just knew we were loved."

*Sound design:* Clock ticking. Fridge humming. Paper money sliding on table.

---

## CLIP 3 — THE DRIVE / WATCHING (0:12–0:18)

**First Frame:**
Interior of old pickup truck, POV from backseat. Father driving, mother in passenger seat. Through the dirty windshield: a nice house in a better neighborhood. Early morning light. The parents are looking at it — not with envy, with calculation. What would it take.

**Last Frame:**
Same POV but now we see the boy's reflection in the rearview mirror. He's watching his parents watch that house. His eyes are learning something. Determination.

**Video Motion:**
Slow drift of camera, handheld intimacy. Dust motes in morning light through windshield. Father's jaw tightens slightly. Brief eye contact in rearview mirror between father and son.

**Audio/Dialogue:**
*Voiceover:*
"They never said 'we can't afford it.' They said 'not yet, mijo.' But I saw the weight."

*Sound design:* Idling engine. AM radio playing faint ranchera. Leather seat creak.

---

## CLIP 4 — THE WORK (0:18–0:24)

**First Frame:**
Modern day. Close-up on hands — similar to father's hands from Clip 1, but younger. These hands are pruning a cannabis plant with surgical precision. Professional grow facility. Clean. Green. Alive. The hands wear a nice watch now.

**Last Frame:**
Pull back to reveal the adult son (30s) in his own cultivation facility. He owns this. Rows of plants stretch behind him. He looks at his hands the same way his father did — but now there's pride, not exhaustion.

**Video Motion:**
Macro shot of hands on plant transitions to medium shot. Slow cinematic push-out. Light shifts from practical grow lights to a shaft of natural light hitting his face. Color grade warms.

**Audio/Dialogue:**
*Voiceover:*
"They planted seeds they never saw bloom."

*Sound design:* Clean facility hum. Scissors on stem. Breath.

---

## CLIP 5 — THE BLOOM (0:24–0:30)

**First Frame:**
The adult son standing in his facility, but superimposed/double-exposed with the image of his young self from Clip 1. Ghost of the past. Modern facility fills with warm 90s light.

**Last Frame:**
Clean product shot — Apex Cultivation packaging on a beautiful surface. Simple. Elegant. Behind it, out of focus: a family photo (parents and son). Logo fades in: **APEX CULTIVATION** with tagline beneath: *"We Grow Different."*

**Video Motion:**
Double exposure fades. Slow push into product. Everything settles. Final logo animation is subtle — the "A" in APEX subtly references a mountain peak / growth / rising.

**Audio/Dialogue:**
*Voiceover:*
"Now we grow our own."

*Music swells:* Emotional acoustic guitar with subtle 808 heartbeat. Fades on logo.

## TECHNICAL NOTES

**Overall Aesthetic:**
- 35mm film grain overlay
- 2.39:1 aspect ratio (cinematic letterbox)
- Color grade: Warm shadows, lifted blacks, amber highlights (Kodak 5219 emulation)
- Slow motion: 60fps overcranked, played at 24fps for dreamy quality

**Music Direction:**
- Sparse acoustic guitar (nylon string, Mexican folk influence)
- Subtle 808 sub-bass heartbeat pulse
- Modern ambient production with nostalgic instrumentation
- Reference: "Sueños" by Kali Uchis meets early Terrence Malick scores

**Voiceover Direction:**
- Male voice, 30s
- Slight accent but fully bilingual-fluid
- Tone: Reverent, not sad. Proud, not boastful.
- Intimate, like telling a story to someone who understands
