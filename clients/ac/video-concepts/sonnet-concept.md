# AC Social Video Concept: "Lo Hice Yo" (I Did It Myself)

**Total Runtime:** 30 seconds  
**Theme:** Mexican American struggle, 90s nostalgia, generational resilience  
**Brand:** Apex Cultivation (AC)

---

## CLIP 1: The Memory
**Duration:** 0:00–0:06 (6 seconds)

**First Frame:**  
Grainy 90s home video aesthetic. Close-up of weathered brown hands counting crumpled dollar bills on a worn Formica kitchen table. Early morning golden light streaming through a small window with lace curtains. Coffee mug with faded floral pattern visible in soft focus. Film grain heavy, slight VHS scan lines, warm amber color grade.

**Last Frame:**  
Slow pull back reveals young boy (8-10 years old) watching from doorway in the background, backlit, clutching a school backpack. Father's tired face now partially visible, eyes down, counting. Kitchen clock shows 5:47 AM.

**Video Motion:**  
Slow push in on hands counting money, then gentle pull back to reveal the scene. Handheld micro-movements for authenticity. Sun flare occasionally kisses the lens.

**Audio/Dialogue:**  
[Ambient kitchen sounds: coffee percolating, distant morning birds]  
[Voiceover - gravelly, intimate, present-day]: *"Every morning, 5:30. Same table. Never enough."*  
[Subtle Spanish guitar undertone begins]

---

## CLIP 2: The Fields
**Duration:** 0:06–0:12 (6 seconds)

**First Frame:**  
Wide shot. Agricultural fields at harsh midday sun. Father's silhouette bent over rows of crops, straw hat, long-sleeve work shirt soaked with sweat. Heat haze distorting the horizon. Harsh shadows. Color grade: sun-bleached, dusty ochre and faded denim blue tones. Film grain, 16mm texture.

**Last Frame:**  
Ground-level angle looking up at father standing upright, hand on lower back, looking at endless rows ahead. Silhouette against brutal white sky. A moment of exhaustion and quiet defeat.

**Video Motion:**  
Lateral tracking shot moving past father working, then cuts to low angle Dutch tilt looking up as he stands and wipes brow. Camera steady but oppressive framing.

**Audio/Dialogue:**  
[Sound of wind through crops, distant farm equipment]  
[VO continues]: *"He broke his back. My mom, dos trabajos. Still couldn't catch up."*  
[Music builds - deeper bass, hint of trap percussion emerging under guitar]

---

## CLIP 3: The Kitchen Table (Present Day)
**Duration:** 0:12–0:19 (7 seconds)

**First Frame:**  
Same kitchen table, present day. Now it's night. Modern ring light off-camera casting dramatic shadows. Young man's hands (present-day protagonist, 25-30) placing AC cannabis products carefully on the table—sleek packaging, professional branding. Tattoos visible on forearms. Sharp focus, cinematic 2.39:1 crop, warmer modern color grade but still textured.

**Last Frame:**  
Tilt up to protagonist's face, eyes focused, determined. Slight smile—not cocky, but proud. Jaw set. Behind him, blurred photo on wall: his parents' wedding photo. Rack focus shifts to the photo briefly, then back to his eyes.

**Video Motion:**  
Overhead shot slowly descending to table level, then smooth tilt up to face. Deliberate, controlled camera work—contrasts with the handheld nostalgia of earlier clips.

**Audio/Dialogue:**  
[Sound: Product being set down with satisfying weight]  
[VO]: *"So I did it myself. Built something they never could."*  
[Music shift: Beat drops—modern corrido/trap fusion, but tasteful and cinematic. 808s hit]

---

## CLIP 4: The Grind
**Duration:** 0:19–0:25 (6 seconds)

**First Frame:**  
Rapid montage begins. First image: Hands trimming cannabis plants under grow lights—purple and amber hues, trichomes glistening like frost. Clinical but beautiful. Macro lens, shallow depth of field.

**Last Frame:**  
Final montage image: Protagonist in dispensary or cultivation facility, checking quality, holding product up to light. Professional but personal. AC logo visible on his shirt. Expression: focused, exacting standards. Film grain throughout, color grade shifts from cool (grow lights) to warm (final frame).

**Video Motion:**  
Quick cuts (1-2 seconds each):  
1. Hands trimming plants (macro)  
2. Grow room lights switching on in sequence (dolly forward)  
3. Packaging being sealed/labeled (overhead)  
4. Final shot: protagonist inspecting product (medium close-up, slow-mo)

**Audio/Dialogue:**  
[VO]: *"Apex Cultivation. Every plant, every product—"*  
[Music: Full beat, layered with emotional strings, proud but grounded]  
[Sound design: Mechanical clicks, zippers, professional work sounds rhythmic with beat]

---

## CLIP 5: The Inheritance
**Duration:** 0:25–0:30 (5 seconds)

**First Frame:**  
Close-up: Protagonist's hand placing an AC product box into his mother's weathered hands (same hands from Clip 1). Her hands cradle it gently, examining the logo. Kitchen background soft-focused—the SAME kitchen, but renovated, brighter. Photos of family on the wall behind.

**Last Frame:**  
Cut to wide shot: Mother and son at the table together, both smiling, her hand on his shoulder. Warmth. Pride. Golden hour light through window (callback to Clip 1's morning light, but softer, kinder). AC product centered on table between them. Fade to black with AC logo revealed in clean white.

**Video Motion:**  
Intimate close-up on hands, then cuts to medium-wide two-shot. Camera static, grounded. Let the moment breathe. Final 1 second: clean fade to logo on black.

**Audio/Dialogue:**  
[VO]: *"—I made it for us."*  
[Mother's voice, soft, in Spanish]: *"Mijo, qué orgullosa estoy."* (Son, I'm so proud.)  
[Music: Strings swell, beat fades, leaving emotional guitar resolution]  
[Final second: Silence except subtle ambient room tone as logo appears]

## POST-PRODUCTION NOTES

**Color Grade:**  
- Clips 1-2: Warm nostalgic (amber, ochre, faded film stock)
- Clip 3-4: Modern cinematic (cooler grow-light purples transitioning to confident warm tones)
- Clip 5: Golden, hopeful, family warmth

**Film Grain:**  
Heavy in Clips 1-2 (VHS/16mm texture), moderate in 3-5 (35mm cinematic texture)

**Music Suggestion:**  
Start with acoustic Mexican guitar, evolve into modern Regional Mexicano/corrido trap hybrid (think Natanael Cano, Peso Pluma vibe but instrumental and cinematic). End on emotional resolution.

**Text Overlays (Optional):**  
- Clip 3 or 5: "APEX CULTIVATION" in clean sans-serif
- Final frame: "Cultivated with Pride" tagline

**Aspect Ratio:**  
2.39:1 (cinematic widescreen) for Instagram/TikTok with letterboxing, or 9:16 vertical crop for Stories (adjust framing accordingly)

---

**EMOTIONAL CORE:**  
This isn't just a cannabis ad. It's about honoring the generation that sacrificed everything, and building something worthy of that sacrifice. Every frame should feel earned, real, and deeply personal. The product is secondary to the story—but the story makes the product meaningful.

**Make them feel it.**
