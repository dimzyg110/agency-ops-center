# Apex Cultivation (AC) — 30s Cinematic Social Concept

**Working title:** *“Man of the House”*  
**Length:** ~0:30 (6 clips, ~5s each)  
**Tone:** Mexican heritage + warm 90s nostalgia, raw realism, modern ambition.  
**Look:** 35mm film grain, warm tungsten interiors, sun-bleached exteriors, soft halation, subtle gate weave, shallow depth of field, handheld + slow dolly moments.

> **Note:** Childhood visuals stay **separate** from cannabis imagery (no minors around product/consumption). Cannabis appears only in adult-present-day cultivation context.

---

## CLIP 1 — “Kitchen Table Math”
1. **Duration:** 0:00–0:05
2. **First Frame (image prompt):**
   - *1997 interior, small East LA apartment kitchen at night, warm tungsten light, 35mm film still, heavy grain, slight halation. A worn Formica table with a chipped mug, folded paycheck stubs, a calculator, and a Spanish-language bill (“Luz / Electricidad”). A mother’s hands (working-class, cracked nails) slide coins into a small pile. A father’s work gloves rest by a lunch pail. Background: a Virgen de Guadalupe candle flickers. Cinematic, shallow depth of field, warm 90s color grading, authentic lived-in clutter.*
3. **Last Frame (image prompt):**
   - *Same kitchen, camera pushes closer: a young boy (only partially shown—back of head/shoulders, no face) sits quietly at the table, listening. Parents’ faces are out of focus in the background, tired silhouettes. The coin pile is smaller than the bills. 35mm grain, soft bloom, intimate documentary feel.*
4. **Video Motion:**
   - Slow push-in from doorway to the table; subtle handheld tremble like home video. Parents’ hands move—counting, re-counting, then pausing.
5. **Audio/Dialogue:**
   - **SFX:** fridge hum, distant traffic, calculator clicks, candle crackle.
   - **VO (adult, present-day):** “In our house… effort was never the question. It was the math.”
   - **Music:** low, nostalgic piano pad + faint cassette hiss.

## CLIP 2 — “Two Jobs, One Dream”
1. **Duration:** 0:05–0:10
2. **First Frame (image prompt):**
   - *Morning blue hour, 90s street scene. Father in a faded work jacket boards an old bus, lunch pail in hand. Sodium streetlights glow; light fog; 35mm film grain; warm-cool contrast. Background: storefront signs in Spanish, chain-link fence, palm silhouettes. Cinematic wide shot, subtle vignette.*
3. **Last Frame (image prompt):**
   - *Cut to mother in a factory/warehouse break room, hairnet and apron, hands rubbing sore wrists by a time clock. Fluorescent lights with greenish tint but graded warm 90s. Close-up on her name tag (no brand), steam from a microwave cup. Film grain, raw realism.*
4. **Video Motion:**
   - Match-cut rhythm: father steps onto bus → door closes; mother punches a time clock → the stamp hits hard. Quick, purposeful camera moves, like memory flashes.
5. **Audio/Dialogue:**
   - **SFX:** bus air brakes, punch clock *CHUNK*, machinery faintly.
   - **VO:** “They worked like the sun owed them something… and still we were behind.”
   - **Music:** percussion enters—soft kick like a heartbeat.

## CLIP 3 — “Be the Man”
1. **Duration:** 0:10–0:15
2. **First Frame (image prompt):**
   - *Late afternoon, small bedroom. A teenager (face mostly shadowed) stands at a mirror tying a tie for a job interview; thrift-store suit jacket, cracked mirror edges. On the dresser: a family photo in a cheap frame, rosary, a notebook with goals written in Spanglish (“Trabajo. Familia. Futuro.”). Warm sunset light through blinds, 35mm grain, cinematic contrast.*
3. **Last Frame (image prompt):**
   - *Close-up: the teenager’s hands close a notebook; on the page a single line underlined: “Make it happen.” The pen is worn down. Light flares through blinds, dust in air, film halation.*
4. **Video Motion:**
   - Slow handheld orbit around the mirror; tighten to hands and notebook. The tie pull becomes a visual “snap” into resolve.
5. **Audio/Dialogue:**
   - **SFX:** cloth rustle, pen cap click, distant TV Spanish news muffled.
   - **VO:** “I learned early… nobody’s coming. So you become the man yourself.”
   - **Music:** strings swell slightly—still restrained.

## CLIP 4 — “The Dirt Remembers”
1. **Duration:** 0:15–0:20
2. **First Frame (image prompt):**
   - *Present day, adult hands (tattoos optional but subtle) scoop dark soil in a greenhouse aisle. Rich texture, moist earth, sunbeams cutting through dust. Rows of healthy cannabis plants visible but not foregrounded; focus on hands and soil. Warm golden grade, 35mm grain, cinematic macro.*
3. **Last Frame (image prompt):**
   - *Close-up: water drips from a hose nozzle onto soil; droplets sparkle in backlight. In bokeh: greenhouse structure, hanging fans. Film grain, warm highlights, crisp detail.*
4. **Video Motion:**
   - Macro glide along the hands → rack focus to plants → tilt to water as it hits soil. Calm, intentional camera.
5. **Audio/Dialogue:**
   - **SFX:** gentle water spray, greenhouse fans, leaves brushing.
   - **VO:** “Our people know the land. The dirt remembers every sacrifice.”
   - **Music:** subtle guitar (nylon-string) joins—heritage-coded, not cliché.

## CLIP 5 — “Legacy in the Making”
1. **Duration:** 0:20–0:25

Adult workers with gloves, focused faces, respectful teamwork. Cinematic medium shot; 35mm grain; warm 90s-inspired grade applied to modern setting; shallow depth; authentic textures.*
3. **Last Frame (image prompt):**

5. **Audio/Dialogue:**
   - **SFX:** soft tape seal, gloves on plastic, distant laughter.
   - **VO:** “This isn’t a flex. It’s proof. For the ones who never got to exhale.”
   - **Music:** beat lifts—still cinematic, not club.

## CLIP 6 — “We Made It Home” (Emotional button)
1. **Duration:** 0:25–0:30
2. **First Frame (image prompt):**
   - *Golden hour exterior, modest home porch. Adult protagonist stands with parents (now older). The parents’ hands—weathered—hold a small box/bag (gift) and a bouquet of grocery-store flowers. Warm sunlight, lens flare, 35mm grain, intimate, respectful. No cannabis shown in hands—focus on family and relief.*
3. **Last Frame (image prompt):**
   - *Close-up: family embrace. Father’s eyes wet, mother’s smile small but real. In the background, the porch light clicks on as the sun falls—symbolic of stability. Film grain, soft halation, warm 90s grade. End frame holds for logo/title safe area.*
4. **Video Motion:**
   - Slow push-in to the embrace; slight handheld like a home video but cinematic. End on a 12–18 frame “hold” for impact.
5. **Audio/Dialogue:**
   - **SFX:** porch screen door creak, distant neighborhood kids playing, soft wind.
   - **VO:** “I didn’t chase a dream to leave you behind… I built it to bring us up.”
   - **Super/Tag (on-screen text):** “APEX CULTIVATION — For the ones who carried us.”
   - **Music:** resolves with a warm chord + tape-stop style fade.
