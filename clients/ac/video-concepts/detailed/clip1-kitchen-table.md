# CLIP 1 — "Kitchen Table Math" (0:00-0:05)

## Scene Overview
**Setting:** 1997 East LA kitchen, nighttime  
**Duration:** 5 seconds  
**VO:** "In our house… effort was never the question. It was the math."

---

## 1. FIRST FRAME — Static Image Prompt

### Midjourney / DALL-E / Flux Style

```
Cinematic still photograph, 1997 East Los Angeles kitchen at night. Close-up of weathered brown hands belonging to a Latina mother, mid-40s, fingers spreading coins across a worn seafoam green Formica table with chrome edging. Scattered paycheck stubs, utility bills printed in Spanish, a few crumpled dollar bills. A glass Virgen de Guadalupe prayer candle burns in the background, casting warm amber glow.

Shallow depth of field, the soft silhouette of a young boy's head visible in the far background, out of focus, watching silently. Kitchen details: avocado-green refrigerator, floral contact paper on cabinet shelves, wall calendar from a local carnicería.

Lighting: Single overhead incandescent bulb with pull chain, warm 2700K tungsten glow mixed with flickering candlelight creating soft dancing shadows. Harsh practical light from above creates rim lighting on the coins and hands.

Shot on Kodak Vision3 500T film stock, pushed one stop for grain. 35mm Zeiss Master Prime lens, f/2.0 aperture. Slight film halation around the candle flame. Color grade: desaturated midtones, lifted blacks with slight teal tint, warm orange highlights.

Textures: visible wood grain texture on table legs, scratched Formica surface, worn calluses on mother's hands, oxidized copper pennies mixed with silver quarters, paper texture on bills.

Mood: intimate, melancholic, quiet desperation mixed with determination. Documentary realism.

Aspect ratio 16:9, photorealistic, Roger Deakins cinematography style, Emmanuel Lubezki naturalistic lighting, References: "Babel" (2006), "Sin Nombre" (2009), "Real Women Have Curves" (2002).
```

- **Lens:** 35mm prime (standard focal length for intimate drama)
- **Aperture:** f/2.0 (shallow DOF, subject isolation)
- **Film Stock:** Kodak Vision3 500T (tungsten balanced, fine grain)

## 2. LAST FRAME — Static Image Prompt

### Midjourney / DALL-E / Flux Style

```
Cinematic still photograph, extreme close-up of coins being sorted on worn seafoam green Formica table. Latina mother's hands now still, palms flat on table surface, fingers spread with quiet exhaustion. Coins arranged in small deliberate stacks—pennies, nickels, dimes, quarters—geometric pattern of poverty mathematics.

The back of a young Latino boy's head, age 8-10, dark hair slightly disheveled, visible in soft focus foreground right, taking up 1/3 of frame. He is motionless, observing.

Virgen de Guadalupe candle flame reflected in the polished surface of a quarter. Prayer card tucked under the candle's base. A single paycheck stub positioned prominently, numbers visible but not readable.

Lighting: Same tungsten overhead, but candle flame has grown slightly dimmer, creating more shadow. Mother's face remains unseen but her weariness is communicated through her hand position—fingers slightly curled, tension released.

Color grade: cooler than first frame, shadows pushed toward blue-black, desaturated except for the warm glow of candle and coins' metallic warmth.

Composition follows rule of thirds—boy's silhouette left third, hands center, candle right third. Dutch angle subtle, 3 degrees, creating unease.

Mood: resignation meeting resolve, the weight of responsibility, inheritance of burden. Sacred mundane.

Aspect ratio 16:9, photorealistic, intimate documentary style, References: "Selena" (1997), "Mi Familia" (1995), Sebastião Salgado photography.
```

## 3. VIDEO MOTION PROMPT

### Runway / Pika / Sora Style

```
5-second cinematic video clip. 1997 East LA kitchen at night.

CAMERA MOVEMENT: Slow dolly-in from medium shot to close-up, moving approximately 18 inches forward over 5 seconds. Subtle handheld micro-movements for organic feel—not locked off, not shaky. Camera drifts almost imperceptibly to the left during push-in.

SUBJECT MOVEMENT:
- Mother's hands: Deliberate, tired movements. She slides coins across Formica table surface with index finger, sorting them into stacks. Movement is slow, methodical, almost ritualistic. 3-4 coin slides during the clip.
- Coins: Gentle scraping movement across table, some rolling slightly before settling.
- Candle flame: Continuous subtle flicker, casting dancing shadows on wall behind.
- Boy: Completely still silhouette, only his hair moves slightly from ambient air.

LIGHTING CHANGES: Candle flicker creates gentle lighting variation—soft shadows pulse on wall, highlights on coins shift subtly. No dramatic changes, just organic candlelight movement.

PACING: Slow, contemplative. Each coin movement deliberate with pauses between. Feels like held breath.

DEPTH TRANSITION: As camera pushes in, the boy's silhouette in foreground grows larger and softer, eventually filling more of frame edge. Focus remains on hands and coins.

ATMOSPHERIC ELEMENTS: Slight dust particles visible in the overhead light cone. Steam or warmth shimmer barely visible near candle.

TRANSITION STYLE: Ends on a held frame of the final coin being placed, setting up cut to next clip.

Technical: 24fps, film grain overlay, subtle lens breathing on focus, slight barrel distortion at edges. Tungsten white balance, crushed blacks, warm highlights.
```
