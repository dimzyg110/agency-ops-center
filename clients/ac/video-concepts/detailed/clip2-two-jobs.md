## Production Notes

### Single-Shot Design
This clip is designed as ONE continuous take with no cuts. The camera moves from a medium-wide establishing shot to an intimate close-up through physical dolly movement and descent. Both parents are captured in the same space, at the same time, reinforcing that their sacrifice is shared and simultaneous.

### Visual Continuity with Clip 1
- Same kitchen, same Formica table, same overhead bulb
- Virgen de Guadalupe candle present but unlit (nighttime prayer is over, workday begins)
- Coins from Clip 1 are gone — the math was done, now the labor begins
- The table transforms from a surface of worry (Clip 1) to a staging ground (Clip 2)

### Symbolic Elements
- **Medical tape:** Mother armors herself against the damage her job inflicts — labor as warfare
- **Lunch pail:** Father's portable life — everything he needs to survive 10 hours away from home
- **House key:** The boy sleeping upstairs will wake to an empty house, left to fend for himself — bridge to Clip 3
- **No eye contact between parents:** Not coldness, but the efficiency of people who've run out of energy for anything beyond the essential
- **Clock at 4:47:** The math from Clip 1 continues — time is currency they're always spending

### Emotional Throughline
Clip 1 showed the problem (not enough money). Clip 2 shows the response (relentless work). The transition from night to pre-dawn — they didn't even sleep. The kitchen is the same but the energy has shifted from worry to grim preparation. This is the cost of the math.
