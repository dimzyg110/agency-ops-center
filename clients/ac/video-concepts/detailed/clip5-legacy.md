## 1. FIRST FRAME (Static Image)

### Composition
Wide establishing shot of a modern indoor cultivation facility's processing room. The frame opens on a clean, industrial workspace where 3-4 workers in branded AC uniforms sit at long stainless steel trimming stations. They're positioned in a gentle diagonal line receding into frame depth, creating natural leading lines toward the background. Foreground left shows gloved hands mid-trim, holding pristine cannabis flower with surgical precision. The workers are diverse—Latino men and women of varying ages, faces calm and focused, suggesting quiet dignity rather than rushed labor. One worker in the mid-ground shares a subtle, knowing look with another—a moment of unspoken camaraderie.

### Camera Specifications
- **Lens:** 35mm anamorphic (2.39:1 aspect ratio)
- **Aperture:** f/2.8 for moderate depth of field—workers sharp, deep background softly diffused
- **Height:** Eye-level, shooting slightly across the table surface
- **Position:** Camera positioned at the end of the trimming table row, looking down the line

### Lighting
Mixed practical and cinematic lighting. Cool-white LED grow lights visible in the ceiling create the primary ambient wash—clean, almost clinical. Warm secondary fill from pendant work lights above each station casts pools of golden-amber on the trimming surfaces, creating contrast between the sterile facility and the human warmth of the labor. Soft rim lighting from background windows (frosted glass, no direct sun) separates workers from the backdrop. Light catches the fine trichomes on flower in the foreground, creating a subtle crystalline sparkle.

### Color Palette
- **Dominant:** Cool whites and surgical greens of the facility
- **Accent:** Warm amber from work lights, forest green of the cannabis
- **AC Branding:** Muted earth tones on uniforms (olive, terracotta, cream)
- **Contrast:** The cold efficiency of the space vs. the warm skin tones of the workers

### Textures
- Brushed stainless steel tables with subtle fingerprint smudges—evidence of use
- Latex gloves with a slight shine from handling resinous flower
- Fabric texture of AC-branded aprons and shirts (cotton twill, slightly worn)
- Visible trichomes on cannabis flower—frosted, sticky, organic
- Concrete floors with subtle scuff marks and wear patterns

### Mood & Atmosphere
Quiet industry. The sacred made mundane through repetition, yet never losing its reverence. This is not a factory—it's a workshop. The energy feels like a family kitchen before a holiday meal: everyone knows their role, no one needs to speak, the work is worship. A faint humidity hangs in the air. The moment feels suspended between past and future.

## 2. LAST FRAME (Static Image)

### Composition
Intimate close-up, overhead three-quarter angle. The frame has narrowed from the wide facility shot to focus tightly on a single moment: a sealed AC-branded package (matte kraft paper with minimalist branding—embossed logo, clean typography) sits on the stainless steel surface. Immediately beside it, slightly overlapping the package's edge, rests a handwritten note on torn paper: **"Pa' mis jefes"** written in blue ballpoint, the handwriting slightly hurried but deliberate—the penmanship of someone who writes rarely but means every word. 

In the immediate foreground, resting on the table surface at the bottom edge of frame, a hand is partially visible—specifically the left hand, palm-down, fingers relaxed. On the ring finger: a simple gold wedding band, slightly worn, catching a glint of warm light. The hand suggests the worker has just placed the note, now resting in quiet completion.

### Camera Specifications
- **Lens:** 50mm macro capability (or 85mm with close focus)
- **Aperture:** f/1.8 for shallow depth—note and package tack-sharp, wedding ring in soft focus but clearly identifiable
- **Angle:** 45-degree overhead, looking down at the table surface
- **Distance:** Approximately 18 inches from subject

### Lighting
Single dominant key light from upper frame left—warm tungsten (3200K) simulating the pendant work lights established earlier. This creates soft shadows falling to the lower right, giving dimension to the package and note. A subtle cool fill from the ambient facility lighting prevents shadows from going full black. The wedding ring catches a small specular highlight—a tiny star of light on the gold band.

### Color Palette
- **Dominant:** Warm kraft brown of the package, cream of the note paper
- **Accent:** Blue ink of the handwriting, gold of the wedding band
- **Background:** Cool steel grey of the table, slightly out of focus
- **Skin tone:** Warm brown of the Latino worker's hand, weathered but strong

### Textures
- Kraft paper with visible fiber texture, matte finish with subtle embossing
- Torn edge of the note paper—organic, imperfect, human
- Ballpoint ink slightly indented into paper where pressure was applied
- Wedding band with micro-scratches from years of wear—not new, not polished, lived-in
- Skin texture of the hand—knuckles slightly pronounced, calluses suggesting manual work
- Cool smooth surface of brushed stainless steel beneath

### Mood & Atmosphere
Arrival. This is the destination the entire clip has been moving toward—the culmination. The note transforms the commercial product into an offering, a prayer, a promise kept. The wedding ring anchors this in family, in commitment, in the responsibility that drives someone to build something legitimate after generations of struggle. The warmth of the light feels like late afternoon—the work is nearly done. There is no flexing here, only proof. Only exhale.

## 3. VIDEO MOTION PROMPT

### Camera Movement
Slow, deliberate **dolly-in combined with subtle crane-down** movement. The camera begins on the wide facility shot (First Frame) and travels forward through the space, gradually descending as it advances. The movement should feel like being drawn inward by gravity—not pushed, but pulled. Total travel distance: approximately 15 feet forward, 4 feet down.

### Movement Specifications
- **Duration:** 5 seconds
- **Speed:** Steady, meditative—approximately 3 feet per second forward
- **Easing:** Gentle ease-in at start (0.5 sec), linear middle, subtle ease-out landing (0.75 sec)
- **Stabilization:** Fluid head smoothness—no handheld shake, no mechanical stiffness
- **Focal shift:** Rack focus from mid-ground workers (at 2 sec) to the package/note (at 4 sec)

### Motion Breakdown by Second
- **0:00-1:00:** Wide on facility, camera begins slow push forward. Workers visible in their rhythm. Subtle parallax as we pass the first workstation.
- **1:00-2:00:** Camera continues forward, beginning gentle descent. We pass through the space between workers—they don't acknowledge the camera, absorbed in their craft. Focus holds on the mid-ground.
- **2:00-3:00:** Camera now at table height, still advancing. The background workers begin to soften. Our attention is being directed. The package becomes visible in the distance.
- **3:00-4:00:** Dolly slowing as we approach final position. Focus racks smoothly to the package. The note becomes legible. The hand with wedding ring enters frame from the left, having just placed the note.
- **4:00-5:00:** Camera settles into final position—the intimate overhead angle. Micro-drift only (barely perceptible movement to maintain organic feel). We hold on the tableau: package, note, ring. Breathe.

### Visual Transitions

## 4. SOUND DESIGN

### Ambient Foundation (Continuous, Subtle)
- **HVAC hum:** Low, constant 60Hz drone of industrial air circulation—felt more than heard, creates subconscious sense of enclosed space. -20dB, filtered to remove harsh frequencies.
- **Facility room tone:** The specific acoustic signature of a large concrete room with high ceilings—slight natural reverb on all sounds.
- **Fluorescent/LED buzz:** Almost subliminal high-frequency whine from overhead grow lights. -25dB, 8kHz region.

### Foreground Sound Events
**0:00-2:00 (Wide Shot Phase)**
- **Scissor snips:** Precise metallic clicks of trimming scissors, scattered across stereo field. 3-4 distinct pairs at varying distances. Close pair slightly left, mid-ground center, far pair right. Rhythm is organic, not synchronized—each worker in their own tempo.
- **Glove texture:** Subtle latex-on-plant friction. Soft, almost ASMR-quality handling sounds.
- **Stem breaks:** Occasional quiet snap of small stems being removed. Organic, wet, satisfying.
- **Gentle movement:** Fabric rustle of workers shifting, chairs adjusting. Human presence.

**2:00-4:00 (Transition Phase)**
- Scissor sounds begin to recede in volume as we move past the workers
- Introduce subtle **paper handling**—the note being positioned
- A single **wedding ring tap** against the steel table (almost accidental, very subtle)—metallic, brief, at -12dB

**4:00-5:00 (Arrival Phase)**
- **Tape seal:** Satisfying adhesive sound as package is finalized—the "zip" of packing tape, then the press/smooth. This is THE sound of completion. Centered, clear, -6dB.
- **Paper set-down:** The soft "pff" of the note being placed. Almost silent, but present.
- **Breathing:** A single, quiet exhale—not a sigh, not dramatic, just release. Human. Real.

### Music Layer
- **0:00-2:00:** Score continues from previous clip—sparse, contemplative. Piano or guitar holding suspended chord, no resolution yet.
- **2:00-3:00:** New element enters—subtle strings (cello) or synth pad swelling underneath. Building toward emotional release. Low end deepens.
- **3:00-5:00:** **Music lift**—the harmonic resolution arrives as we land on the final frame. The suspended chord resolves to major or mixolydian. This is the "exhale" the VO speaks of. Not triumphant, not sad—earned. Warm. Volume increases 3-4dB.
- New melodic motif enters—simple, memorable, the "legacy" theme. Could be a single trumpet line, a vocal "ooh," or ambient piano phrase.

### VO Integration
- **Timing:** VO begins at 0:00, ends approximately at 4:00, leaving final second for breathing room
- **Treatment:** Warm, intimate processing. Slight room verb (plate reverb, ~1.2 sec decay). Low-mid boost for chest resonance. No compression artifacts—dynamic and human.
- **Ducking:** All sound design ducks 2-3dB beneath VO, swells back up after "exhale"

### Emotional Arc (Sound)
1. **Industry → Intimacy:** Mechanical sounds (HVAC, scissors) give way to human sounds (breathing, paper)
2. **Distance → Presence:** Reverb decreases as we approach—sounds become dryer, closer, more immediate
3. **Tension → Release:** Music resolves, VO completes, tape seals—everything arrives together
4. **Silence as Sound:** The final half-second should feel almost suspended—a held breath before the next clip

## TECHNICAL NOTES

### For Image Generation (Midjourney/DALL-E/Stable Diffusion)
- Emphasize photorealistic quality, not illustration
- Reference cinematography: Roger Deakins, Bradford Young, Emmanuel Lubezki
- Anamorphic lens characteristics: subtle barrel distortion, oval bokeh, horizontal flares
- Avoid over-saturation—this is grounded, documentary-influenced commercial work

### For Video Generation (Runway/Pika/Sora)
- Prioritize smooth motion over speed
- Camera movement should feel gravity-influenced, not mechanical
- Maintain consistent lighting temperature throughout movement
- Human figures should have subtle idle animation (breathing, micro-movements) but not distracting action

### For Sound Design (DAW/Foley)
- Layer real Foley recordings over synthesized ambience
- Tape seal sound should be recorded, not synthesized—audiences know this sound
- VO should be recorded in treated booth, not on location
- Music stems should allow for VO ducking automation
