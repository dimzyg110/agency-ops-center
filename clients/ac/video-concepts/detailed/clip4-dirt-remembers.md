## 4. SOUND DESIGN

### Primary Elements

**AMBIENT BED (Continuous)**
```
Greenhouse atmosphere: Warm, enclosed acoustic space with subtle natural reverb
- Low-frequency hum of ventilation fans (32-50Hz), barely perceptible, felt more than heard
- Distant bird calls (sparrows, finches) muffled by greenhouse panels—placed in stereo field at 60% L and 40% R
- Soft rustle of plant foliage in background (white noise character, 2-8kHz), mixed low
- Occasional creak of greenhouse structure expanding/contracting with temperature
- No mechanical intrusion—no motors, no traffic, no modernity
```

**WATER ELEMENTS**
```
- Individual droplet impacts: Soft, intimate "plip" sounds—not splashy, more absorbed
  - First droplet (0:02): Clean attack, short decay, 800Hz fundamental with harmonic overtones
  - Subsequent droplets: Slightly varied pitch and timing (900Hz, 750Hz) to avoid mechanical repetition
- Water on soil absorption: Subtle sizzle/settling sound as moisture penetrates dry surface
- Distant drip rhythm: Background irrigation or condensation drops every 2-3 seconds (placed far back in mix, 20% volume)
```

**FOLEY - EARTH/HANDS**
```
- Soil texture: Soft granular movement, almost like fine sand shifting
  - Recorded close-mic (12 inches) for intimacy
  - Low rumble component (60-100Hz) as hands move
  - High-frequency detail (4-12kHz) of individual grains
- Soil cascade (0:03-0:05): Gentle rain-like patter as particles fall
  - Layered with varied grain sizes—some heavier thuds, mostly light ticks
  - Recorded onto hard surface and soft surface, blended 60/40 for mixed texture
- Skin friction: Nearly subliminal—very subtle sound of soil against palm skin
- Finger movement: Quiet leather-like creak of skin as fingers open (processed, not literal)
```

**EMOTIONAL/DESIGN ELEMENTS**
```
- Sub-bass presence: Deep 30Hz tone, slowly modulating, creating physical sensation of gravity and earth
- Tonal pad: Single sustained cello note (D2) processed to remove attack, faded in at 60% through clip
  - Warm, not mournful—supportive rather than sad
  - Slight chorus effect to widen in stereo field
- Breath of earth: Processed white noise resembling deep, slow exhale—matches rhythm of soil release
- Resonance: Very subtle metallic shimmer when water droplets catch light (synesthetic sound design—light = shimmer)
```
