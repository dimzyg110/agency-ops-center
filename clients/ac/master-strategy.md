## Executive Summary

Advanced Cultivators (AC) is building a vertically integrated cannabis ecosystem that connects breeders, budtenders, dispensaries, and consumers through three interlocking initiatives:

1. **BGC (Breeders Genetics Club)** — VIP cultivation service that documents elite genetics
2. **Cue** — AI-powered budtender training and incentive platform (SaaS)
3. **Retail Launch (4/20)** — Direct-to-consumer dispensary opening

This master strategy unifies all initiatives under a premium brand positioning inspired by **Jeeter** — the #1 pre-roll brand that built a billion-dollar business through lifestyle marketing, cultural authenticity, and community-driven growth.

**The North Star:** By December 2026, AC will be recognized as the premier craft cannabis brand in Massachusetts, with a thriving BGC community, Cue deployed across 30+ dispensaries, and a flagship retail location driving brand awareness.

### 3.1 Platform Overview

**Cue is a multi-tenant SaaS platform** that AC can deploy as:
- **Option A:** Anchor tenant on shared Cue platform (fastest launch)
- **Option B:** White-label "AC Inner Circle" (full brand control) ← **Recommended**
- **Option C:** Dedicated instance maintained by Cue team

### 3.2 Feature Breakdown (From Codebase Analysis)

#### Mission System

| Mission Type | How It Works | AC Application |
|--------------|--------------|----------------|
| **Commission** | $2/unit sold (or custom) | Base incentive for all AC products |

### 3.3 Cue Circle: The VIP Program Layer

**Tier Structure:**

| Tier | Name | Requirements | Benefits |
|------|------|--------------|----------|
| 1 | Circle Member | Complete onboarding + 1 certification | Standard missions, Circle badge |
| 2 | Circle Select | 3 months active + $500+ earned | Early mission access (24hr), exclusive trainings |
| 3 | Inner Circle | Top 10% performer + 6 months tenure | Highest-value missions ($5-10/unit), monthly bonus pool, direct brand line |

**VIP-Only Features:**
- Flash missions (2x payout, limited time)
- Mystery missions (surprise rewards)
- Brand ambassador opportunities
- Exclusive events (breeder meetups, facility tours)
- First access to limited strain drops

### 3.4 Security & Compliance

**Fraud Detection:**
- SHA-256 image hashing (detects duplicate photos)
- Velocity limits (30/hour, 100/day)
- Volume spike detection (5x average flags review)
- Suspicious hours flagging (3-5 AM submissions)
- Idempotency keys (prevents double payouts)

**Audit Trail:**
- Every action logged with userId, timestamp, IP
- Money events tracked end-to-end
- Admin actions logged (approvals, rejections)

**Compliance:**
- Medical claim filtering (treats, cures, therapy)
- Health benefit blocking
- Youth targeting prevention
- Age verification (21+)

- Content capture (customer interactions, testimonials)
