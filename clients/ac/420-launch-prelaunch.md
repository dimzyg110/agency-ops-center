### Google Business Profile Setup

**Can claim BEFORE opening with "Opening Soon" status:**

1. **Create/Claim GBP** (google.com/business)
2. **Select "Opening Soon"** as business status
3. **Add opening date:** April 20, 2026
4. **Complete all fields:**
   - Business name
   - Category: "Cannabis Store" or "Marijuana Dispensary"
   - Address (full address with suite number)
   - Phone number
   - Website URL
   - Hours: Set as "Opening April 20, 2026"
   - Business description (compliant version)
   - Photos (exterior, logo, team)

5. **Enable messaging** (respond within 24 hours)
6. **Set up Q&A monitoring**

**Important:** GBP can drive local SEO months before opening. Start collecting reviews from B2B partners/cultivation customers immediately (if compliant in your state).

## 4. What We CAN'T Do Until License Approved

### Restricted Content

**❌ DO NOT publish:**
- Specific product pricing
- Direct sales content ("Buy now", "Order here")
- Inventory availability claims
- Medical claims about products
- Delivery/pickup service details (until operational)
- Promotions involving purchase requirements

### Platform-Specific Restrictions

**Instagram/Facebook:**
- ❌ Cannabis sales content
- ❌ Pricing in posts
- ❌ Direct purchase links
- ✅ Educational content
- ✅ Brand story content
- ✅ "Coming soon" announcements

**Google Ads (if considering):**
- ❌ Cannabis product ads (prohibited)
- ✅ Educational content ads (sometimes allowed)
- ✅ Brand awareness (non-sales)

**Email Marketing:**
- ✅ Pre-launch list building
- ✅ Educational content
- ✅ Brand updates
- ❌ Sales emails until licensed

**Website:**
- ✅ Informational content
- ✅ "Coming soon" pages
- ✅ Blog posts
- ❌ E-commerce functionality
- ❌ Shopping cart
- ❌ "Add to cart" buttons

### Safe Content Categories (Pre-License)

✅ **Always Safe:**
- Brand story and mission
- Team introductions
- Cultivation education
- Strain information (no purchase links)
- Location announcements
- "Coming soon" messaging
- Community involvement
- Industry education
- Behind-the-scenes content
- Event announcements (educational)

⚠️ **Proceed with Caution:**
- Product photos (without pricing)
- Inventory teasers
- Service descriptions (without booking)

### State-Specific Considerations

**Check your state's specific regulations for:**
- Pre-license marketing allowances
- Required disclaimers
- Social media restrictions
- Advertising limitations
- Educational content guidelines
