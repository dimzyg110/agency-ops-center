## 2026 STRATEGY

### Track 1: Retail Growth (Dispensary Accounts)

**Goal:** Double retail flower revenue. Increase account count. Reduce churn.

**Tactics:**

1. **AC Certified** - Convert top accounts to recurring relationship (5 pilots by end of Feb)
2. **Superbowl Event** - Relationship building, book follow-up meetings
3. **Reactivation Calls** - Win back dormant accounts before chasing new
4. **Own Store Launch** - Direct-to-consumer channel, brand showcase

**Targets:**

- 5 AC Certified stores by March
- 15 AC Certified stores by EOY
- 20 warm leads converted to ordering accounts
- Reorder velocity improved 30%

---

### Track 2: B2B Expansion (Bulk, Toll, White-Label)

**Goal:** Protect cash flow. Add recurring bulk relationships. Capture toll processing revenue.

**Market Size:** 85 cold B2B prospects identified (Bucket A/B from lead tool). Plus 8 existing customers with upsell potential.

**Tactics:**

1. **B2B Pricing Sheet** - Professional, ready to send (`sales/one-pagers/wholesale-pricing.md`)
2. **Toll Processing Pitch** - Target 45 Bucket A operators with pressure signals (`sales/outreach/b2b_prospects_*.csv`)
3. **Upsell Existing Customers** - 8 customers have cultivation/manufacturing licenses (see ACCOUNTS TO WATCH)
4. **Dedicated Outreach** - Separate B2B from retail sales activity
5. **Volume Commitments** - Lock in recurring bulk buyers

**Targets:**

- 5 new recurring bulk relationships
- 3 toll processing accounts
- 2 upsells from existing retail customers
- No single account >15% of B2B revenue

---

### Track 3: Pre-Roll & Brand Building

**Goal:** Grow pre-roll as margin play and brand builder. Bullet Chamber is differentiated.

**Tactics:**

1. **Toll Rolling Service** - Revenue from other brands' product
2. **Pre-Roll Box Partnerships** - Mexican Haze box model
3. **Content Push** - Pre-roll machine content, Bullet Chamber visuals
4. **Retail Placement** - Bundle pre-rolls into AC Certified pop-ups

**Targets:**

- Pre-roll revenue from 12% to 15% of total
- 2 toll rolling clients
- Pre-roll presence in all AC Certified stores

---

### Track 4: Own Store Launch

**Goal:** Direct-to-consumer channel. Brand showcase. Data capture. Margin improvement.

**Tactics:**

1. **Priority Inventory** - Best batches go to own store
2. **Exclusive Drops** - Build demand through scarcity
3. **Cue Integration** - Test budtender platform in controlled environment
4. **Customer Data** - Email capture, loyalty program

**Targets:**

- Store operational Q2 2026
- 5% of total revenue by EOY
- Email list of 5,000+ customers
