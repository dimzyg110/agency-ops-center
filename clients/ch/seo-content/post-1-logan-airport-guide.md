## Yes, Tourists Can Legally Buy Cannabis in Massachusetts

Let's start with the question most visitors have: *Can I actually buy weed here if I'm not from Massachusetts?*

Absolutely. Massachusetts legalized recreational cannabis for adults 21 and older back in 2016. The state doesn't require residency to purchase. If you have a valid government-issued ID — a driver's license from any state, a passport, a military ID — and you're 21 or older, you can walk into any licensed recreational dispensary in the state and make a purchase.

That includes Cannabis Healing.

No medical card needed. No Massachusetts address needed. No advance registration. Just your ID and a sense of what you're looking for (and if you don't know what you're looking for, our budtenders are here to help).

### What Are the Purchase Limits?

Massachusetts law allows you to buy up to one ounce (28 grams) of cannabis flower per transaction. For concentrates — things like vape cartridges, wax, or shatter — the limit is five grams. Edibles are measured by total THC content. Your budtender can walk you through the specifics based on what you're buying.

These limits apply equally to Massachusetts residents and out-of-state visitors. There's no reduced limit for tourists.

### How Can You Pay?

Here's one thing that catches visitors off guard: **most dispensaries in Massachusetts are cash or debit only.** Federal banking regulations make credit card processing difficult for cannabis businesses. Cannabis Healing accepts cash and debit cards. If you need cash, there's an ATM on-site. 

Pro tip: pull out cash before your Uber ride so you're ready to go when you walk in.

## The Rules: What You Can and Can't Do

This is the part most tourist guides skip or bury in fine print. Read it anyway — it'll save you a headache.

### You CAN:
- Buy cannabis at any licensed dispensary with a valid ID (21+)
- Possess up to one ounce of flower in public
- Consume on private property where the property owner permits it
- Keep your purchase in your hotel room (if the hotel allows it — check first)
- Carry sealed cannabis products in your car's trunk or locked glove compartment

### You CANNOT:
- **Smoke, vape, or consume cannabis in any public place.** Parks, sidewalks, beaches, the Common, the Esplanade — all off-limits. The fine is up to $100.
- **Bring cannabis into Logan Airport.** The airport is federal property. TSA follows federal law, and cannabis is still federally illegal. Do not bring any cannabis products through security. Period.
- **Take cannabis across state lines.** Driving to New Hampshire, Connecticut, or anywhere else with your Massachusetts purchase is a federal offense — even if cannabis is legal in the destination state.
- **Consume in a rental car or any vehicle.** Open container laws apply to cannabis the same way they apply to alcohol. Fine: up to $500.
- **Mail cannabis anywhere.** Not across state lines, not within Massachusetts, not internationally. USPS, FedEx, UPS — all federal carriers.

### The 2026 Update: Social Consumption Lounges

Here's something new: as of January 2026, Massachusetts approved regulations for social consumption lounges. Think cannabis cafes, tasting rooms, and event spaces where adults can legally consume on-site. These are still getting off the ground — individual businesses are applying for licenses — but keep an eye out. By later this year, you may have legal public-ish options beyond your hotel room.

For now, though, private property is your only legal consumption spot.
