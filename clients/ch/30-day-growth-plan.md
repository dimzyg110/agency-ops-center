## Flyer Distribution

**See:** `operations/flyer-distribution-schedule.md`

### Phase 1: Test Week (Week 1-2)

**Goal:** Find which locations/times actually convert before scaling.

Test 3 spots, 2 hours each, track results:

| Test | Location | Time | Why Test |
| ---- | -------- | ---- | -------- |
| A | Logan Terminal B arrivals | 5-7pm weekday | Peak arrival window |
| B | Maverick Station | 11am-1pm weekday | Blue line from airport |
| C | Hotel shuttle pickup area | 6-8pm Fri/Sat | Weekend travelers |

**Tracking:** Unique code per location (LOGAN10, MAVERICK10, HOTEL10). Count redemptions.

**Decision point (end of Week 2):**
- Winner = 5+ redemptions → scale that spot
- Loser = <2 redemptions → cut it
- Middle = retest with different time

### Phase 2: Scale Winners (Week 3-4)

Based on test results:
- Double down on winning locations (more hours, more days)
- Add similar spots (if Terminal B works, try Terminal A)
- Cut losers entirely—don't waste labor

**Budget reality:** Active flyering = labor cost. At $15/hr, 10 hrs/week = $150/week. Only worth it if CAC < $10/customer.

### Passive (Leave-Behinds)

Start with 5 high-traffic spots, not 20:

| Priority | Location | Why |

| 1 | Hilton Logan | Closest hotel to airport |
| 2 | Embassy Suites | Business travelers |
| 3 | Santarpio's | Famous, tourists go here |
| 4 | Rino's Place | Same |
| 5 | Maverick coffee shop | Daily foot traffic |

**Restock:** Check weekly. If flyers aren't moving, swap location.

**Expand list only after proving these 5 work.**

## Phase 2: Ideas to Try Later

_Captured 2026-01-23 — revisit after Month 1 baseline is running_

### 1. Own the LAST 10 MINUTES Before the Airport

Everyone fights arrivals. Almost nobody fights departures.

**"Before You Fly" Capture** — Target people _leaving_ Boston, not arriving.

| Where                        | When          |
| ---------------------------- | ------------- |
| Airport hotel checkout areas | Early morning |
| Hotel shuttle queues         | 6-9am         |
| Rental car return routes     | All day       |

**Message:** "Flying out? Don't waste your stash. Grab something fresh for the next trip."

**Why it works:**

- People hate throwing away weed at TSA
- Emotional trigger: "I should grab something for later"
- Zero competition messaging this way
- New customer + future return when they come back to Boston

---

### 2. Concierge-Level Partnerships (Beyond Flyers)

Flyers are entry-level. We want **endorsement**.

**Pitch to hotels:**

> "Your guests always ask where to go. We're the closest, fastest, and cleanest option. We'll take care of them."

**Offer:**

- Monthly gift card to front desk staff
- VIP Flight Kit for concierges
- Discreet staff-only incentive

**Reality:** Hotels already recommend restaurants, bars, car services. Cannabis is just underutilized — not forbidden.

---

### 3. Uber/Lyft Drivers as MOBILE BILLBOARDS

Punch cards are good. But they're passive.

**Driver Dashboard Placards:**

- Small, clean placard on headrest: "Closest dispensary to Logan — ask me"
- QR code → CH airport page
- Driver earns bonus if scanned

**Why this scales:**

- One driver = dozens of rides/day
- Riders trust drivers more than ads
- Zero CAC beyond printing

This is **distributed sales**, not marketing.

---

### 4. Airport Workers = Hidden Goldmine

Thousands of airport employees. They live nearby. They come often. They talk.

**Airport Worker Program:**

- Show badge → permanent perk (10% always)
- "Airport Workers Pick" shelf section
- Monthly exclusive drop for workers only

**Why this is lethal:**

- Consistent volume
- Word-of-mouth inside Logan
- Builds internal ambassadors
