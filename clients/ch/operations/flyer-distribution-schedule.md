# CH Flyer Distribution Schedule

**Goal:** Consistent presence at high-traffic locations near Logan Airport
**Team:** Brian + 2 flyer distributors

---

## Two Types of Flyering

### 1. ACTIVE (Handouts)
Person physically handing flyers to people at high-traffic spots.

**Best locations:**
- Uber/Lyft pickup areas at Logan (Terminal A, B, C, E)
- Rental car shuttle stops
- Blue Line Airport station
- Maverick Station (Blue Line)
- Hotel shuttle pickup areas

**Best times:**
- Arrivals peak: 10am-2pm, 5pm-9pm
- Weekends: Higher leisure travel

### 2. PASSIVE (Leave-behinds)
Flyers/cards left at partner businesses for customers to take.

**Target businesses:**
- Hotels (front desk, concierge, lobby)
- Restaurants (counter, host stand)
- Bars (near register)
- Coffee shops (community board, counter)
- Yacht clubs (member areas)
- Airbnb hosts (welcome packet)
- Auto shops / car washes
- Laundromats
- Gyms

## Outreach Script (For Business Partnerships)

**When asking to leave flyers:**

> "Hey, I'm with Cannabis Healing - we're the dispensary on Addison Street, closest one to the airport. A lot of your customers are probably travelers looking for a dispensary. Mind if we leave some flyers here? We can send people your way too."

**What to offer in return:**
- "We'll recommend your place to our customers"
- "We can put your cards at our register"
- "Happy to do a cross-promo on social"

## Tracking

**Weekly check-in questions:**
1. How many flyers handed out this week?
2. How many locations restocked?
3. Any new business partnerships made?
4. Any locations that said no or removed flyers?

**Monthly review:**
- Are we seeing more "heard about you from a flyer" customers?
- Which locations seem to drive traffic?
- Adjust schedule based on what's working
