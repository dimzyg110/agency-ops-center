# CH Driver Rewards Program

**Purpose:** Incentivize Uber/Lyft drivers to bring passengers to Cannabis Healing

---

## How It Works

1. Driver brings a customer to CH
2. Customer mentions driver OR driver comes in with them
3. Staff punches the driver's card
4. After 10 punches → driver picks 1 of 3 rewards

---

## Rewards (Driver's Choice)

| Option | Value | Why It Works |
|--------|-------|--------------|
| ☐ $40 Dunkin Donuts Gift Card | $40 | Drivers live on coffee |
| ☐ $40 Gas Gift Card | $40 | Direct cost offset |
| ☐ 40% OFF entire order | ~$40+ | For drivers who want product |

**Cost to CH:** ~$40 per 10 referrals = **$4 per new customer**

## Staff Training

**When a driver brings someone:**
1. Ask: "Did someone drive you here today?"
2. If yes and driver is present: punch their card
3. If driver not present: give customer a card to hand to driver next time

**When driver redeems:**
1. Verify 10 punches
2. Let them choose reward
3. Process reward (gift card from drawer OR apply 40% discount)
4. Give them a fresh card to start again

**Where to keep gift cards:** Drawer at register, restock weekly

---

## Card Distribution

**Where to give cards:**
- Uber/Lyft staging areas near Logan
- To any driver who comes in
- Leave stack at register for staff to hand out

**Who gets cards:**
- Any rideshare driver (Uber, Lyft, taxi)
- Don't need to verify - honor system
