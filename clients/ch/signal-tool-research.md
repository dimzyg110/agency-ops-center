## Executive Summary

After comprehensive research, **there is no specific product called "Signal"** in the cannabis dispensary marketing space. The term may refer to:

1. **Twilio SIGNAL** - An annual developer conference (not a tool)
2. **SignalWire** - A telecom API platform for SMS/voice

4. **Possible confusion with Alpine IQ** - The leading cannabis marketing automation platform CH already uses

This report covers the **major cannabis marketing automation platforms** available to dispensaries, with specific recommendations for Cannabis Healing.

---

## What IS Cannabis Marketing Automation?

Cannabis marketing automation platforms are purpose-built CRM and communication tools designed specifically for the cannabis industry. They solve three core problems:

1. **Compliance** - Navigate state-specific cannabis marketing regulations
2. **Retention** - Keep customers coming back through loyalty programs
3. **Reach** - SMS/email marketing with high open rates (98% for SMS vs. 20% for email)

These platforms integrate with dispensary POS systems (Dutchie, Blaze, Flowhub, etc.) to track customer purchase history and trigger automated campaigns.

## How Cannabis Healing Benefits from Marketing Automation

### Current State (Alpine IQ)

CH already uses Alpine IQ for:
- **Flight Club loyalty program** (points on every purchase)
- **Welcome sequence** (15% off next visit)
- **Win-back campaigns** (20% off after 30 days)
- **Birthday automation** (free pre-roll)
- **Weekly deals** (Monday, Wednesday, Friday campaigns)

### Recommended Optimizations

Based on research, CH could improve ROI by:

1. **Segment by Product Type**
   - Happy Cabbage data shows segmented campaigns outperform blast messages
   - Target edible buyers vs. flower buyers vs. concentrate users separately

2. **MMS Over SMS**
   - MMS (picture messages) see 80% fewer opt-outs
   - Show Flight Kit photos, new strain images, or deal graphics

3. **Review Generation**
   - Alpine IQ has built-in review automation
   - Target travelers for reviews mentioning "Logan Airport" (boosts local SEO)

4. **Win-Back Timing**
   - Current: 30 days
   - Research shows optimal recapture at 45-60 days for cannabis

5. **ROI Tracking**
   - Calculate per-campaign ROI (Springbig reports 1,713% ROI on sponsored messages)
   - Track which messages drive same-day traffic
