## Executive Summary

Cannabis Healing's 2026 content calendar requires an estimated **25-30 hours/week** of manual execution. Through strategic automation, we can reduce this to **8-12 hours/week** — a **60-70% time savings** while maintaining (or improving) content quality and consistency.

### Key Automation Priorities:
1. **Alpine IQ sequences** → Full automation (zero touch after setup)
2. **Social media scheduling** → Batch + schedule (80% time reduction)
3. **Content generation** → AI-assisted templates (50% time reduction)
4. **Reporting/analytics** → Automated dashboards (90% time reduction)
5. **Review requests** → Triggered automations (zero touch)

## What CANNOT Be Automated

### 1. Photo/Video Shoots

**Why It Needs Humans:**
- Product photography requires lighting, angles, styling
- Video content needs authentic human presence
- In-store atmosphere can't be automated
- Seasonal/holiday decorations change
- New product arrivals need fresh photos

**Minimum Required:**
- **Weekly:** 2-3 hours for quick product shots and video clips
- **Monthly:** 4-6 hours for batch content creation day
- **Quarterly:** 4-hour professional photoshoot

**Outsource Option:**
- Hire local photographer quarterly — $500-800/shoot
- Still need staff for daily video content (authenticity)
