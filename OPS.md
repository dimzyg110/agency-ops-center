## Revenue Model

Randy hates traditional sales. The model is: **build valuable things → give value first → let people come to us.**

### How Money Comes In

**1. Client Services (Recurring)**

| Client                    | Services                                      | Revenue |
| ------------------------- | --------------------------------------------- | ------- |
| AC (Advanced Cultivators) | Content, social, video, merch, brand strategy | Active  |
| CH (Cannabis Healing)     | Marketing, design, growth, SEO, automations   | Active  |
| HOP (House of Pride)      | GTM, website, branding, launch                | Active  |

**CH is also a partner channel** — Randy can broker relationships to get other brands and manufacturers into their store.

**2. Products (One-Time)**

| Product                     | Price     | How It Sells                           |
| --------------------------- | --------- | -------------------------------------- |
| 14-Day Margin Rescue Sprint | $3,000    | Newsletter → Snapshot → discovery call |
| MA Operator Intel Pack      | $500      | Newsletter → direct purchase           |
| Toll Processing Connector   | $750 + 2% | Cultivator outreach                    |

**3. Newsletter → Product Funnel**
The Dispensary Playbook has two tracks:

- **Operator Margin Report** (for dispensary owners) → leads to Sprint sales
- **Wholesale Intel Report** (for brands/vendors) → leads to Intel Pack sales

Funnel: Free newsletter → 1-page Margin Snapshot (free, custom) → proves value → they ask for more → Sprint or Intel Pack.

**4. Digital Products (Passive)**

- Intel Subscription: $299/mo recurring vendor data
- Distress List for M&A firms: $2,500
- Templates, playbooks, design assets

**5. Portfolio/Inbound (Zero Sales Effort)**
Every client deliverable is a portfolio piece. Showcase site + contact form = inbound leads with zero cold outreach.

## The Ecosystem

```
Newsletter (free value)
    → Builds trust with operators
    → 1-page Margin Snapshots (free lead magnet)

        → Sprint ($3K) or Intel Pack ($500) sale

Client work (AC, CH, HOP)
    → Builds portfolio & case studies
    → CH as partner channel → gets brands into their dispensary
    → AC products → direct revenue
    → HOP products → direct revenue

Lead Tool data
    → Feeds newsletter content
    → Generates Margin Snapshots
    → Powers outreach sequences
    → Creates Intel Packs for sale
```
