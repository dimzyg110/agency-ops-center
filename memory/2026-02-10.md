## MA Delivery Compliance (935 CMR 500) — Key Rules
- Pre-verification of customer ID required (first-time account link)
- ID check at door (must match pre-verified customer, customer physically present)
- Residence only — no hotels, dorms, public housing, shelters
- One order per consumer per trip per day
- Manifests required for every route
- Delivery hours vary by municipality (state AND local rules)
- Vehicles must be unmarked (no cannabis branding)
- GPS tracking required on all vehicles
- Drivers must complete CCC Delivery Core Curriculum before first delivery
- Background checks + CCC agent registration required
- Body cameras, ID verification photos, digital signatures
