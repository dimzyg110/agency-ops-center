## Production Notes

### For Midjourney Image Generation:
- Use `--ar 16:9` for video first frames and banners
- Use `--ar 4:5` for Instagram feed posts
- Use `--ar 9:16` for Reels/TikTok first frames
- Use `--ar 1:1` for carousel posts
- Use `--ar 21:9` for blog headers
- Always include `--style raw --q 2` for maximum quality and brand authenticity

### For Runway Video Generation:
- Scene duration: 5 seconds per scene (125 frames at 25fps)
- Total video length: 30 seconds max (6 scenes x 5 seconds)
- Include camera movements per scene: dolly, handheld, macro, static, drone
- Specify motion speed per scene: slow, medium, fast
- Include color palette references in each scene prompt
- Add brand-appropriate music style and audio cues per scene
- Specify transition types between scenes: cut, dissolve, fade, match cut
- Add text overlays within specific scenes, not across the whole video

### Brand Safety Notes:
- No visible consumption in any imagery
- Age-gate appropriate (21+ implied)
- Compliant with Massachusetts cannabis advertising regulations
- Focus on lifestyle, education, and community (not intoxication)
